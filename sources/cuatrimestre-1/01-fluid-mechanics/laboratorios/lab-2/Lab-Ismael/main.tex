\textbf{What dominates?}
When $U\gg\alpha$, advection dominates: the pulse just travels at speed $U$ and keeps its shape, because diffusion has hardly any time to smooth it out ($U=10$, $\alpha=0.1$ gives $\max f=0.99$ at $t=5$). When $U\ll\alpha$, diffusion wins: the pulse barely moves and spreads until it is almost flat ($U=0.1$, $\alpha=10$ gives $\max f=0.20$ at $t=5$). In the figures the pulse has moved $Ut=15$ by $t=15$ but has only spread about $\sqrt{2\alpha t}\simeq1.7$, so advection is the one in charge. That is why with Neumann it leaves through $x=10$, and with periodic it comes back in around $x\simeq-7$.
