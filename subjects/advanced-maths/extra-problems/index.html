Advanced Maths — Extra Problems

Exam-style problems for the first midterm, the second midterm and the final exam, written for this portal, grouped by exam and solved step by step. They are practice material, not official exercises: the official ones are on the Problems page.

1st Midterm

The first partial covers Units 1 to 4: first-order models and qualitative analysis, second-order linear equations and linear systems with their phase portraits. Practise the problems one by one or take a timed exam built from them in the Exam simulator.

1st Midterm • Problems

Exam-Style Problems P01 to P07

7 problems solved step by step, with the key results of every part and the common mistakes of the exam.

  • P01: Two brine tanks in cascade: separation for the first tank, integrating factor for the second, the equal-rate case and the peak of salt
  • P02: Logistic population with constant harvesting: equilibria against the harvest rate, the critical rate 1/4, extinction times by partial fractions and by an arctangent
  • P03: A variable-coefficient equation with the solution x: reduction of order to x e^x, Abel's identity against the direct Wronskian, variation of parameters for the forcing x^4 and an IVP
  • P04: Inverse problem: rebuild y'' + a y' + b y = f from one homogeneous solution and one particular solution, then the resonant forcing e^{-x} sin 2x, an IVP and which forcings need the modified guess
  • P05: The system x' = A(a) x with A = (-4, a; 1, 0): type of the origin for every a, the stable node a = -3 with P, P^{-1}, P^{-1}AP, and the stable focus a = -8 in real form with its sense of rotation
  • P06: Inverse problem: rebuild the matrix of a spiral sink from one real solution, solve an IVP and measure the decay per turn
  • P07: Non-homogeneous planar system with a defective matrix: Jordan form, equilibrium shift, IVP and the improper node

2nd Midterm

The second partial covers nonlinear systems and their linearisation, Fourier series and the separation of variables for the heat, wave and Laplace equations. Practise the problems one by one or take a timed exam built from them in the Exam simulator.

2nd Midterm • Problems

Exam-Style Problems P08 to P14

7 problems solved step by step, with the key results of every part and the common mistakes of the exam.

  • P08: Two competing species with a competition parameter b: equilibria, Jacobians, the threshold b = 4/3 between coexistence and competitive exclusion
  • P09: Conservative double-well oscillator x'' = 4x - x^3: linearisation, first integral, true centres, the figure-eight separatrix and the orbit types by energy
  • P10: Full Fourier series on (-pi, pi) of the function equal to 0 on the left half and x^2 on the right half: coefficients, Dirichlet values and two numerical series
  • P11: Half-range sine and cosine series of f(x) = x^2 on (0, pi): continuity of the extensions, decay of the coefficients, values of the series and three numerical sums
  • P12: Heat equation with a constant source and fixed, different end temperatures: steady state, separation of variables for the deviation, series solution and the time constant of the slowest mode
  • P13: Laplace's equation in a rectangle with Neumann conditions on the vertical sides and Dirichlet data given by finite cosine sums: the zero mode, cosh and sinh modes, flux balance and uniqueness
  • P14: Vibrating string with fixed ends started from the rest position with a parabolic initial velocity: modes, coefficients from the velocity, d'Alembert check at the midpoint, energy of each mode and periodicity

Final exam

The final exam has four problems of about 2.5 points, one per block of the course. Practise the problems one by one or take a timed exam built from them in the Exam simulator.

Final exam • Problems

Exam-Style Problems P15 to P22

8 problems solved step by step, with the key results of every part and the common mistakes of the exam.

  • P15: x y'' - (x+2) y' + 2y = 0 with the known solution e^x: a polynomial second solution by reduction of order, the forced equation with x^3 e^x, and initial-value problems at the singular point x = 0
  • P16: x^2 y'' - x y' + 2y = x sin(ln x): reduction to constant coefficients by x = e^z, a resonant particular solution x ln x cos(ln x), and a boundary-value problem on [1, b] whose existence and uniqueness depend on b
  • P17: Predators and prey with logistic prey growth: equilibria, Jacobians, the focus-node threshold of the coexistence point and the long-time outcome
  • P18: Damped mass-spring as a first-order system: the origin in the trace-determinant plane as the damping grows, and the critically damped motion through a generalised eigenvector
  • P19: Sturm-Liouville problem with a Dirichlet end and a Robin end: positivity, the transcendental eigenvalue equation, large eigenvalues, orthogonality and norms
  • P20: Fourier series of the full-wave rectified sine |sin x|: coefficients by product-to-sum formulas, uniform convergence with an error bound, and two numerical series
  • P21: Laplace's equation in a circular wedge with one edge held at zero and the other insulated: angular eigenfunctions, bounded radial powers and a datum that is a hidden finite sum
  • P22: Heat equation in a rod with insulated ends: conservation of the total heat, the zero eigenvalue, a cosine-power initial profile and the time for the midpoint to settle
8 Problems 2.5 points each Units 1 to 6 Verified with SymPy
