Jet Impact on Surfaces & Drag Coefficient Determination

Experimental measurement of the dynamic reaction force exerted by a high-velocity water jet on three distinct surface geometries (flat plate, oblique $45^\circ/30^\circ$ deflector, and hemispherical cup). Derivation of the momentum conservation law via Reynolds Transport Theorem (RTT), calibration curve analysis, drag coefficient $C_d$ evaluation, and systematic diagnostic of physical discrepancies.

Equipment Edibon FME01 / FME02
Nozzle Diameter $d = 8\text{ mm}$
Flow Range $500 - 1500\text{ l/h}$
