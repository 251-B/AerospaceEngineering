Master Formula Cheat Sheet (Eqs. 3.1 to 3.46)

Complete reference handbook from official UC3M lecture notes
Eq. # Mathematical Formulation Physical Meaning & Nomenclature Hypothesis / Scope
3.1 $$\Phi(t) = \int_{V_f(t)} \phi(\vec{x},t) \, dV$$ Definition of extensive property from volumetric density $\phi = \rho, \rho\vec{v}, \rho(e+v^2/2)$. Closed material system $V_f(t)$.
3.2 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f(t)} \phi dV\right] = \lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{1}{\Delta t}\left[\int_{V_f(t+\Delta t)}\phi(t+\Delta t)dV - \int_{V_f(t)}\phi(t)dV\right]$$ Formal limit definition of ordinary material derivative of volume integral. General continuum mechanics.
3.3 $$\lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{1}{\Delta t}\left[\int_{V_f(t)}[\phi(t+\Delta t)-\phi(t)]dV + \int_{V_f(t+\Delta t)-V_f(t)}\phi(t+\Delta t)dV\right]$$ Decomposition into internal unsteadiness storage and boundary swept volume. Domain splitting.
3.4 $$\lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{1}{\Delta t}\int_{V_f(t)}[\phi(t+\Delta t)-\phi(t)]dV = \int_{V_f(t)}\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial t}dV$$ Internal limit contribution: local unsteadiness within instantaneous volume. 1st-order Taylor expansion.
3.5 $$\lim_{\Delta t\to 0}\frac{1}{\Delta t}\int_{V_f(t+\Delta t)-V_f(t)}\phi(t+\Delta t)dV = \int_{\Sigma_f(t)}\phi \vec{v}\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma$$ Swept volume limit: net convective flux escaping through moving boundary $\Sigma_f(t)$. Differential geometry: $dV = (\vec{v}\Delta t)\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma$.
3.6 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f(t)} \phi \, dV\right] = \int_{V_f(t)} \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} \, dV + \int_{\Sigma_f(t)} \phi \, \vec{v}\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma$$ Reynolds Transport Theorem (RTT) for a closed material fluid volume $V_f(t)$. Material boundary moving at fluid velocity $\vec{v}$.
3.7 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_c(t)} \phi \, dV\right] = \int_{V_c(t)} \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} \, dV + \int_{\Sigma_c(t)} \phi \, \vec{v}_c\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma$$ RTT applied to an arbitrary moving geometric control volume $V_c(t)$ with surface speed $\vec{v}_c$. Arbitrary geometric volume.
3.8 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f(t)} \phi \, dV\right] = \frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_c(t)} \phi \, dV\right] + \int_{\Sigma_c(t)} \phi \, (\vec{v} - \vec{v}_c)\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma$$ Universal Reynolds Relation: connects material derivative to moving control volume. Relative convective velocity $(\vec{v}-\vec{v}_c)$.
3.9 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f(t)} \rho \, dV\right] = 0$$ Principle of mass conservation for a closed material fluid volume. Classical non-relativistic mechanics.
3.10 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_c(t)} \rho \, dV\right] + \int_{\Sigma_c(t)} \rho (\vec{v} - \vec{v}_c)\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma = 0$$ Integral Continuity Equation for an arbitrary moving control volume $V_c(t)$. Moving control volume.
3.11 $$\int_{V_0} \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} \, dV + \int_{\Sigma_0} \rho \vec{v}\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma = 0$$ Mass conservation for a fixed spatial control volume ($V_0, \vec{v}_c = 0$). Rigid, stationary control volume.
3.12 $$d\vec{F}_m = \rho \vec{f}_m(\vec{x},t) \, dV$$ Elemental volumetric (body / mass) force acting on fluid particle. Long-range external forces.
3.13 $$\vec{f}_m = \vec{g} - \vec{a}_0 - \frac{d\vec{\Omega}}{dt}\wedge\vec{x} - \vec{\Omega}\wedge(\vec{\Omega}\wedge\vec{x}) - 2\vec{\Omega}\wedge\vec{v}$$ Body force in non-inertial frame: gravity + translation + angular + centrifugal + Coriolis. Accelerating / rotating frame.
3.14 $$\vec{g} - \vec{a}_0 - \vec{\Omega}\wedge(\vec{\Omega}\wedge\vec{x}) = -\nabla U$$ Conservative potential $U = -\vec{g}\cdot\vec{x} + \vec{a}_0\cdot\vec{x} - \frac{1}{2}\|\vec{\Omega}\wedge\vec{x}\|^2$. Steady rotation and linear translation.
3.15 $$d\vec{F}_s = \vec{f}_n(\vec{n}, \vec{x}, t) \, d\sigma$$ Surface contact force (stress vector $\vec{f}_n$) across surface element with normal $\vec{n}$. Intermolecular contact force.
3.16 $$dA \vec{f}_n - dA_1 \vec{f}_1 - dA_2 \vec{f}_2 - dA_3 \vec{f}_3 = 0$$ Force equilibrium on Cauchy's differential tetrahedron as size $h \to 0$. Surface forces $\sim h^2 \gg$ mass forces $\sim h^3$.
3.17 $$\vec{f}_n = \bar{\bar{\tau}}\cdot\vec{n}$$ Cauchy's Stress Principle: linear mapping from normal $\vec{n}$ to stress vector $\vec{f}_n$. Classical continuum media.
3.18 $$\bar{\bar{\tau}} = [\tau_{ij}]$$ Components of Cauchy stress tensor in Cartesian coordinate system. 3x3 tensor matrix.
3.19 $$\tau_{ij} = \tau_{ji}$$ Symmetry of Cauchy stress tensor: proved via angular momentum balance on cube. No body couples / micropolar effects.
3.20 $$\bar{\bar{\tau}}\cdot\vec{n} = \lambda \vec{n}$$ Eigenvalue equation for principal stress directions and magnitudes (zero shear stress). Principal axes of stress.
3.21 $$|\bar{\bar{\tau}} - \lambda \bar{\bar{I}}| = 0$$ Secular characteristic equation: yields 3 real eigenvalues and orthogonal eigenvectors. Guaranteed by symmetry of $\bar{\bar{\tau}}$.
3.22 $$\vec{F}_s = \int_\Sigma \bar{\bar{\tau}}\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma$$ Resultant surface force acting on a finite surface $\Sigma$. Any fluid surface.
3.23 $$\int_\Sigma \bar{\bar{\tau}}\cdot\vec{n} \, d\sigma = \int_V (\nabla\cdot\bar{\bar{\tau}}) \, dV$$ Divergence theorem: $\nabla\cdot\bar{\bar{\tau}}$ represents net surface force per unit volume. Closed boundary enclosing volume $V$.
3.24 $$\rho dV \frac{D\vec{v}}{Dt} = (\nabla\cdot\bar{\bar{\tau}})dV + \rho\vec{f}_m dV$$ Newton's second law applied to an infinitesimal fluid particle. Fluid parcel element.
3.25 $$\rho \frac{D\vec{v}}{Dt} = \nabla\cdot\bar{\bar{\tau}} + \rho\vec{f}_m$$ Cauchy's Differential Equation of Motion for any continuum medium. Universal continuum balance.
3.26 $$\vec{f}_n = -p\vec{n}$$ Purely normal hydrostatic stress in fluid at rest or in rigid body motion. Zero velocity gradients ($\bar{\bar{T}}_d = 0$).
3.27 $$\bar{\bar{\tau}} = -p\bar{\bar{I}}$$ Isotropic spherical hydrostatic stress tensor. Static fluid state.
3.28 $$\bar{\bar{\tau}} = -p\bar{\bar{I}} + \bar{\bar{\tau}}'$$ Stress decomposition into thermodynamic pressure $p$ and viscous deviatoric stress $\bar{\bar{\tau}}'$. Moving fluid.
3.29 $$\tau'_{ij} = \alpha_{ijkl} \gamma_{kl}$$ Newtonian fluid hypothesis: linear constitutive relationship with strain rate tensor. Newtonian fluid assumption.
3.30 $$\bar{\bar{\tau}}' = 2\mu\bar{\bar{T}}_d + \lambda(\nabla\cdot\vec{v})\bar{\bar{I}}$$ Navier-Poisson equation with dynamic shear viscosity $\mu$ and second viscosity $\lambda$. Isotropic Newtonian fluid.
3.31 $$\bar{\bar{\tau}}' = 2\mu\bar{\bar{T}}_d + \left(\mu_B - \frac{2}{3}\mu\right)(\nabla\cdot\vec{v})\bar{\bar{I}}$$ Canonical Navier-Poisson Equation: formulated with bulk viscosity $\mu_B = \lambda + \frac{2}{3}\mu$. Stokes hypothesis: $\mu_B \approx 0 \implies \lambda = -\frac{2}{3}\mu$.
3.32 $$\vec{F} = -\int_\Sigma p\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_\Sigma \bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma$$ Aerodynamic Force on Submerged Body: pressure lift/form drag + viscous skin friction. Wetted body surface ($\vec{n}$ outwards into fluid).
3.33 $$\vec{M}_{\vec{x}_0} = -\int_\Sigma (\vec{x}-\vec{x}_0)\wedge(p\vec{n})d\sigma + \int_\Sigma (\vec{x}-\vec{x}_0)\wedge(\bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n})d\sigma$$ Aerodynamic Moment on Submerged Body about reduction point $\vec{x}_0$. Pitching / yawing moments.
3.34 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f}\rho\vec{v}dV\right] = -\int_{\Sigma_f}p\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{\Sigma_f}\bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_f}\rho\vec{f}_m dV$$ Newton's 2nd law for a closed material fluid volume $V_f(t)$. Closed fluid system.
3.35 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_c}\rho\vec{v}dV\right] + \int_{\Sigma_c}\rho\vec{v}[(\vec{v}-\vec{v}_c)\cdot\vec{n}]d\sigma = -\int_{\Sigma_c}p\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{\Sigma_c}\bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_c}\rho\vec{f}_m dV$$ Integral Linear Momentum Conservation for arbitrary moving control volume $V_c(t)$. Fundamental equation for thrust & forces.
3.36 $$\int_{V_0}\frac{\partial(\rho\vec{v})}{\partial t}dV + \int_{\Sigma_0}\rho\vec{v}(\vec{v}\cdot\vec{n})d\sigma = -\int_{\Sigma_0}p\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{\Sigma_0}\bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_0}\rho\vec{f}_m dV$$ Linear momentum conservation for fixed spatial control volume ($V_0, \vec{v}_c = 0$). Pipes, nozzles, wind tunnels.
3.37 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f}\rho(\vec{x}-\vec{x}_0)\wedge\vec{v}dV\right] = \sum \vec{M}_{\vec{x}_0, \text{ext}}$$ Angular momentum conservation for material volume $V_f(t)$. Closed material mass.
3.38 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_c}\rho[(\vec{x}-\vec{x}_0)\wedge\vec{v}]dV\right] + \int_{\Sigma_c}\rho[(\vec{x}-\vec{x}_0)\wedge\vec{v}][(\vec{v}-\vec{v}_c)\cdot\vec{n}]d\sigma = \sum \vec{M}_{\vec{x}_0, \text{ext}}$$ Integral Angular Momentum Conservation for moving control volume $V_c(t)$. Turbomachinery rotors, fans, elbows.
3.39 $$d\dot{Q}_{\text{cond}} = q_n(\vec{n}, \vec{x}, t)d\sigma$$ Heat conduction rate per unit area across surface element in direction $\vec{n}$. Molecular thermal transfer.
3.40 $$q_n dA = q_1 dA_1 + q_2 dA_2 + q_3 dA_3$$ Thermal equilibrium in Cauchy's tetrahedron as size $h \to 0$. Local heat flux equilibrium.
3.41 $$q_n = \vec{q}\cdot\vec{n}$$ Thermal Cauchy relation: scalar heat flux is projection of heat flux vector $\vec{q}$. Continuous thermal medium.
3.42 $$\dot{Q}_{\text{cond}} = \int_\Sigma \vec{q}\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma$$ Total heat conducted through a finite surface $\Sigma$. Thermal boundaries.
3.43 $$\int_\Sigma \vec{q}\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma = \int_V (\nabla\cdot\vec{q})dV$$ Thermal Gauss theorem: $\nabla\cdot\vec{q}$ is net heat loss rate per unit volume. Closed boundary.
3.44 $$\vec{q} = -k\nabla T$$ Fourier's Law of Heat Conduction: heat flows against the thermal gradient. Isotropic thermal conductivity $k$.
3.45 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_f}\rho(e+\frac{|\vec{v}|^2}{2})dV\right] = -\int_{\Sigma_f}p\vec{v}\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{\Sigma_f}\vec{v}\cdot\bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_f}\rho\vec{f}_m\cdot\vec{v}dV - \int_{\Sigma_f}\vec{q}\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_f}(Q_c+Q_r)dV$$ First Law of Thermodynamics for closed material fluid volume $V_f(t)$. Conservation of total energy.
3.46 $$\frac{d}{dt}\left[\int_{V_c}\rho(e+\frac{|\vec{v}|^2}{2})dV\right] + \int_{\Sigma_c}\rho(e+\frac{|\vec{v}|^2}{2})[(\vec{v}-\vec{v}_c)\cdot\vec{n}]d\sigma = -\int_{\Sigma_c}p\vec{v}\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{\Sigma_c}\vec{v}\cdot\bar{\bar{\tau}}'\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_c}\rho\vec{f}_m\cdot\vec{v}dV - \int_{\Sigma_c}\vec{q}\cdot\vec{n}d\sigma + \int_{V_c}(Q_c+Q_r)dV$$ Integral Total Energy Conservation for arbitrary moving control volume $V_c(t)$. Master energy balance with work & heat.
