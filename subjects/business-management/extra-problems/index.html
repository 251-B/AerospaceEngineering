Business Management — Extra Problems

Short theory cases and exam-style practice problems written for this portal, grouped by exam and solved step by step. They are practice material, not official exercises: the official ones are on the Problems page.

1st Midterm

The first partial exam has two parts: three short theory cases of 2 points each (6 points) and one practical problem of 4 points, in 90 minutes. Practise each part on its own page, or take a timed exam built from these items in the Exam simulator.
