1st Midterm

The first partial exam covers point-particle mechanics (Units 1 to 5) and is one problem of 10 points. Practise the problems on one page, the shorter conceptual questions on the other, or take a timed exam built from these items in the Exam simulator.

Conceptual Questions C01 to C08

8 short questions on Units 1 to 5, each worth 2 points: true or false with a justification, reading a potential, frames and inertia forces, with the key points of every answer.

  • C01: Intrinsic acceleration on a parabolic helix: constant angular rate is not constant speed
  • C02: Motion in a cubic potential: a separatrix that is never crossed and an asymmetric well
  • C03: Energy of a pendulum hanging from a slider: one particle, the system, and a driven support
  • C04: Angular momentum under a non-central force and about a moving point
  • C05: Work of inertia forces: the Coriolis force on a rotating rod and a free particle in an accelerating frame
  • C06: Particle on a moving sphere: degrees of freedom, constraint type and the work of a smooth reaction
  • C07: Damped and forced oscillator: energy decay per period and where the amplitude really peaks
  • C08: Apsidal speeds and a perigee impulse: areal velocity, vis-viva and which apse moves

1st Midterm • Problems

Exam-Style Problems P01 to P16

16 problems of 10 points with 5 to 7 parts, solved step by step with a technical sketch, the key results of every part and the common mistakes of the exam.

  • P01: Bead on a vertical helical wire: Frenet triad, constant angular acceleration and the reaction
  • P02: Particle on a smooth paraboloid of revolution: axial angular momentum, circular orbits and radial oscillations
  • P03: Bead on a parabolic wire spinning about its axis: Coriolis side force, Jacobi integral and the critical rate
  • P04: Radial tube on a turntable with increasing rate: Euler force, three regimes and the motor torque
  • P05: Flight pendulum on an accelerating support: inertia forces, equilibrium and a change of regime
  • P06: Precessing orbit under an attractive inverse-cube perturbation: Binet equation, circular orbits and apsidal advance
  • P07: Insertion orbit and circularisation impulse: conic from the energy, apsides, apogee burn and time of flight
  • P08: Seismic accelerometer on a vibrating base: inertia force, frequency response and the two operating regimes
  • P09: Bead on a vertical hoop held by a spring: reaction, equilibria and a pitchfork bifurcation
  • P10: Free fall from a tower on the rotating Earth: eastward deflection, the order of the north-south effect and an upward shot
  • P11: Pendulum whose string is hauled through a ring: a rheonomic constraint, angular momentum and the work of the hauling force
  • P12: Block on an accelerating turntable: the friction cone, the instant of slip and the direction in which the block starts to slide
  • P13: Capsule descent under quadratic drag with two parachute stages: terminal speed, quadratures and the work of drag
  • P14: A spiral trajectory flown at constant speed: polar and intrinsic kinematics, a non-central force and zero power
  • P15: Relative orbital motion near a target on a circular orbit: Clohessy-Wiltshire equations, drift and a counter-intuitive rendezvous
  • P16: Particle on a free wedge: a moving constraint, the reaction, momentum and energy, and the limit of a massless wedge
