## p48-meridian
View: the meridian plane of P (vertical plane through the axis and P), e_R horizontal to the right and k_0 up; e_theta points INTO the paper (⊗ at O, label "e_theta"; note "e_theta = k_0 x e_R, into the paper").
Draw: the axis k_0 in grey from O, 1.6 units up and 1.6 units down (dash-dotted below O). The meridian parabola z = c R^2 for -1.4 le R le 1.4 (both branches, black), with c = 0.8. P on the right branch at R = 1 (so z = 0.8), filled dot, label "P". The string: thick black segment O-P labelled "rho = R sqrt(1 + c^2 R^2)" above it; and thick black segment from O down the axis to Q at the depth l - rho = 1.0 (filled dot, label "Q"), labelled "l - rho". Right triangle O, F = (1, 0), P shaded light yellow: horizontal leg O-F dashed labelled "R" (adjacent to the angle of the string), vertical leg F-P dashed labelled "c R^2" (opposite), hypotenuse "rho"; angle arc at O between e_R and OP. Slope triangle at P (small, separate shading): legs "1" along e_R and "2 c R" along k_0 from P, hypotenuse along the tangent of the parabola. At P: orange t_R along the tangent of the parabola (up and to the right, slope 2cR = 1.6), 0.8 units, label "t_R = e_R + 2cR k_0"; orange unit normal n_hat perpendicular to it pointing into the bowl (up and to the left), 0.8 units, label "n_hat". Grey e_R and k_0 at O, 0.6 units.
Values to draw: c = 0.8; R = 1; l - rho = 1.0.
Alt: Meridian section of the paraboloid with the string from O to P and down the axis to Q, the right triangle of legs R and c R^2, and the tangent and normal at P.
Caption: Meridian plane of $P$, $\mathbf e_\theta$ into the paper. The triangle of legs $R$ and $cR^2$ gives the length $\rho = R\sqrt{1 + c^2R^2}$ of string on the side of $P$; the tangent $\mathbf t_R = \mathbf e_R + 2cR\,\mathbf k_0$ rises with the slope $2cR$ and the normal points into the bowl.
