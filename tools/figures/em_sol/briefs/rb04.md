## rb04-i-shape
View: the plane of the plate; i to the right, j up, k out of the paper (⊙ at G). Origin at G, the centre of the letter.
Draw: the I as a light translucent blue fill with a dark blue outline: top bar x from -w/2 to w/2, y from h/2 - t to h/2; bottom bar its mirror image below; stem x from -t/2 to t/2 between the bars. Thin grey axes x and y through G, labelled; ⊙ at G labelled z; point G (dot, labelled). Dashed outline of the enclosing rectangle w by h, with the two empty rectangles beside the stem lightly hatched and labelled "(w - t) x (h - 2t) removed". Dimension lines: w (above), h (left), t (thickness of the top bar, right), t (stem width, below the stem inside the gap). Mass element: small dark orange square dA at (x, y) in the top bar with dashed leaders to the axes.
Values to draw: w = 4, h = 5, t = 0.8.
Alt: I-shaped plate centred at G with its two symmetry axes and the enclosing rectangle minus the two empty side rectangles.
Caption: The I in its central frame $S_G$; the planes $Gxz$ and $Gyz$ are planes of symmetry. About $Gx$ it equals the rectangle $w \times h$ minus the two empty rectangles beside the stem.

## rb04-c-shape
View: the plane of the plate; i to the right, j up, k out of the paper (⊙ at O'). Origin O' at the midpoint of the outer (left) vertical edge; the C opens to the right.
Draw: the C as a light translucent blue fill with a dark blue outline: top bar x from 0 to w, y from h/2 - t to h/2; bottom bar its mirror image; back x from 0 to t between the bars. Thin grey axes x (right) and y (up) through O', labelled; ⊙ at O' labelled z. Points O' and G (dots, labelled) on the x axis, G at x_G to the right of O' (in the open space between the bars for the drawn values). Dashed grey axis through G parallel to y, labelled "Gy". Dimension lines: w (above), h (left), t (top bar thickness, right end), t (back thickness, at the bottom inside), x_G between O' and G below the x axis. Mass element: small dark orange square dA at (x, y) in the top bar with dashed leaders.
Values to draw: w = 4, h = 5, t = 0.8 (then x_G = 1.52).
Alt: C-shaped plate with its auxiliary frame at the back, its centre of mass on the horizontal symmetry axis and an area element.
Caption: The C in the frame $S_{O'}$; the plane $O'xz$ is a plane of symmetry, so $G$ is on $O'x$ at the abscissa $x_G$, and the axis $Gy$ is parallel to $O'y$.
