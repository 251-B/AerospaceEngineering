## rb33-rot-psi
View: the ground seen from above, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O), i0 to the right, j0 up; origin O.
Draw: thin grey axes i0 and j0 from O. Blue i1 and j1 turned counterclockwise by psi. Arc of psi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i0 to i1 and from j0 to j1, both labelled psi. Light translucent triangles: i1 with adjacent leg along i0 (cos psi) and dashed opposite leg along j0 (sin psi); j1 with adjacent leg along j0 (cos psi) and dashed opposite leg towards negative i0 (sin psi). Along j1 the trace of the line OPQ with the contact points P (at 2a) and Q (at 4a) as dots, and the top view of the axle as a thick dark segment above that line. A curved arrow about O labelled psi-dot.
Values to draw: psi = 30 deg, a = 1.
Alt: Top view: the rotating basis i1, j1 turned by psi, with the contact line OPQ along j1.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\psi$. The line of the contact points $OPQ$ is $\mathbf j_1$; $P$ is at $2a$ and $Q$ at $4a$ from $O$.

## rb33-cone
View: the vertical plane of the axle, j1 to the right, k1 up, i1 out of the paper (⊙ at O); origin O on the ground.
Draw: the ground as a hatched horizontal line through O. The axle line from O at 30 deg above the ground, with C1 (dot) at sqrt(3) a and C2 (dot) at 2 sqrt(3) a from O; the massless axle as a thick black segment from C1 to C2. The two wheels edge-on as thick grey segments through C1 and C2, normal to the axle: the small wheel of half-length a, the large one of half-length 2a; their lower ends are P (at 2a from O) and Q (at 4a from O) on the ground. G (dot) on the axle at 4 sqrt(3) a / 5 from C1. Light translucent orange right triangle O, C1, P (right angle at C1, small square): adjacent leg OC1 labelled sqrt(3) a, opposite leg C1P labelled a, hypotenuse OP labelled 2a; larger translucent triangle O, C2, Q with legs 2 sqrt(3) a and 2a and hypotenuse 4a. Angle gamma = 30 deg at O between the ground and the axle (arc, counterclockwise from j1, label "30°"). Blue unit vectors at O: j2 along the axle and k2 normal to it (up and to the left); arc 30 deg from j1 to j2 and from k1 to k2. Grey j1 and k1. Dashed vertical from G to the ground with the height labelled 9 sqrt(3) a / 10 and the horizontal distance from O labelled 27 a / 10.
Values to draw: a = 1, gamma = 30 deg.
Alt: Section through the axle: the wheels as two sections of a cone with apex O and half-angle 30 degrees.
Caption: Plane of the axle. The right triangles $OC_1P$ and $OC_2Q$ are similar: $OC_1 = \sqrt3\,a$, the half-angle is $30^\circ$, and $\mathbf j_2$ is $\mathbf j_1$ turned by $30^\circ$ about $\mathbf i_1$ (⊙).

## rb33-rot-phi
View: along the axle from O, j2 pointing into the paper (⊗ at C2), i2 to the right, k2 up; origin C2.
Draw: the large wheel face-on as a grey disc of radius 2a centred at C2. Thin grey axes i2 and k2 and the dashed direction minus k2 (down), which is the direction C2Q, labelled "C2Q". Blue body vectors i3 (from minus k2 turned clockwise by phi, so pointing down and to the left) and k3 (from i2 turned clockwise by phi, pointing right and down). Arc of phi (clockwise, arrowhead) from minus k2 to i3 and from i2 to k3, both labelled phi. Light translucent triangles: i3 with adjacent leg along minus k2 (cos phi) and opposite leg along minus i2 (sin phi); k3 with adjacent leg along i2 (cos phi) and opposite leg along minus k2 (sin phi). Note beside the ⊗: "positive rotation about an axis into the paper looks clockwise".
Values to draw: phi = 35 deg, a = 1.
Alt: View along the axle: the body basis i3, k3 turned by phi about j2 from its initial position along C2Q.
Caption: Rotation about the axle $\mathbf j_2$ (⊗) by $\phi$. At $\phi = 0$, $\mathbf i_3$ points along $C_2Q$, which is $-\mathbf k_2$.

## rb33-fbd
View: the vertical plane of the axle as in rb33-cone (j1 right, k1 up, i1 out of the paper ⊙).
Draw: the body (axle and both wheels edge-on) and G. At G: weight mg downwards (red). At P: N_P upwards (green), Y_P along j1 (green, to the left), and X_P along i1 drawn as ⊙ in a green circle (label X_P). At Q: N_Q upwards (green), Y_Q along j1 (green, to the left), X_Q as ⊙ in a green circle. Dashed arms from G to P and from G to Q with their components labelled: GP horizontal -7a/10 and vertical -9 sqrt(3) a/10; GQ horizontal 13a/10 and vertical -9 sqrt(3) a/10. A thick dotted line along OPQ labelled "common line of action of Y_P and Y_Q". Curved arrow about the vertical through O labelled psi-dot.
Values to draw: as in rb33-cone.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the wheels and axle with the weight and the two contact forces.
Caption: Free-body diagram. $Y_P$ and $Y_Q$ act along the same line $OPQ$, so only their sum enters the equations of the rigid body.
