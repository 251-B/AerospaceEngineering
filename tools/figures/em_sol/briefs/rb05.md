## rb05-a-bar
View: oblique (cabinet) 3D view; x to the right along the bar, y towards the lower left (out of the paper, oblique), z up; origin G at the middle of the bar.
Draw: thin grey axes x, y, z through G, labelled. The bar as a thick dark blue segment along x from -L/2 to L/2 (a thin cylinder outline is enough). Point G (dot, labelled). Dimension lines L/2 and L/2 above the bar. Mass element: a short dark orange piece of the bar of length dx at abscissa x, labelled "dm = λ dx", with a dashed leader down to the x axis labelled x.
Values to draw: L = 4.
Alt: Slender bar along the x axis centred at G with a line element dx at abscissa x.
Caption: The slender bar in its central frame; the mass element is $\lambda\,dx$ and every point has zero $y$ and $z$.

## rb05-b-cylinder
View: oblique 3D view; z along the axis of the cylinder towards the lower left, x to the right, y up; origin G at the centre.
Draw: the cylinder of radius R and length L (light translucent blue fill, dark blue outline, end circles drawn as ellipses). Thin grey axes x, y, z through G, labelled. Point G (dot). Dimensions L/2 and L/2 along the axis, R on the near end face. Mass element: inside a cross-section at height z, a small dark orange curvilinear block at radius r and angle θ (measured from x towards y), with sides dr, r dθ and dz labelled; a dashed radius from the axis to it labelled r, and the arc of θ from the x direction labelled θ.
Values to draw: R = 1, L = 3, r = 0.65, theta = 40 deg.
Alt: Cylinder with its axis along z, centred at G, and the cylindrical volume element r dr dθ dz.
Caption: Cylindrical coordinates about the axis $z$: the volume element has sides $dr$, $r\,d\theta$ and $dz$, so its jacobian is $r$.

## rb05-d-sphere
View: oblique 3D view; x towards the lower left, y to the right, z up; origin G at the centre.
Draw: the sphere of radius R (light translucent blue, dark blue outline, the equator as a dashed ellipse). Thin grey axes x, y, z through G, labelled. A point P on a ray from G: the ray of length r (dark), its projection onto the plane Gxy dashed, the longitude θ as an arc in the plane Gxy from x to that projection, and the latitude φ as an arc in the vertical plane from the projection up to the ray. Right triangle G - projection foot - P with light translucent orange fill: hypotenuse r, horizontal leg r cos φ, vertical leg r sin φ, labelled. Small dark orange volume element at P with sides dr, r cos φ dθ, r dφ labelled.
Values to draw: R = 1.5, r = 1.1, theta = 35 deg, phi = 30 deg.
Alt: Sphere with the spherical coordinates of the notes: radius r, longitude θ and latitude φ, and the right triangle that gives r cos φ and r sin φ.
Caption: Spherical coordinates of the notes, with the latitude $\varphi$ measured from the plane $Gxy$: the triangle with hypotenuse $r$ gives the legs $r\cos\varphi$ and $r\sin\varphi$, and the volume element is $r^2\cos\varphi\,dr\,d\theta\,d\varphi$.

A ray from O at latitude φ below the face to a point P at distance r: right triangle O - foot on the x axis - P with light translucent orange fill, hypotenuse r, leg along x labelled r cos φ, vertical leg labelled r sin φ (this is z), arc φ at O from the x axis to the ray. Caption: Meridian section ($\mathbf j$ into the paper). The body is the latitude range from 0 to $\pi/2$ on the side of positive $z$; $G$ lies on $Oz$ at $3R/8$, and the axes $Gx$ and $Ox$ are parallel at that distance.

## rb05-f-cone
View: meridian section in the plane Vxy, seen with z out of the paper (⊙ at V); apex V on the right, axis x pointing to the left from V towards the base (as in the statement figure, x beyond the base), y up.
Draw: the triangle of the section (light translucent blue fill, dark blue outline): apex V, base of half-height R at distance L from V. Thin grey axis line through V along the axis, arrow towards the base labelled x; ⊙ at V labelled z. Points V and G (G on the axis at 3L/4 from V; dots, labelled), dimension 3L/4 between them and L for the whole height. A vertical strip at distance s from V: dashed line from the axis up to the slant side, its length labelled R_s; the small similar right triangle V - foot - top of the strip with light translucent orange fill (legs s and R_s, hypotenuse along the slant side), and the big triangle legs L and R labelled. Dashed grey axis through G parallel to y, labelled "Gy", and a parallel one through V labelled "Vy".
Values to draw: R = 1.2, L = 4, s = 2.5.
Alt: Meridian section of the cone with the apex at V, the centre of mass at 3L/4 from it and the similar triangles that give the radius of a section.
Caption: Meridian section of the cone ($\mathbf k$ out of the paper). The similar right triangles with legs $s$, $R_s$ and $L$, $R$ give the radius of the section at the distance $s$; Steiner moves the transverse axis from $V$ to $G$ over $3L/4$.
