Alt: Free-body diagram of the falling ball with its weight pointing down and the drag pointing up.
Caption: Forces on the ball while it falls. The weight acts along $\mathbf i$ and the drag, opposite to the downward velocity, along $-\mathbf i$ with magnitude $\tfrac12\rho S C_D v^2$; they balance at the terminal speed.

## p16-time-laws
View: plain 2D graph of dimensionless quantities; horizontal axis the dimensionless time u = g t / v_t from 0 to 4; vertical axis from 0 to 3.5.
Draw: grey axes with ticks at u = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 3 on the vertical axis. Curve 1 (green, solid): v / v_t = tanh(u). Dashed grey horizontal asymptote at 1. Thin green dashed line v / v_t = u from u = 0 to u = 1.2 (free fall, tangent at the origin). Curve 2 (black, solid): s g / v_t^2 = ln cosh(u) (values 0, 0.434, 1.325, 2.309, 3.307 at u = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4). Thin purple dashed line u - ln 2 from u = 1 to u = 4, drawn over the curves (late-time asymptote, a colour distinct from the black curve). Thin black dotted parabola u^2 / 2 from u = 0 to u = 1.6 (free fall).
Labels (placed in empty space next to each curve, never on a line): "u = g t / v_t" under the horizontal axis; "v / v_t = tanh u"; "s g / v_t^2 = ln cosh u"; "free fall: u"; "free fall: u^2 / 2"; "u - ln 2" (purple, just below the dashed line near u = 2, above the horizontal line at 1); "1" at the left end of the asymptote.
Values to draw: as listed.
Alt: Graph of the dimensionless velocity and distance fallen against the dimensionless time, with the free-fall and terminal asymptotes.
Caption: Time laws of the fall. The velocity tends to the terminal speed and the distance fallen tends to a straight line of slope 1 shifted by $\ln 2$; at early times both follow free fall.
