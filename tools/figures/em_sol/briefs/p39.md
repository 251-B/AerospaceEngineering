Values to draw: theta = 35 degrees; phi = 25 degrees; d = 1; l = 3 (so u = 3 cos 35 deg - 1 = 1.457 and rho = 1.779).
Alt: Plate with the string from O to Q on the edge and hanging to P, the equal angles at the edge, the right triangle of u cos phi and u sin phi below the edge, and an inset with the triangle of d and d tan theta.
Caption: The string runs from $O$ to $Q$ on the plate (legs $d$ and $d\tan\theta$, inset) and hangs from $Q$ to $P$. The smooth edge makes the two segments meet it at equal angles, so $\mathbf e_{QP}\cdot\mathbf j_0 = \sin\theta$, and $P$ lies at the distance $u = \ell\cos\theta - d$ from the edge, swung out by $\phi$.
