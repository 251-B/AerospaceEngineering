## rb16-rot-alpha
View: vertical plane containing the line of maximum slope; j points into the paper (⊗, drawn at O), I to the right, K up; origin O on the inclined plane.
Draw: the inclined plane seen edge-on as a thick line through O going down to the right at the angle alpha below the horizontal (hatching below it). Thin grey axes I (horizontal, right) and K (vertical, up) from O. Blue unit vectors i (along the plane, downhill, down to the right) and k (normal to the plane, leaning to the right of K). Arc of alpha (with arrowhead, clockwise as seen) from I to i and from K to k, both labelled alpha. Light translucent triangles: for i, adjacent leg along I labelled cos alpha and dashed opposite leg pointing down (along -K) labelled sin alpha; for k, adjacent leg along K labelled cos alpha and dashed opposite leg along I labelled sin alpha. A red vector -g K (weight direction) drawn from a point above the plane, with its dashed components g sin alpha along i and g cos alpha along -k. Small ⊗ symbol at O labelled j.
Values to draw: alpha = 25 deg.
Alt: The basis of the inclined plane obtained by turning the horizontal basis by alpha about j, with the components of the weight.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf j$ (⊗) by $\alpha$: $\mathbf i$ points downhill and $\mathbf k$ is the normal to the plane. The weight splits into $mg\sin\alpha$ along $\mathbf i$ and $mg\cos\alpha$ against $\mathbf k$.

## rb16-plane
View: the inclined plane seen from above along -k (k out of the paper, ⊙ at O); i (downhill) to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i and j from O. Point E on the i axis at distance x_E from O (dot, label E, dimension x_E = g sin alpha / kappa). Projection of G on the plane: point P (contact point, dot, labels "P (below G)") at coordinates (x, y), with dashed projections to the axes labelled x and y. Green arrow from G towards O labelled "attraction -m kappa r (in-plane part)", red arrow along +i labelled "m g sin alpha". Blue velocity vector v_G at P (tangent to the ellipse). A dashed ellipse centred at E passing through P (the trajectory of G), with its semi-axes not aligned with i (tilted about 20 deg). Small circular arrow about the normal at P labelled omega_z.
Values to draw: x_E = 4, ellipse semi-axes 3 and 1.5 tilted 20 deg, P on the ellipse at (6.5, 1.2).
Alt: Top view of the inclined plane with O, the equilibrium point E and the elliptical path of the centre of the sphere.
Caption: Top view of the plane. $P$ is the contact point, straight below $G$; the in-plane forces vanish at $E$, and $G$ moves on an ellipse centred above $E$.

## rb16-sphere-element
View: oblique 3D view of the sphere centred at G with axes x, y, z (grey).
Draw: the sphere of radius R (light grey shading, outline). A volume element at distance r from G: small curved box with edges dr (radial), r d chi (along the meridian) and r sin chi d lambda (along the parallel), shaded orange. Dashed radius from G to the element labelled r; arc of the colatitude chi from z to the radius; arc of the longitude lambda in the xy plane from x to the projection of the radius (dashed projection line labelled r sin chi).
Values to draw: R = 3, r = 2.2, chi = 50 deg, lambda = 35 deg.
Alt: Spherical volume element of the solid sphere with its coordinates r, chi and lambda.
Caption: Volume element $dV = r^2\sin\chi\,dr\,d\chi\,d\lambda$ used for the mass and the moments of inertia of the sphere.

## rb16-fbd
View: section of the sphere by the vertical plane containing the line of maximum slope through G; j into the paper (⊗); i downhill (down to the right along the plane), k normal to the plane.
Draw: the inclined plane as a line at alpha below the horizontal (faint, hatched). Sphere outline of radius R touching the plane at P; G at the centre. Dashed segment GP labelled R (along -k). At G: red weight vector mg straight down (vertical), with dashed components mg sin alpha along i and mg cos alpha along -k; green-dark attraction vector -m kappa r_G drawn from G towards O (O placed on the plane up and to the left of P), label "m kappa |OG|". At P: green normal reaction N along k; green friction F_x along -i (pointing uphill), label F_x. Small text "F_y along j (normal to the paper)". Blue basis i, k drawn near O. Arc of alpha at the foot of the plane.
Values to draw: alpha = 25 deg, R = 1.5, O at 5 units uphill from P.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the sphere: weight and attraction at G, normal reaction and friction at the contact point.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The weight and the attraction act at $G$ and have no moment about it; only the friction at $P$, with the arm $-R\,\mathbf k$, changes the rotation.
