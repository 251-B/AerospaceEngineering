## rb37-rot-psi
View: the plane of the figure, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O), i0 to the right, j0 up; origin O at the centre of the cylinder.
Draw: the fixed cylinder as the lower half of a circle of radius 3R centred at O (hatched, "fixed"). Thin grey axes i0 and j0 at O and a dashed downward vertical from O. The line of centres from O through C (on the cylinder, at 3R) to G (at 4R), with the disk of radius R centred at G touching the cylinder at C from outside. Arc of psi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) at O from the downward vertical to OG, labelled psi. Light translucent orange triangle for the unit vector along OG: hypotenuse 1 along OG, adjacent leg along minus j0 labelled cos psi, dashed opposite leg along i0 labelled sin psi. Blue unit vectors at G: j1 pointing from G towards C (towards O) and i1 normal to it, tangent to the circle of G (to the right and up); arcs psi from i0 to i1 and from j0 to j1. Dimensions: OC labelled 3R, CG labelled R, OG labelled 4R.
Values to draw: psi = 40 deg, R = 1.
Alt: The basis of the line of centres, turned by psi, with G at 4R from O.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\psi$, measured from the downward vertical. $\mathbf j_1$ points from $G$ towards $O$, so $\mathbf r_0^G$ is $-4R\,\mathbf j_1$.

## rb37-rot-phi
View: at G, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at G), i1 to the right, j1 up (the picture of rb37-rot-psi turned so that GC is vertical).
Draw: the disk of radius R centred at G, with C at the top (on GC, along j1). Thin grey axes i1 and j1 at G. Blue body vectors j_b (j1 turned clockwise by phi, up and to the right) and i_b (i1 turned clockwise by phi, down and to the right); P (dot) on the rim at the tip of R i_b. Arc of phi (clockwise, arrowhead) from j1 to j_b and from i1 to i_b, both labelled phi; note near G: "phi turns clockwise: rotation about minus k0 (⊗)". Light translucent triangles: j_b with adjacent leg along j1 (cos phi) and opposite leg along i1 (sin phi); i_b with adjacent leg along i1 (cos phi) and opposite leg along minus j1 (sin phi).
Values to draw: phi = 60 deg, R = 1.
Alt: The body basis of the disk turned clockwise by phi from the line GC.
Caption: The body basis is turned by $\phi$ about $-\mathbf k_0$ (⊗) from $\mathcal B_1$, so the disk has turned by $\psi - \phi$ in $S_0$; rolling gives $\phi = -3\psi$.

## rb37-fbd
View: the plane of the figure, k0 out of the paper, as in rb37-rot-psi.
Draw: the cylinder faint grey; the disk at psi = 40 deg with its centre G, contact C and the body point P at R i_b, with the body angle 4 psi = 160 deg (i_b at 160 deg from i0, so P is to the upper left of G). The fixed point A at (0, -6R) (dot, label A). Dashed line from A to P. At G: weight mg downwards (red). At P: the repulsion F_P (red) along AP, pointing away from A, label "F_P = k AP". At C: normal reaction N along minus j1 (green, pointing away from O, i.e. from C towards G), label N; friction T along i1 (green), label T. Dashed arms: G to C labelled R j1, G to P labelled R i_b. Curved arrow 4 psi-ddot counterclockwise about G.
Values to draw: psi = 40 deg, R = 1, A at (0, -6).
Alt: Free-body diagram of the disk: weight, repulsion from A at P, and the normal reaction and friction of the cylinder at C.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The normal reaction pushes the disk away from $O$ and can only be positive; the repulsion $k\,\overrightarrow{AP}$ presses the disk against the cylinder.
