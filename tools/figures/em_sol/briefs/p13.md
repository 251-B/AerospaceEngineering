## p13-potential
View: energy diagram, horizontal axis y = x/a, vertical axis V/(m g a).

Caption: The energy equals the barrier top $V(-a) = 2mga$: the particle turns at $x = 2a$ and then creeps towards $x = -a$, which it reaches only as $t \to \infty$.

Alt: The position x/a as a function of time: up to 2 at t1, then down towards the asymptote -1.
Caption: Explicit time law $x(t) = a\left[2 - 3\tanh^2\left(\sqrt{3g/(2a)}\,t - \operatorname{arctanh}(1/\sqrt3)\right)\right]$: one formula for both legs, with the asymptote $x = -a$.
