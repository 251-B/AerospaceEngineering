## rb22-rot-gamma
View: vertical plane containing the line of maximum slope through O; j points into the paper (⊗ drawn at O), I to the right, K up.
Draw: the inclined plane edge-on as a thick line through O going down to the right at gamma below the horizontal (hatched below). Thin grey axes I and K from O. Blue i (down the slope) and k (normal to the plane, leaning right of K). Arcs of gamma (clockwise as seen) from I to i and from K to k, labelled gamma. Light translucent triangles: for i, adjacent leg cos gamma along I and dashed opposite leg sin gamma pointing down; for k, adjacent leg cos gamma along K and dashed opposite leg sin gamma along I. A red weight vector Mg (vertical, down) drawn at a point above the plane with dashed components Mg sin gamma along i and Mg cos gamma along -k.
Values to draw: gamma = 25 deg.
Alt: The basis of the inclined plane, turned by gamma about the horizontal axis j, with the components of the weight.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf j$ (⊗) by $\gamma$; the weight is $Mg\sin\gamma\,\mathbf i - Mg\cos\gamma\,\mathbf k$.

## rb22-rot-theta
View: the inclined plane seen from above along -k (k out of the paper, ⊙ at O); i (downhill) to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i and j from O. Blue i_2 and j_2 turned counterclockwise by theta. Contact point P on i_2 at distance d from O (dot, label P, dimension d). Arcs of theta from i to i_2 and from j to j_2. Light translucent triangles: for i_2, adjacent leg cos theta along i and dashed opposite leg sin theta along j; for j_2, adjacent leg cos theta along j and dashed opposite leg sin theta towards -i. Dashed circle of radius d centred at O (the path of P when rolling). Red arrow at P for the in-plane weight component Mg sin gamma along i, with its dashed components Mg sin gamma cos theta along i_2 and Mg sin gamma sin theta along -j_2. Projection of the rod as a thick line from O through P (the rod is above the plane, its projection lies on OP).
Values to draw: theta = 35 deg, d = 4.
Alt: Top view of the inclined plane with the auxiliary basis i_2, j_2 and the contact point P at distance d from O.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k$ (⊙) by $\theta$. $P$ is at the distance $d$ from $O$ along $\mathbf i_2$; with rolling, $P$ stays on the circle of radius $d$.

## rb22-rod-section
View: vertical section of the plane through O, P and G; i_2 to the right, k up, j_2 into the paper (⊗ at O).
Draw: the plane as a horizontal line (hatched below) from O to beyond P. Circle of radius R tangent to the plane at P, centre G. The rod as a thick line from O through G and beyond (drawn through the sphere as dashed inside). Right triangle OPG lightly shaded: leg d along the plane (label d), leg R from P to G (label R), hypotenuse L along the rod (label L = sqrt(d^2 + R^2)), angle beta at O with its arc (counterclockwise as seen). Grey axes i_2 and k at O; blue u along the rod and blue w normal to the rod (up-left), with the arc beta between i_2 and u and between k and w. Small triangles for u (adjacent leg cos beta along i_2, opposite sin beta along k) and for w (adjacent cos beta along k, opposite sin beta along -i_2). Near the top: text "rotation about -j_2 (counterclockwise as seen)".
Values to draw: R = 1, d = 2.15 (beta = 25 deg).
Alt: Section through O, P and G with the right triangle of the rod, the angle beta and the rod basis u, w.
Caption: Rod frame: $\mathbf u = \cos\beta\,\mathbf i_2 + \sin\beta\,\mathbf k$ and $\mathbf w = -\sin\beta\,\mathbf i_2 + \cos\beta\,\mathbf k$, from the right triangle $OPG$ with legs $d$, $R$ and hypotenuse $L$.

## rb22-rot-phi
View: along the rod, u out of the paper (⊙ at G), j_2 to the right, w up.
Draw: circle of radius R (the sphere seen along the rod), centre G. Thin grey axes j_2 and w. Orange body vectors e_2 and e_3 turned counterclockwise by phi. Arcs of phi from j_2 to e_2 and from w to e_3. Light translucent triangles: for e_2, adjacent leg cos phi along j_2 and dashed opposite leg sin phi along w; for e_3, adjacent leg cos phi along w and dashed opposite leg sin phi towards -j_2. A dot on the circle at the end of e_3 (a mark painted on the sphere). The direction towards the plane (roughly -w) indicated by a short hatched line below the circle labelled "plane".
Values to draw: phi = 40 deg.
Alt: The rotation phi of the sphere about the rod, measured from the normal w to the rod.
Caption: Rotation about the rod $\mathbf u$ (⊙) by $\varphi$, measured from $\mathbf w$, the normal to the rod in the vertical plane of $\mathbf i_2$ and $\mathbf k$ (read from the statement figure).

## rb22-exploded
View: same section as rb22-rod-section (i_2 right, k up, j_2 into the paper); exploded view with the rod drawn above and the sphere below, separated.
Draw: Top part, the rod alone (thick segment from O to G): at O the force R_O (green) with components drawn as F_w along w and a note "F_j along j_2 (normal to the paper)"; label "joint torque Q_O i_2 = 0". At G on the rod: the force -F_r (orange) = -F_w w and the torque -Q_r (curved orange arrow about j_2). Bottom part, the sphere alone: at G the rod force F_r = F_w w (orange, drawn in the -w direction since F_w is negative near equilibrium, labelled F_w w) and the rod torque Q_r (curved arrow, label L F_w j_2); red weight Mg at G (vertical down, drawn tilted by gamma relative to the plane normal); at P green normal reaction N along k, green friction T_1 along -i_2 (uphill, label T_1) and a note "T_2 along j_2". Dashed arm from G to P labelled -R k. Dashed grey lines connecting each action-reaction pair between the two parts.
Values to draw: R = 1, d = 2.15, beta = 25 deg, gamma = 25 deg; drawn for theta = 0 (the equilibrium of part (g)), so the section contains the line of maximum slope and the vertical.
Alt: Exploded view of the rod and the sphere with the action-reaction pair at G, the joint force at O and the forces of the plane at P.
Caption: Exploded view. The massless rod is in equilibrium under $\mathbf R_O$ at $O$ and $-\mathbf F_r$, $-\mathbf Q_r$ at $G$, which gives $\mathbf R_O = \mathbf F_r$ and $\mathbf Q_r = -L\,\mathbf u\times\mathbf F_r$.
