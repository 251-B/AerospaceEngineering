## rb25-theta
View: vertical plane containing the axis of the cylinder, j_1 out of the paper (⊙ at G, labelled j_1 = j_2); k_1 to the right, i_1 up (because k_1 x i_1 = j_1).
Draw: thin grey axes k_1 (right) and i_1 (up) from G, labelled. Rotated axes in blue: i_2 (= x_B, the cylinder axis) at angle theta from i_1 towards -k_1 (counterclockwise in this view, leaning left), k_2 at angle theta from k_1 towards i_1 (counterclockwise). Counterclockwise arcs theta from i_1 to i_2 and from k_1 to k_2, labelled theta. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_2: hypotenuse 1, leg cos theta along i_1, leg -sin theta along -k_1 (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for k_2: leg cos theta along k_1, leg sin theta along i_1 (dashed). Points C and D on the axis i_2 at distances -l and +l from G (dots, labelled), with the vertical drop of D below C, 2l sin theta, as a dashed dimension measured along k_1. Note beside the figure: "in the usual side view (k up) i_2 dips below the horizontal by theta".
Values to draw: theta = 25 deg; unit length 150 px; l = 1 unit.
Alt: Pitch rotation by theta about the horizontal axis j_1, tilting the cylinder axis below the horizontal.
Caption: Pitch: $\mathcal B_2$ is $\mathcal B_1$ turned by $\theta$ about $\mathbf j_1$ (⊙, with $\mathbf k_1$ to the right and $\mathbf i_1$ up). $\mathbf i_2$ leans to negative $\mathbf k_1$, which gives $-\sin\theta$: the axis dips below the horizontal.

## rb25-fbd
View: axonometric view like the statement figure: k up, i towards the lower left, j to the right; A at the origin and B at 3l along j on the ceiling.
Draw: ceiling points A and B with small hatched supports. The cylinder (outline as in the statement figure) with its axis x_B through C, G, D; C and D as dots at the centres of the end faces. The strings removed and replaced by thick red arrows: at C the force T_C pointing from C towards A, labelled T_C; at D the force T_D pointing from D towards B, labelled T_D; thin dashed red lines from C to A and from D to B showing the string directions. Weight mg as a thick red arrow straight down at G. Arms r_G^C = -l i_2 and r_G^D = l i_2 as blue arrows along the axis from G. Fixed axes x, y, z at A in grey.
Values to draw: beta = 70 deg, theta = 15 deg, phi = 20 deg; l = 1 unit = 90 px; G placed so that both strings have length l (approximately G = (0.2, 1.5, -0.8) l).
Alt: Free-body diagram of the hanging cylinder with its weight and the two string tensions along the strings.
Caption: Free-body diagram: weight $-mg\,\mathbf k$ at $G$, $T_C$ at $C$ towards $A$ and $T_D$ at $D$ towards $B$. Both strings act on the axis, so they have no moment about it.
