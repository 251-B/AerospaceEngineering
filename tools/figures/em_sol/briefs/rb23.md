## rb23-rot-theta
View: plane of the plate, k1 out of the paper (⊙ drawn at G), i1 to the right, j1 up; origin at G.
Draw: thin grey axes i1 and j1 from G. Blue i_B and j_B turned counterclockwise by theta (i_B up and to the right, j_B up and to the left). Arc of theta (counterclockwise, with arrowhead) from i1 to i_B and from j1 to j_B, both labelled theta. Light translucent triangles: for i_B, hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along i1 labelled cos theta, dashed opposite leg parallel to j1 labelled sin theta; for j_B, hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along j1 labelled cos theta, dashed opposite leg towards negative i1 labelled sin theta. Faint outline of the long side of the plate along j_B, labelled "longitudinal axis y_B".
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg.
Alt: The body basis of the plate, turned counterclockwise by theta from the fixed basis.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_1$ (⊙) by $\theta$. The longitudinal axis $\mathbf j_B$ is $\mathbf j_1$ turned counterclockwise, so its component along $\mathbf i_1$ is minus the sine of $\theta$.

## rb23-contact
View: plane of the plate, k1 out of the paper (⊙ at O), i1 to the right, j1 up; origin O at the centre of the knob.
Draw: the fixed knob, a grey disc of radius a1 centred at O (hatched, "fixed"). The hole, a white circle of radius a2 = 3 a1 with centre G, touching the knob from outside the knob and inside the hole at the point I. O, I and G on one straight line: I up and to the left of O, G down and to the right of O. Thin grey axes i1, j1 from O. Green unit vectors at O: u2 along OI (labelled u2) and u1 normal to it (labelled u1), both turned counterclockwise by phi from j1 and i1. Arc of phi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from j1 to u2, labelled phi; second arc from i1 to u1, labelled phi. Light translucent orange triangle for u2: hypotenuse 1, leg along j1 labelled cos phi, dashed leg towards negative i1 labelled sin phi. Dimensions: OI labelled a1, GI labelled a2, OG labelled a2 - a1 (on the line, on the side of G). Point G dot with the velocity arrow along u1 labelled (a2 - a1) phi-dot. Faint dashed long axis of the plate through G at theta = 20 deg from the vertical (to show that theta and phi differ).
Values to draw: a1 = 1, a2 = 3, phi = 30 deg, theta = 20 deg.
Alt: The knob and the hole in internal contact at I, with O, I and G aligned along u2 and G at a2 minus a1 from O.
Caption: Contact geometry. The common normal $\mathbf u_2$ is $\mathbf j_1$ turned by $\phi$; $G$ lies on it at $a_2 - a_1$ from $O$, opposite to $I$, so $\mathbf r_1^G$ is $-(a_2 - a_1)\,\mathbf u_2$.

## rb23-composite
View: plane of the plate in its body axes, k1 out of the paper, origin G, i_B (short side) down as in the statement figure (a), j_B (long side) to the right.
Draw: left: full rectangle h by w (light grey) labelled "Q: mass 4m/3", with a thin vertical strip element at y of width dy and a small square dx dy, labelled "dm = sigma dx dy"; coordinates x from -w/2 to w/2 and y from -w to w marked. Minus sign. Middle: disc of radius a2 centred at G labelled "H: mass m/3", with a thin annular element of radius rho and width d rho (dashed edges) labelled "dm = sigma rho d rho d chi". Equals sign. Right: the plate with the hole, G marked at the common centre.
Values to draw: w = 2, h = 4, a2 = 0.8.
Alt: The plate as a full rectangle minus a centred disc, with the Cartesian and polar mass elements.
Caption: Composite body: full rectangle of mass $\tfrac43 m$ minus the hole of mass $\tfrac13 m$, both centred at $G$. The rectangle is integrated in Cartesian coordinates, the hole in polar coordinates with the jacobian $\rho$.

## rb23-fbd
View: plane of the plate, same position as rb23-contact (k1 out of the paper).
Draw: outline of the plate with its hole (the plate tilted with its long axis at theta = 20 deg), the knob faint grey. At G: weight -mg j1 (red, downwards), label mg. At I: normal reaction N u2 (green, along OI, pointing away from O, i.e. up and to the left), label N; friction F u1 (green, along the tangent u1 at I), label F. Dashed arm from G to I labelled a2 u2. Curved arrow theta-ddot counterclockwise about G. Small green basis u1, u2 at I.
Values to draw: as in rb23-contact.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the plate: weight at G, normal reaction and friction of the knob at I.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The weight has no moment about $G$; the normal reaction acts along $GI$ and has no moment either, so only the friction $F$ turns the plate, with the arm $a_2$.
