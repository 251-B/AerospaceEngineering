# Briefs of problem 25 (particle, Topic 5): figures and simulator

The module `tools/figures/em_sol/p25.py` holds `p25-orbit` and `p25-cord`. Both names are kept, but the labels changed (the inertial basis is now written i0, j0, k0, the unit tangent of the orbit e_t, the frame basis i1, j1, k1 with k1 = k0), so both are briefed again in full below. One figure is new: `p25-fbd`. Colour code (as in the module): fixed axes grey; moving basis i1, j1 blue; cord basis e_c, e_theta and the tangent e_t orange; orbit dark grey dashed; actual forces (tension, gravity) pink solid; inertia forces pink dashed with the word "inertia" in their label; right triangles light translucent fill.

## p25-orbit
View: the orbital plane seen from the side of k0, which points OUT of the paper (⊙ at F, label "k0"); i0 to the right, j0 up; origin at the focus F.
Draw: grey axes "x (i0)" and "y (j0)" through F. The orbit as a dashed ellipse, the conic r = p/(1 + e cos nu) with F at the origin, drawn for nu from 0 to 360 degrees (F is then the right focus and the periapsis lies on the positive x axis). The spacecraft S on the orbit at nu = 110 degrees (filled dot, label "S"). The segment FS (thick, label "r" beside it). The shaded right triangle with the right angle at the foot of the perpendicular from S to the x axis: leg along the x axis labelled "r cos ν" (it lies on the negative side of the axis at this nu: label it "r cos ν (negative here)"), vertical leg labelled "r sin ν", hypotenuse FS. The angle arc nu at F from the x axis to FS, counterclockwise, label "ν". At S: blue "i1" along FS outwards (away from F), blue "j1" (i1 turned +90 degrees, counterclockwise), both 0.35 p; orange "e_t" the unit tangent of the orbit at S in the sense of growing nu, 0.35 p. A small curved arrow along the orbit near S labelled "ν̇ > 0".
Values to draw: p = 1 (unit 150 px), e = 0.5, nu = 110 degrees, so r = 1/(1 + 0.5 cos 110°) = 1.206 p.
Alt: Orbit of the spacecraft about F with the true anomaly, the right triangle of the radius and the moving basis at S.
Caption: Orbital plane seen from $+\mathbf k_0$ (⊙). The shaded right triangle has hypotenuse $|FS| = r$, leg $r\cos\nu$ along $\mathbf i_0$ (adjacent to $\nu$) and leg $r\sin\nu$ along $\mathbf j_0$ (opposite). $\mathbf i_1$ is the radial direction and $\mathbf j_1$ is $\mathbf i_1$ turned $+90^\circ$ (blue); $\mathbf e_t$ is the unit tangent of the orbit (orange).

## p25-cord
View: the moving frame Sx1y1 with i1 to the right, j1 up and k1 OUT of the paper (⊙ at S, label "k1"); origin at S. F lies to the left, off the drawing: an arrow at the left edge labelled "towards F (−i1)".
Draw: blue axes "x1 (i1)" and "y1 (j1)" through S. The astronaut P at theta = 40 degrees from x1, counterclockwise (filled dot, label "P"); the cord SP thick, labelled "L". The shaded right triangle with the right angle at the foot of the perpendicular from P to the x1 axis: leg "L cos θ" along i1, leg "L sin θ" along j1, hypotenuse the cord. The angle arc theta at S from x1 to SP, counterclockwise, label "θ". Orange unit vectors at P: "e_c" along SP outwards, "e_θ" (e_c turned +90 degrees), 0.3 L each. A grey axis "i0" through S at the angle −nu from i1 (clockwise by nu = 110 degrees, so it points down and to the left), with an arc between i0 and the cord labelled "θ + ν".
Values to draw: theta = 40 degrees, nu = 110 degrees; L = 1 (unit 180 px).
Alt: The astronaut on the circle of radius L about the spacecraft, with the right triangle of the cord and the cord basis.
Caption: Frame $Sx_1y_1$ seen from $+\mathbf k_1$ (⊙), with $\mathbf i_1$ to the right. In the shaded triangle the hypotenuse is the cord $|SP| = L$, the leg adjacent to $\theta$ is $L\cos\theta$ (along $\mathbf i_1$) and the opposite leg is $L\sin\theta$ (along $\mathbf j_1$). The inertial axis $\mathbf i_0$ (grey) is at $-\nu$ from $\mathbf i_1$, so the cord makes $\theta + \nu$ with it.

## p25-fbd
View: the moving frame as in p25-cord (i1 right, j1 up, k1 out of the paper, ⊙ at S), the astronaut P at theta = 40 degrees, cord SP drawn thin.
Draw: forces at P, each a separate arrow with its label placed off the lines and the other arrows: pink "−T e_c" from P towards S along the cord, 0.8 units; pink "m g_S = −m μ_F / r² i1" along −i1 (to the left), 0.6 units; pink dashed "F_A = −m a_S (inertia)" drawn along +i1 (to the right), 0.6 units, with the note "equal and opposite to m g_S in free flight"; pink dashed "F_E = −m L ν̈ e_θ (inertia)" along −e_theta, 0.5 units (drawn for nu-double-dot positive); pink dashed "F_C = m L ν̇² e_c (inertia)" along +e_c (outwards), 0.55 units; pink dashed "F_COR = 2 m L ν̇ θ̇ e_c (inertia)" along +e_c, 0.45 units, offset sideways by 0.08 units so it does not overlap F_C (drawn for nu-dot and theta-dot positive). Orange e_c and e_theta at P, 0.3 units. A curved arrow about S labelled "ω10 = ν̇ k1" (counterclockwise).
Values to draw: theta = 40 degrees; unit 180 px; nu-dot, nu-double-dot and theta-dot positive.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the astronaut in the moving frame: cord tension, gravity and the four inertia forces.
Caption: Forces on $P$ in $S_1$: the tension $-T\,\mathbf e_c$, the gravity $m\,\mathbf g_S$ (equal at $P$ and $S$ by hypothesis) and the inertia forces $\mathbf F_A = -m\,\mathbf a_0^S$, $\mathbf F_E$ across the cord and $\mathbf F_C$, $\mathbf F_{COR}$ along it. In free flight $m\,\mathbf g_S$ and $\mathbf F_A$ cancel.
