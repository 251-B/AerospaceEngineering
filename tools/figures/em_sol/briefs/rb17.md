## rb17-rot-theta
View: vertical plane of the arm of the fork; j1 (along the axis AB) points out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O1), i1 to the right, k1 down. Origin O1 at the upper left of the drawing.
Draw: thin grey axes i1 (right) and k1 (down) from O1, with a short horizontal hatched stub at O1 for the axis AB seen end-on (small circle with ⊙ labelled "AB, j1"). The arm of the fork as a thick grey line from O1 going down to the right along k2. Blue k2 (along the arm, from O1) and blue i2 (normal to the arm, pointing up-right). Arc of theta from k1 to k2 (counterclockwise as seen) and arc of theta from i1 to i2 (counterclockwise as seen), both labelled theta. Light translucent triangles: for k2, adjacent leg cos theta along k1 and dashed opposite leg sin theta along i1; for i2, adjacent leg cos theta along i1 and dashed opposite leg sin theta pointing up (towards -k1). Points on the arm: G at distance a from O1 (dot, label G, dimension a) and O at distance b (dot, label O, dimension b), with b larger than a. At O draw the disc edge-on: its plane contains j2 (the viewing direction) and k2, so it is a thick light-grey segment of length 2R along k2 centred at O, drawn slightly wider than the arm and labelled "disc, edge-on"; a short dashed line through O along i2 labelled "CD". Red weight vectors Mg at G and mg at O, vertical down (along k1).
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, a = 2, b = 4, R = 1.2 (draw b + R = 5.2 so the disc ends beyond O).
Alt: Vertical plane of the fork arm with the fixed basis i1, k1, the fork basis i2, k2 turned by theta about the axis AB, and the points G and O on the arm.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf j_1$ (⊙) by $\theta$: $\mathbf i_2 = \cos\theta\,\mathbf i_1 - \sin\theta\,\mathbf k_1$, $\mathbf k_2 = \sin\theta\,\mathbf i_1 + \cos\theta\,\mathbf k_1$; $G$ and $O$ lie on the arm at the distances $a$ and $b$ from $O_1$.

## rb17-rot-phi
View: the plane of the disc, seen along the bearing axis CD; i2 points out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O), j2 to the right, k2 up. Origin O at the centre.
Draw: circle of radius R centred at O (the disc), lightly filled. Thin grey axes j2 (right) and k2 (up) from O; note that j2 is parallel to AB and k2 points along the arm away from O1 (write "towards O1" with a small arrow pointing down, along -k2, outside the circle). Orange disc vectors j0 and k0 turned counterclockwise by phi. Arcs of phi from j2 to j0 and from k2 to k0 (counterclockwise as seen), labelled phi. Light translucent triangles: for j0, adjacent leg cos phi along j2 and dashed opposite leg sin phi along k2; for k0, adjacent leg cos phi along k2 and dashed opposite leg sin phi towards -j2. A dot on the rim at the tip direction of j0 (a mark painted on the disc). A curved arrow around O labelled "rate of phi, about i2".
Values to draw: phi = 35 deg, R = 3.
Alt: The disc seen along its axis, with the fork basis j2, k2 and the disc basis j0, k0 turned by phi about i2.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf i_2$ (⊙) by $\varphi$: $\mathbf j_0 = \cos\varphi\,\mathbf j_2 + \sin\varphi\,\mathbf k_2$, $\mathbf k_0 = -\sin\varphi\,\mathbf j_2 + \cos\varphi\,\mathbf k_2$. The sense of $\varphi$ is a reading of the statement figure.

## rb17-exploded
View: same as rb17-rot-theta (j2 out of the paper, ⊙), the plane of the arm drawn at theta = 30 deg, with i2 up-right and k2 down-right along the arm; exploded: the fork drawn on the upper left, the disc drawn separately below right, shifted along k2.
Draw: Upper part, the fork alone: thick arm from O1 to O. At O1 the green pin force R_O1 drawn with two components, R_1x along i2 and R_1z along k2 (label "R_O1"), and a green torque arrow labelled "Q_O1 (along i2 and k2 only)". Red weight Mg at G (vertical down, along k1), with G at distance a. At the end O of the fork: orange force -R_O (drawn as two components, -R_O,x along -i2 and -R_O,z along -k2) and an orange curved arrow labelled "-Q_O". Lower part, the disc alone (edge-on segment of length 2R along k2 centred at O): orange force R_O at O (components R_O,x along i2, R_O,z along k2) and orange curved arrow labelled "Q_O (along j2 and k2 only)"; red weight mg at O (vertical down). Dashed grey lines joining O on the fork with O on the disc, to show the action-reaction pair. Small blue basis i2, k2 at a corner (j2 ⊙).
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, a = 2, b = 4, R = 1.2; separation of the two parts 2 units along k2.
Alt: Exploded view of the fork and the disc with the pin loads at O1, the bearing pair at O and the two weights.
Caption: Exploded view: the bearing acts on the disc with $\mathbf R_O$, $\mathbf Q_O$ (no component along $\mathbf i_2$) and on the fork with $-\mathbf R_O$, $-\mathbf Q_O$; the pin acts on the fork with $\mathbf R_{O_1}$, $\mathbf Q_{O_1}$ (no component along $\mathbf j_2$).
