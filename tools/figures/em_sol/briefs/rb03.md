## rb03-gimbal-chain
View: oblique (cabinet) 3D schematic of the gyroscope at a general instant; X towards the lower left, Y to the right, Z up, origin O at the centre of the flywheel.
Draw: thin grey fixed axes X, Y, Z from O, labelled. Outer gimbal as a thin rectangle outline (grey-blue) whose pivot axis is the Y axis, with bearings A and B drawn as small dots on Y on both sides, labelled A and B. Inner gimbal as a smaller rectangle outline (green) pivoting on the outer gimbal about the axis CD (direction i_1, in the XZ plane, turned by beta from X about Y), bearings C and D as dots labelled, with D on the far side and C on the near side (i_1 points from D to C). Flywheel as an ellipse (orange outline, light translucent fill) whose axle EF is along j_2 (tilted from Y by gamma about i_1), bearings E and F labelled. Thick unit vectors: j along Y labelled "beta-dot j" (grey-blue), i_1 along DC labelled "gamma-dot i_1" (green), j_2 along EF labelled "theta-dot j_2" (orange). Small curved arrows around each axis showing the positive sense of beta, gamma and theta (right-hand rule).
Values to draw: beta = 25 deg, gamma = 30 deg, theta irrelevant (draw one radial mark on the flywheel).
Alt: Schematic of the outer gimbal, inner gimbal and flywheel with their three rotation axes AB, CD and EF.
Caption: Kinematic chain of the gyroscope: the outer gimbal turns by $\beta$ about the fixed axis $AB$ ($Y$), the inner gimbal by $\gamma$ about $CD$, and the flywheel by $\theta$ about its axle $EF$; each rate acts along its own bearing axis.

## rb03-rotation-beta
View: looking down the fixed Y axis: j points out of the paper (⊙ at the centre), k to the right, i up.
Draw: thin grey axes k (right) and i (up), labelled k and i. Thick blue unit vectors i_1 = cos β i − sin β k (turned counterclockwise from i, i.e. leaning to the left of the up axis) and k_1 = sin β i + cos β k (turned counterclockwise from k, above the right axis), labelled i_1 and k_1. Counterclockwise angle arcs with arrowheads from i to i_1 and from k to k_1, each labelled β. Dashed projections of the tip of i_1 onto i (cos β) and onto −k (sin β), with a light translucent orange right triangle and leg labels. Label the ⊙ "j = j_1 (axis AB)". Label i_1 also "axis CD (D to C)".
Values to draw: beta = 30 deg, unit vectors of length 1.
Alt: View along the bearing axis AB showing the outer gimbal rotation by beta.
Caption: Outer gimbal, viewed with $\mathbf j$ out of the paper ($\mathbf k$ to the right, $\mathbf i$ up). A counterclockwise turn by $\beta$ makes $\mathbf i_1$ lean towards $-\mathbf k$, which is the minus sign in the third row of the matrix.

## rb03-rotation-gamma
View: looking down the axis CD: i_1 points out of the paper (⊙ at the centre), j_1 to the right, k_1 up.
Draw: thin grey axes j_1 (right) and k_1 (up), labelled. Thick green unit vectors j_2 = cos γ j_1 + sin γ k_1 (above the right axis) and k_2 = −sin γ j_1 + cos γ k_1 (left of the up axis), labelled j_2 and k_2. Counterclockwise arcs with arrowheads from j_1 to j_2 and from k_1 to k_2, each labelled γ. Dashed projections of the tip of j_2 with a light translucent orange right triangle (hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg cos γ along j_1, opposite leg sin γ along k_1) and leg labels. Label the ⊙ "i_1 = i_2 (axis CD)"; label j_2 also "axle EF".
Values to draw: gamma = 30 deg, unit vectors of length 1.
Alt: View along the bearing axis CD showing the inner gimbal rotation by gamma.
Caption: Inner gimbal, viewed with $\mathbf i_1$ out of the paper. The counterclockwise turn by $\gamma$ tilts the flywheel axle $\mathbf j_2$ from $\mathbf j_1$ towards $\mathbf k_1$.

## rb03-rotation-theta
View: looking down the flywheel axle EF: j_2 points out of the paper (⊙ at the centre), k_2 to the right, i_2 up.
Draw: a light orange circle (the flywheel face) centred at the ⊙. Thin grey axes k_2 (right) and i_2 (up), labelled. Thick orange unit vectors i_B = cos θ i_2 − sin θ k_2 (leaning to the left of the up axis) and k_B = sin θ i_2 + cos θ k_2 (above the right axis), labelled i_B (x) and k_B (z). Counterclockwise arcs with arrowheads from i_2 to i_B and from k_2 to k_B, each labelled θ. Dashed projections of the tip of i_B with a light translucent right triangle (hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg cos θ along i_2, opposite leg sin θ along −k_2) and leg labels. Label the ⊙ "j_2 = j_B (axle EF, y)".
Values to draw: theta = 40 deg, unit vectors of length 1.
Alt: View along the flywheel axle showing the spin rotation by theta.
Caption: Flywheel, viewed with its axle $\mathbf j_2$ out of the paper ($\mathbf k_2$ to the right, $\mathbf i_2$ up). The spin by $\theta$ makes $\mathbf i_B$ lean towards $-\mathbf k_2$, as in the outer gimbal view.
