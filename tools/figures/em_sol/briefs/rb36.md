## rb36-gravity
View: vertical plane of motion, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O), horizontal e_h to the right and upward vertical e_v drawn in thin grey. Origin O on the slope line.
Draw: the slope as a thick grey line rising to the right at gamma above a dashed horizontal line, arc of gamma between them at the lower left (counterclockwise, label gamma). Blue basis at O: i0 along the slope (up and to the right), j0 normal to it (up and to the left). Grey e_h and e_v at O. Arc of gamma between e_h and i0 and between e_v and j0 (both counterclockwise, labelled gamma). Red gravity vector g pointing straight down from a point above the slope; light translucent right triangle with g as hypotenuse, the leg along -j0 labelled "g cos gamma" (adjacent to gamma) and the dashed leg along -i0 labelled "g sin gamma" (opposite to gamma); arc of gamma between g and the -j0 leg.
Values to draw: gamma = 15 deg.
Alt: The slope basis i0, j0 turned by gamma from the horizontal and the right triangle that splits gravity into g sin gamma down the slope and g cos gamma into the slope.
Caption: $\mathbf g = -g(\sin\gamma\,\mathbf i_0 + \cos\gamma\,\mathbf j_0)$: the hypotenuse $g$, the leg $g\cos\gamma$ normal to the slope and the leg $g\sin\gamma$ down the slope.

## rb36-rot-phi
View: plane of motion, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at C), i0 along the slope to the right, j0 normal to the slope, drawn as grey axes through C (rotate the whole drawing so that i0 is horizontal). Origin C at the centre of the cylinder.
Draw: the cylinder outline (circle radius R, light grey) with the slot as a white rounded band along i1 through C (length 2 ell). Grey i0, j0 from C; blue i1 (along the slot, down to the right) and j1 (up to the right) turned clockwise by phi. Clockwise arc of phi from j0 to j1, labelled phi, and clockwise arc of phi from i0 to i1, labelled phi. Light translucent triangle for i1: adjacent leg "cos phi" along i0, dashed opposite leg "sin phi" along -j0. Light translucent triangle for j1: adjacent leg "cos phi" along j0, dashed opposite leg "sin phi" along +i0. Curved arrow about C, clockwise, labelled "omega_10 = -phi-dot k0". Particle P as a black dot on the slot at x1 along i1 (label P, dimension x1 from C). Contact point A at the bottom (label A), short segment of the slope below it.
Values to draw: R = 3, ell = 2.2, phi = 30 deg, x1 = 1.5.
Alt: The cylinder basis i1, j1 turned clockwise by phi from the slope basis i0, j0, with the slot along i1 and the particle P on it.
Caption: Rotation by $\varphi$ about $-\mathbf k_0$ (clockwise as seen): $\mathbf i_1 = \cos\varphi\,\mathbf i_0 - \sin\varphi\,\mathbf j_0$, $\mathbf j_1 = \sin\varphi\,\mathbf i_0 + \cos\varphi\,\mathbf j_0$. The sense of $\varphi$ is a reading of the statement figure.

## rb36-exploded
View: plane of motion, k0 out of the paper, i0 along the slope (drawn horizontal), j0 normal to it. Exploded: the particle P drawn 1.2 R outside the cylinder along j1, the cylinder in place on the slope.
Draw: Cylinder alone (circle radius R, slot along i1 at phi = 30 deg): red weight M g at C pointing straight down in the true vertical (show the slope angle gamma by tilting a small "vertical" grey reference arrow by gamma from -j0), green contact force at A with components f_A along i0 and N_A along j0, orange force F at the cylinder point P with its components F_1 along i1 (label "F_1: push of the motor, along the slot") and F_2 along j1 (label "F_2: slot walls"). Particle alone: red weight m g (true vertical), orange force -F with components -F_1 i1 and -F_2 j1. Dashed grey line joining the two copies of P (action-reaction pair). Dimension x1 from C to P along the slot, arm R from C to A.
Values to draw: R = 3, ell = 2.2, phi = 30 deg, x1 = 1.5, gamma = 10 deg.
Alt: Exploded view of the cylinder and the particle with the force pair F and -F at P, the weights and the contact force of the slope at A.
Caption: Exploded view: the particle acts on the cylinder with $\mathbf F = F_1\mathbf i_1 + F_2\mathbf j_1$ at $P$ and the cylinder on the particle with $-\mathbf F$; the slope acts at $A$ with $f_A\mathbf i_0 + N_A\mathbf j_0$.
