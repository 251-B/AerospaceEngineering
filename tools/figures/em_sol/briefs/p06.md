# Figure briefs, Problem 06 (Particle on Sinusoidal Curve)

Colour code: fixed axes grey; curve black; tangent and auxiliary normal blue; principal normal orange; binormal green (shown as ⊙ or ⊗ symbols).

## p06-curve-plane
kind: plane / meridian section
view: k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O); i0 to the right, j0 up.
draw: the curve y = sin x for x from 0 to 2 pi (equal scales on both axes, 1 m = 60 px), grey axes i0, j0 through O with tick marks at pi/2, pi, 3 pi/2, 2 pi and at y = 1, -1. Point P at x = pi/4, y = 0.707. At P: blue e_t (unit, at angle gamma = arctan(cos(pi/4)) = 35.3 deg above the horizontal), blue dashed e_perp (90 deg counterclockwise from e_t, up and to the left), orange e_n = -e_perp (down and to the right, toward the concave side), and the green symbol ⊗ at P for e_b = -k0 (into the paper). Zoomed inset (circle magnifier next to P) showing the differential right triangle at P: horizontal leg dx, vertical leg dy = cos x dx, hypotenuse ds = L dx along the curve, shaded light blue, angle arc gamma between dx and ds. Second point Q at x = 5 pi/4 (y = -0.707) on the lower arch with its e_t (slope cos(5pi/4) = -0.707, so e_t points down and to the right at -35.3 deg), orange e_n = (0.577, 0.816), pointing up and to the right toward the concave side (perpendicular to e_t), and green ⊙ for e_b = +k0. Small marks at the inflection points x = 0, pi, 2 pi with the note "e_n undefined".
labels: "O", "P (x = pi/4)", "Q (x = 5 pi/4)", "i0", "j0", "k0" by ⊙ at O, "y = sin x", "e_t", "e_perp", "e_n", "e_b = -k0 (⊗)" at P, "e_b = +k0 (⊙)" at Q, in the inset "dx" (adjacent leg), "dy = cos x dx" (opposite leg), "ds = L dx" (hypotenuse), "gamma".
alt: The sine curve with the intrinsic basis drawn on an upper arch and on a lower arch, and the differential triangle of the arc element.
caption: Plane of the trajectory. The differential triangle has hypotenuse ds = L dx, adjacent leg dx (cos gamma) and opposite leg dy (sin gamma); the normal points to the concave side and flips between arches together with the binormal.

## p06-rotation-view
kind: rotation view
view: along k0, out of the paper (⊙ at the common origin); i0 right, j0 up.
draw: drawing configuration gamma = 35.3 deg (x = pi/4). Common origin with ⊙ k0. Grey fixed axes i0, j0. Blue rotated axes e_t at 35.3 deg and e_perp at 125.3 deg. Arc gamma from i0 to e_t (counterclockwise) and arc gamma from j0 to e_perp (counterclockwise). Dashed projections of the tip of e_t on i0 (length cos gamma = 1/L) and on j0 (length sin gamma = cos x / L), small right triangle shaded. Dashed projections of the tip of e_perp on the negative i0 axis (length sin gamma) and on j0 (length cos gamma). Short note: "on an upper arch gamma decreases (tangent turns clockwise)".
labels: "i0", "j0", "k0 (⊙)", "e_t", "e_perp", "gamma" (both arcs), "cos(gamma) = 1/L", "sin(gamma) = cos x / L", "-sin(gamma)" on the left projection, "cos(gamma)".
alt: View along k0 of the fixed axes and of the tangent and auxiliary normal turned by the tangent angle gamma.
caption: The tangent basis is the fixed basis turned by gamma about k0 (out of the paper); the component of e_perp along i0 is negative because e_perp leans toward minus i0.

## p06-osculating-circles
kind: other
view: k0 out of the paper; i0 right, j0 up; same scale as p06-curve-plane.
draw: the curve y = sin x for x from 0 to 2 pi. At the crest C1 = (pi/2, 1): osculating circle of radius 1 centred at (pi/2, 0) (dashed orange), orange e_n = -j0 from the crest toward the centre, blue e_t = i0. At the trough C2 = (3 pi/2, -1): osculating circle of radius 1 centred at (3 pi/2, 0) (dashed orange), e_n = +j0. At P = (pi/4, 0.707): part of the osculating circle of radius 2.598 (dashed, lighter), with its centre on the line through P along e_n (down-right direction at -54.7 deg), at P + 2.598 e_n = (0.785 + 2.598*0.577, 0.707 - 2.598*0.816) = (2.28, -1.41); draw only the arc near P. Mark the inflection point (pi, 0) with a small cross and the note "rho -> infinity".
labels: "rho = 1 m" on the radius at the crest, "rho = 1 m" at the trough, "rho = 2.598 m" at P, "e_n", "e_t", "rho -> infinity" at x = pi.
alt: Osculating circles of the sine curve at a crest, a trough and an intermediate point.
caption: The radius of curvature is 1 m at the crests and troughs, grows away from them (2.598 m at x = pi/4) and tends to infinity at the inflection points.
