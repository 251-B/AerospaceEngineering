# Briefs of problem 22 (particle, Topic 5): figures and simulator

The module `tools/figures/em_sol/p22.py` holds `p22-curve-plane` and `p22-top-view`. Both names are kept, but the labels changed (the solution now writes the fixed basis as i0, j0, k0 and the turning basis as i1, j1, k1, with j1 = j0), so both are briefed again in full below. One figure is new: `p22-fbd`. Colour code (as in the module): fixed axes grey; turning basis i1, j1, k1 blue; curve basis e_t, n orange; curve dark grey; actual forces (weight, reaction) pink solid; inertia forces pink dashed with the word "inertia" in their label; right triangles light translucent fill.

## p22-top-view
View: from above, along j0 = j1, which points OUT of the paper (⊙ at O, label "j0 = j1"). Origin O at the centre.
Draw: grey fixed axes "i0" to the right and "k0" DOWN the page, with a grey dashed half-axis up the page labelled "−k0". Blue "i1" (i0 turned counterclockwise by Omega t) and blue "k1" (k0 turned counterclockwise by the same angle, so its tip leans to the right of the downward axis). Angle arc "Ωt" between i0 and i1 and a second arc "Ωt" between k0 and k1, both counterclockwise. Dashed components of i1: "cos Ωt" along i0 (label under the axis) and "sin Ωt" up the page, along −k0 (label to the left). A note at the upper right: "view from +Y; Ω > 0: counterclockwise". The plane of the curve seen edge-on as a thick dark grey segment along i1 through O, labelled "plane of the curve".
Values to draw: Omega t = 35 degrees; unit length 165 px.
Alt: Top view along the vertical axis with the fixed axes i0, k0 and the turning axes i1, k1 rotated by Omega t.
Caption: View from $+Y$, with $\mathbf j_0 = \mathbf j_1$ out of the paper (⊙): $\mathbf k_0$ points down the page. $\mathbf i_1$ has the components $\cos\Omega t$ along $\mathbf i_0$ and $\sin\Omega t$ along $-\mathbf k_0$ (up the page), hence the minus sign in $\mathbf i_1 = \cos\Omega t\,\mathbf i_0 - \sin\Omega t\,\mathbf k_0$.
