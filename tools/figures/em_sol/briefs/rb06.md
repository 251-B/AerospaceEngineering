## rb06-rotation-psi
View: top view of the plane Oxy, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at the origin O); i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from O, labelled i and j, with the symbol ⊙ and the label k at O. Rotated axes in blue from O: i_1 at +45 deg and j_1 at +135 deg (thick arrows, labelled i_1, j_1). Counterclockwise arc of 45 deg between i and i_1, labelled "45°", and the same arc between j and j_1, labelled "45°". Shaded right triangle (light blue fill) for i_1: hypotenuse = i_1 (length 1, labelled 1), adjacent leg along i labelled cos 45°, opposite leg parallel to j labelled sin 45°, dashed. Second shaded triangle (light orange fill) for j_1: hypotenuse j_1 labelled 1, leg along -i labelled -sin 45° (dashed), leg parallel to j labelled cos 45°.
Values to draw: angle 45 deg; unit length about 140 px.
Alt: Top view of the first rotation of the spinner basis, 45 degrees about the vertical axis k, with the right triangles of its sines and cosines.
Caption: First simple rotation, by $45^\circ$ about $\mathbf k$ (⊙). The axis $\mathbf i_1$ takes the direction of the length of the spinner; $\mathbf j_1$ leans to negative $\mathbf i$, which gives the minus sign of $[{}_0R_1]$.

## rb06-rotation-flip
View: view along i_1, i_1 out of the paper (⊙ at the centre G); j_1 to the right, k_1 = k up.
Draw: thin grey axes j_1 (right) and k_1 (up) from G, labelled j_1 and k_1 = k, with ⊙ and the label i_1 = i_B at G. Body axes in blue: j_B pointing left (opposite to j_1) and k_B pointing down (opposite to k_1), thick arrows labelled j_B and k_B. A counterclockwise arc of 180 deg from k_1 round to k_B, labelled "π", and a counterclockwise arc of 180 deg from j_1 round to j_B, labelled "π".
Values to draw: angle 180 deg; unit length about 140 px.
Alt: View along the axis of the car showing the half turn that sends the vertical axis down and the lateral axis to the left.
Caption: Second simple rotation, a half turn about $\mathbf i_1$ (⊙): $\mathbf j_B$ is opposite to $\mathbf j_1$ and $\mathbf k_B$ points to the ground, as in the side view of the statement.

## rb06-iar
View: oblique (cabinet) view of the body frame at G: i_B to the right, k_B down, j_B into the page towards the upper right (drawn at 30 deg, foreshortened 0.5).
Draw: thin grey axes i_B, j_B, k_B from G, labelled. Point A at (L/2, L/4, 0) as a black square with label A. Velocity of G = (0, -3/2, 0) Omega L as an orange arrow from G labelled v_0^G (scale 0.4 per Omega L). Angular velocity (sqrt2, 0, -1) Omega as a green arrow from I labelled omega_B0 (scale 0.4 per Omega). Point I at (-L/2, 0, -sqrt2 L/2) (behind G along -i_B and above it, since -k_B is up) as a dot labelled I. Vector GI as a blue dashed arrow from G to I labelled GI, with a right-angle mark at I between GI and the axis. The instantaneous axis: dashed green line through I along (sqrt2, 0, -1), extended both ways, labelled IAR. Dashed projections of I: -L/2 along i_B and -sqrt2 L/2 along k_B, labelled L/2 and sqrt2 L/2.
Values to draw: L = 1 unit = 160 px; the numbers above.
Alt: Body axes at the centre of mass with the velocity of G, the angular velocity and the instantaneous axis through the point I.
Caption: Instantaneous axis of rotation of the spinner. $I$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $G$; the axis is parallel to $\boldsymbol\omega_{B0}$ and lies in the vertical plane of the car.
