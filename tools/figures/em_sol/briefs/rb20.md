## rb20-bases
View: the plane of the probe (plane of the statement figure); k0 (spin axis) points out of the paper (⊙ drawn at G), i0 to the right, j0 up. Origin G at the centre.
Draw: light-grey filled circle of radius R centred at G (the probe). Thin grey axes i0 and j0 from G. Blue probe basis i1, j1 turned counterclockwise by theta from i0, j0; arcs of theta from i0 to i1 and from j0 to j1, labelled theta. Mark the rim point A at the tip of i1 (dot, label A). Orange cord basis e_r, e_t: e_r turned counterclockwise by phi from i1, starting at G; e_t drawn at the rim point T (tip of e_r), tangent, pointing counterclockwise. Arc of phi from i1 to e_r, labelled phi. Light translucent triangles: for i1, adjacent leg cos theta along i0 and dashed opposite leg sin theta along j0; for j1, adjacent leg cos theta along j0 and dashed opposite leg sin theta towards -i0. Point H on the rim at 135 deg from i1 (counterclockwise), small open circle, label H. A curved arrow near the rim, counterclockwise, labelled "spin, rate of theta".
Values to draw: theta = 20 deg, phi = 60 deg, R = 3.
Alt: The probe seen along its spin axis with the inertial basis, the probe basis turned by theta and the cord basis turned by phi from the probe basis.
Caption: Rotations about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙): $\theta$ from $\mathbf i_0$ to $\mathbf i_1$ (probe) and $\phi$ from $\mathbf i_1$ to $\mathbf e_r$ (point of tangency $T$, measured in the probe from the release point $A$).

## rb20-involute
View: same plane, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at G); drawn in the probe frame: i1 to the right, j1 up.
Draw: grey circle of radius R centred at G. Thin grey axes i1, j1. Point A at (R, 0) (dot, label A). Point T on the rim at angle phi (dot, label T). Thick dark cord: the unwound arc from A to T drawn as a thin dashed arc (label "unwound arc R phi"), the wound part from T counterclockwise to H (at 135 deg) drawn as a thin solid line hugging the rim, and the straight free part from T to P (thick). Point P at R e_r - R phi e_t, i.e. at R(cos phi + phi sin phi, sin phi - phi cos phi) (filled dot, label P, "m/2"). Right triangle G-T-P lightly shaded, right-angle mark at T, leg GT labelled R, leg TP labelled "R phi", hypotenuse GP labelled "R sqrt(1 + phi^2)". Orange e_r at T (outwards along GT) and orange e_t at T (counterclockwise tangent); note that TP points along -e_t. Dashed involute curve traced by P for phi from 0 to 2.3 rad (points R(cos s + s sin s, sin s - s cos s)). At P, two blue velocity components: R phi Omega along e_r (drawn from P parallel to GT direction) and R theta-rate along e_t, labelled "R phi Omega e_r" and "R (rate of theta) e_t". Small text near G: "Omega: sum of the rates of theta and phi".
Values to draw: phi = 1.22 rad (70 deg), R = 3 (P then at about (4.47, 1.57)); velocity components drawn with lengths 1.6 (along e_r) and 1.0 (along e_t).
Alt: The probe frame with the right triangle GTP of the unwound cord, the involute traced by the mass and the two components of its velocity.
Caption: Taut cord tangent at $T$: $\mathbf r_0^P = R\,\mathbf e_r - R\phi\,\mathbf e_t$, with the right angle at $T$, legs $R$ and $R\phi$; relative to the probe, $P$ moves on the involute of the rim.

## rb20-exploded
View: same plane, k0 out of the paper (⊙); exploded: the probe in the middle, the mass P drawn separated up-right and the mass P' separated down-left, each with its cord stub.
Draw: probe as a grey circle of radius R centred at G, with T at angle phi = 70 deg and T' diametrically opposite (at 250 deg). At T a green force arrow along -e_t (pointing towards where P is, down-right tangent direction), labelled "-T_c e_t"; at T' a green force arrow along +e_t, labelled "T_c e_t". A curved green arrow at G, clockwise, labelled "M_G = -2 R T_c k0". At P (separated, at the position of rb20-involute shifted outwards by 1 unit along GP) a green arrow along +e_t (pointing back towards T, parallel to the cord), labelled "T_c e_t"; at P' the opposite arrow, labelled "-T_c e_t". Dashed grey lines joining T with P and T' with P' (the cords, cut), and short tick marks "pair" on each. Orange e_r, e_t drawn small at T. Note near the probe: "no gravity".
Values to draw: phi = 70 deg, R = 3, force arrow length 1.5.
Alt: Exploded view of the probe and the two yo-yo masses with the equal and opposite cord tensions.
Caption: Exploded view: each cord pulls its mass towards $T$ with $T_c$ and the rim towards the mass with $T_c$; the two forces on the probe cancel and their moment about $G$ is $-2RT_c\,\mathbf k_0$.

## rb20-graph
View: graph, horizontal axis omega0 t from 0 to 3, vertical axis from -0.8 to 1.0.
Draw: blue curve of the spin ratio omega/omega0 = (1 - K x^2)/(1 + K x^2) and orange curve of the dimensionless tension T_c R/(I omega0^2) = 2 K x/(1 + K x^2)^2, with x = omega0 t, both for K = 0.5. Grey horizontal line at 0. Mark the zero of the spin at x = 1.414 (dot, label "spin zero, x = 1/sqrt K") and the maximum of the tension at x = 0.816, value 0.459 (dot, label "maximum, x = 1/sqrt(3K)"). Legend: "omega/omega0" (blue), "T_c R/(I omega0^2)" (orange). Axis labels "omega0 t" and "ratio".
Values to draw: K = 0.5; check points (x, spin, tension): (0, 1, 0), (0.5, 0.778, 0.395), (1, 0.333, 0.444), (1.414, 0, 0.354), (2, -0.333, 0.222), (3, -0.636, 0.099).
Alt: Spin of the probe and tension of a cord against time for K equal to one half.
Caption: Part (a) and part (c) for $K = 0.5$: the spin falls from $\omega_0$ and changes sign at $\omega_0 t = 1/\sqrt K$; the tension starts at zero and peaks at $\omega_0 t = 1/\sqrt{3K}$. The curves stop being valid when the cords are released, at $t_f$.
