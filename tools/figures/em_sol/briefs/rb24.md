## rb24-psi
View: horizontal plane seen from above, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O, labelled k = k_1); i to the right, j up; origin O at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from O, labelled. Rotated axes in blue from O: i_1 at angle psi from i and j_1 at psi + 90 deg, thick arrows labelled i_1 and j_1. Counterclockwise arcs psi from i to i_1 and from j to j_1, both labelled psi. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_1: hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along i labelled cos psi, opposite leg parallel to j labelled sin psi (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for j_1: hypotenuse 1, leg along -i labelled -sin psi (dashed), leg along j labelled cos psi. Small label "line of nodes" next to i_1.
Values to draw: psi = 35 deg; unit length 150 px.
Alt: Precession rotation by psi about the vertical axis, seen from above.
Caption: Precession: $\mathcal B_1$ is $\mathcal B_0$ turned by $\psi$ about $\mathbf k$ (⊙). $\mathbf j_1$ leans to negative $\mathbf i$, which gives the minus sign of $[{}_0R_1]$; $\mathbf i_1$ is the line of nodes.

## rb24-theta
View: vertical plane normal to the line of nodes, i_1 out of the paper (⊙ at O, labelled i_1 = i_2); j_1 to the right, k_1 up; origin O.
Draw: thin grey axes j_1 (right) and k_1 (up) from O, labelled. Rotated axes in blue: j_2 at angle theta above j_1 and k_2 at angle theta to the left of k_1 (leaning towards -j_1), thick arrows labelled j_2 and k_2 = k_B (symmetry axis). Counterclockwise arcs theta from j_1 to j_2 and from k_1 to k_2, labelled theta. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for j_2: hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along j_1 labelled cos theta, opposite leg parallel to k_1 labelled sin theta (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for k_2: hypotenuse 1, vertical leg along k_1 labelled cos theta, horizontal leg along -j_1 labelled -sin theta (dashed). A dot G on k_2 at distance ell from O, labelled G, with the dimension ell along the axis and its height ell cos theta as a dashed vertical projection on k_1, labelled ell cos theta.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg (pi/6, the initial value); unit length 160 px; ell = 0.75 units.
Alt: Nutation rotation by theta about the line of nodes, with the symmetry axis tilted from the vertical.
Caption: Nutation: $\mathcal B_2$ is $\mathcal B_1$ turned by $\theta$ about $\mathbf i_1$ (⊙). The axis $\mathbf k_2$ leans to negative $\mathbf j_1$ (minus sign of $[{}_1R_2]$); the height of $G$ is $\ell\cos\theta$.

## rb24-phi
View: plane normal to the symmetry axis, k_2 out of the paper (⊙ at G, labelled k_2 = k_B); i_2 to the right, j_2 up.
Draw: thin grey axes i_2 (right) and j_2 (up), labelled. Body axes in blue: i_B at angle phi from i_2 and j_B at phi + 90 deg, thick arrows labelled i_B and j_B. Counterclockwise arcs phi from i_2 to i_B and from j_2 to j_B, labelled phi. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_B: hypotenuse 1, legs cos phi along i_2 and sin phi along j_2 (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for j_B: legs -sin phi along -i_2 (dashed) and cos phi along j_2. A thin circle of radius 0.8 around G suggesting the cross-section of the top, with the note "axisymmetric: equal moments A m ell^2 about every transverse axis".
Values to draw: phi = 40 deg; unit length 150 px.
Alt: Spin rotation by phi about the symmetry axis of the top.
Caption: Spin: $\mathcal B_B$ is $\mathcal B_2$ turned by $\varphi$ about $\mathbf k_2$ (⊙). Because the cross-section is axisymmetric, the inertia tensor has the same components in $\mathcal B_2$ and $\mathcal B_B$.

## rb24-steiner
View: the plane containing the symmetry axis, k_B up along the page, i_B to the right (j_B into the paper, ⊗).
Draw: outline of the pegtop (simple axisymmetric shape: cone tip at O at the bottom, body widening above) in thin black, symmetric about the vertical axis. Point O at the tip (dot), point G on the axis (dot) at distance ell above O, with the dimension ell between them. Axes through G in grey (x_B right, z_B up, labelled) and parallel axes through O in blue (x_B', z_B' labelled "parallel axes at O"). The vector r_O^G as an orange arrow from O to G, labelled r_O^G = ell k_B. Small note at the side: "Steiner: + m ell^2 on x_B and y_B, + 0 on z_B".
Values to draw: ell = 1 unit = 150 px; body max radius 0.8 units at 1.2 ell from O.
Alt: Pegtop with its tip O, centre of mass G on the axis and the parallel axes at O and G.
Caption: Steiner's theorem from $G$ to $O$: the offset $\ell\,\mathbf k_B$ lies on the symmetry axis, so only the two transverse moments grow, by $m\ell^2$.

## rb24-fbd
View: axonometric view of S_0 like the statement figure (k up, i towards the lower left, j to the right), origin O at the tip.
Draw: the pegtop with its axis k_2 tilted by theta = 30 deg from k, in the plane through k normal to i_1 (as in rb24-theta); G as a dot on the axis at distance ell. Forces as thick red arrows: weight mg straight down at G, labelled mg; reaction R at O drawn as a generic arrow pointing up and slightly inwards, labelled R (unknown direction), with its three components shown as thin dashed red arrows along i_2, j_2, k_2 and labelled R_1, R_2, R_3. The moment of the weight about O as a curved orange arrow about the line of nodes i_1 (drawn as a thin grey line through O), labelled M_O = mg ell sin theta i_2. Axis k_2 in blue, line of nodes i_1 = i_2 in grey.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, psi = 35 deg; ell = 1 unit = 150 px.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the top: weight at the centre of mass and reaction at the fixed tip.
Caption: Free-body diagram: weight $-mg\,\mathbf k$ at $G$ and the reaction $\mathbf R$ at the fixed vertex $O$ (no torque). About $O$ only the weight has a moment, $mg\ell\sin\theta\,\mathbf i_2$, along the line of nodes.
