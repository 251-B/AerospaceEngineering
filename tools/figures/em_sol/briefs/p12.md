## p12-potential
View: energy diagram, horizontal axis x, vertical axis V / (m omega0^2).
Draw: grey axes "x" and "V". Dashed grey parabola V = m omega0^2 x^2 / 2 (harmonic, b = 0). Blue curve V = m omega0^2 (x^2 - b x^4) / 2 for b = 1, from x = -0.95 to 0.95, with its maxima marked at x = ±1/sqrt(2b) (dots, label "x^2 = 1/(2b)"). Orange horizontal level E = V(a) for the amplitude a = 0.5, meeting the blue curve at ±a (dots, vertical dashed drops labelled "-a" and "+a"), and the allowed interval shaded light orange. A small vertical double arrow at a generic x between the blue curve and the level labelled "V(a) - V(x)".
Values to draw: omega0 = 1, m = 1, b = 1, a = 0.5 (E = 0.09375); vertical range 0 to 0.3.
Alt: The anharmonic potential compared with the harmonic parabola, with the energy level of an oscillation of amplitude a.
Caption: For $b > 0$ the potential lies below the harmonic parabola, so the particle moves more slowly and the period grows with the amplitude; oscillations need $2ba^2 < 1$.
