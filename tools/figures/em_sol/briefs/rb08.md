## rb08-rotation
View: plane of motion, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at the centre G); i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from G, labelled; ⊙ with label k at G. Body axes in blue from G: i_B at angle theta and j_B at theta + 90 deg, thick arrows labelled i_B and j_B. Counterclockwise arc theta from i to i_B and from j to j_B, both labelled theta. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_B: hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along i labelled cos theta, opposite leg parallel to j labelled sin theta (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for j_B: hypotenuse 1, leg along -i labelled -sin theta (dashed), leg parallel to j labelled cos theta.
Values to draw: theta = 35 deg; unit length about 140 px.
Alt: Rotation of the triangle body axes by the angle theta about the axis normal to the plane.
Caption: Body basis of the triangle, turned by $\theta$ about $\mathbf k$ (⊙). The axis $\mathbf j_B$ leans to negative $\mathbf i$, which gives the minus sign of $[{}_0R_B]$.

## rb08-body-frame
View: the triangle in its own frame x_1y_1 (theta = 0), k out of the paper.
Draw: equilateral triangle B(-a/2, 0), C(a/2, 0), A(0, h) with h = 0.866 a, thick black outline, vertices labelled. Origin M at the midpoint of BC, labelled M; grey axes x_1 (right, along BC) and y_1 (up, through A). Point G at (0, h/3) as a dot labelled G, with blue body axes i_B (right) and j_B (up) from G. Shaded right triangle (light green) M-C-A: right angle mark at M, hypotenuse AC labelled a, leg MC labelled a/2, leg MA labelled h = sqrt3 a/2. Dimension marks on the y_1 axis: MG labelled sqrt3 a/6 and GA labelled sqrt3 a/3 (= a/sqrt3).
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 220 px.
Alt: Equilateral triangle in its body frame with the right triangle used to compute the height and the position of the centre of mass.
Caption: The right triangle $MCA$ gives the height $h = \sqrt3 a/2$; $G$ is at one third of it above $BC$, so $GA = a/\sqrt3$.

## rb08-geometry
View: plane of motion, i right, j up, k out of the paper (⊙).
Draw: grey axes from O; dash-dot vertical guide at x = 2a labelled x = 2a. Point G on the guide at (2a, y_G) with y_G = -1.2a, as a dot. The triangle drawn at theta = 35 deg (A at G + (a/sqrt3)(-sin theta, cos theta), B and C accordingly), thin black outline. Segment GA as an orange arrow labelled r_G^A. Shaded right triangle (light orange) with hypotenuse GA (labelled a/sqrt3), vertical leg from G upwards (adjacent, labelled (a/sqrt3) cos theta), horizontal leg to the left (opposite, labelled (a/sqrt3) sin theta), dashed legs; arc theta at G between the vertical leg and GA. Position vector r_0^A from O to A as a thin grey dashed line.
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 120 px; theta = 35 deg; y_G = -1.2.
Alt: The triangle in the vertical plane with the right triangle that gives the components of the vector from G to A.
Caption: Position of $A$: the segment $GA$ of length $a/\sqrt3$ makes the angle $\theta$ with the vertical; its horizontal leg (opposite) points to negative $\mathbf i$ and its vertical leg (adjacent) to $\mathbf j$.

## rb08-strip
View: the triangle in the frame x_1y_1, as in rb08-body-frame.
Draw: the triangle outline; a thin horizontal strip at height y_1 (light blue fill) from -w to w with thickness dy_1; labels y_1 (height of the strip, dimension from the base), dy_1, and the half width w on the right half. Similar triangles: dashed line from A down the right side, with labels "w" at height y_1 and "a/2" at the base. Point G marked.
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 220 px; strip at y_1 = 0.35 a, thickness 0.04 a.
Alt: Horizontal strip of the triangle used to integrate the centre of mass and the moments of inertia.
Caption: Mass element of the triangle: a horizontal strip at height $y_1$ with half width $w = \tfrac a2(1 - y_1/h)$, obtained from similar triangles.

## rb08-fbd
View: plane of motion, i right, j up, k out of the paper.
Draw: the triangle isolated at theta = 45 deg with G at (2a, -2a) (same as the statement figure): thin black outline, vertices A, B, C labelled; dash-dot guide line through G (vertical). Forces as thick red arrows: weight -mg j at G (down) labelled mg; guide reaction R i at G (horizontal, to the right) labelled R; spring force at A pointing from A towards O, labelled k|r_0^A| (the spring itself drawn as a thin zigzag from O to A in grey, behind). A small grey arc showing the positive sense of theta. No inertia forces (inertial frame).
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 90 px; theta = 45 deg; G at (2, -2); O at the top left.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the triangle with the weight, the guide reaction and the spring force.
Caption: Free-body diagram: the weight and the guide reaction act at $G$ (no moment about $G$); the spring force $-k\,\mathbf r_0^A$ acts at $A$ and points to $O$.

## rb08-iar
View: plane of motion at t = 0, i right, j up.
Draw: Ox axis; the triangle at theta = 0 with BC on Ox, centred on x = 2a (B at (1.5a, 0), C at (2.5a, 0), A at (2a, 0.866a)); G at (2a, 0.289a). Velocity of A as an orange arrow (u_0, v_0) with u_0 = 0.5, v_0 = 0.6 (scale 0.6 a per unit), labelled v_0^A. Velocity of G vertical (0, v_0), orange, labelled v_0^G. The angular velocity sense: clockwise arc at G labelled theta-dot (negative for positive u_0). Point I on the horizontal line through G, to the right at GI = a v_0/(sqrt3 u_0) = 0.69 a, marked with a dot and labelled I, and a dashed blue segment GI labelled GI.
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 150 px; u_0 = 0.5, v_0 = 0.6.
Alt: Initial configuration of the triangle with the velocities of A and G and the instantaneous centre of rotation I.
Caption: Instantaneous axis at $t = 0$: normal to the plane through $I$, on the horizontal through $G$, at the distance $a v_0/(\sqrt3 u_0)$ to the right of $G$.
