# Briefs of problem 26 (particle, Topic 5): figures and simulator

The module `tools/figures/em_sol/p26.py` holds `p26-wire-plane` and `p26-top-view`. `p26-wire-plane` still fits the new solution and is kept unchanged. `p26-top-view` keeps its name but its labels changed (the fixed basis is now written i0, j0, k0), so it is briefed again in full below. One figure is new: `p26-fbd`. Colour code (as in the module): fixed axes grey; turning basis i1, j1, k1 blue; wire basis e_t, e_n orange; wire dark grey; actual forces (weight, reaction) pink solid; inertia forces pink dashed with the word "inertia" in their label; right triangles light translucent fill.

The wire seen edge-on as a thick dark grey segment along i1 through O, from −a to a, labelled "wire (edge-on)". A note at the upper right: "view from +Y; Ω > 0: counterclockwise".
Values to draw: Omega t = 35 degrees; unit length 165 px; the wire segment of half-length 0.8 units.
Alt: Top view along the vertical axis with the fixed axes i0, k0 and the turning axes i1, k1 rotated by Omega t.
Caption: View from $+Y$, with $\mathbf j_0 = \mathbf j_1$ out of the paper (⊙): $\mathbf k_0$ points down the page. $\mathbf i_1, \mathbf k_1$ are $\mathbf i_0, \mathbf k_0$ turned counterclockwise by $\Omega t$; the components of $\mathbf i_1$ are $\cos\Omega t$ along $\mathbf i_0$ and $\sin\Omega t$ along $-\mathbf k_0$ (up the page), hence the minus sign.
