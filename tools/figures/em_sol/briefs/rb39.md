## rb39-rot-theta
View: the vertical plane of the yo-yo, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O), i0 to the right, j0 up; origin O at the ceiling attachment.
Draw: a short hatched ceiling segment through O. Thin grey axes i0 (right) and j0 (up) at O and a dashed downward vertical from O. The straight string from O downwards to the right at the angle theta from the downward vertical (solid black line, length about 3 units). Arc of theta (counterclockwise, arrowhead) at O from the downward vertical to the string, labelled theta. Blue unit vectors at O: i1 (i0 turned counterclockwise by theta, to the right and slightly up) and j1 (j0 turned by theta, up and slightly to the left); arcs theta from i0 to i1 and from j0 to j1. Label next to the string: "-j1 along the string". Light translucent orange triangle for i1: hypotenuse 1 along i1, adjacent leg along i0 labelled cos theta, dashed opposite leg along j0 labelled sin theta. Light translucent green triangle for j1: hypotenuse 1 along j1, adjacent leg along j0 labelled cos theta, dashed opposite leg along minus i0 labelled sin theta.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, unit vectors 1.2 units long.
Alt: The basis of the string, turned counterclockwise by theta from the fixed basis.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\theta$; the string runs along $-\mathbf j_1$ from $O$ and $\mathbf i_1$ is normal to it.

## rb39-position
View: the vertical plane, k0 out of the paper, i0 to the right, j0 up, as in rb39-rot-theta.
Draw: O at the top (dot, hatched ceiling). The string from O to the tangent point T (dot, label T) along minus j1, labelled xi. The inner cylinder of radius a (thick circle) centred at C (dot, label C), with T on its right-hand side, and the outer disk of radius R (thin circle) centred at C. The radius from C to T (along i1) labelled a. Light translucent orange right triangle O-T-C with the right angle at T (small square mark at T): leg OT labelled xi, leg TC labelled a, hypotenuse OC dashed. Blue unit vectors i1 and j1 drawn at T. Dashed downward vertical from O with the arc theta to the string. Small curved arrow at C labelled phi-dot (counterclockwise) with a note "string unwinds without slip at T".
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, xi = 3, a = 0.35, R = 1.
Alt: The position of the centre C as the string segment OT plus the radius TC normal to the string.
Caption: $\mathbf r_0^C = \overrightarrow{OT} + \overrightarrow{TC} = -\xi\,\mathbf j_1 - a\,\mathbf i_1$; the triangle $OTC$ is right-angled at $T$ because the string is tangent to the inner cylinder.

## rb39-fbd
View: the vertical plane, k0 out of the paper, as in rb39-position.
Draw: the yo-yo alone (outer circle R, inner circle a, centre C, tangent point T on the right-hand side of the inner circle) with the cut string stub at T. At C: weight mg downwards (red), label "mg". At T: tension F along the string towards O (red, along j1), label "F". Dashed arm from C to T labelled "a i1". Blue i1 and j1 at C. Curved arrow about C, counterclockwise, labelled "M_C = aF". Faint dashed line along the string towards O (O not drawn, arrow "to O").
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, a = 0.35, R = 1.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the yo-yo: its weight at C and the string tension at the tangent point T.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The tension $F\,\mathbf j_1$ acts at $T$, at the arm $a\,\mathbf i_1$ from $C$, and gives the counterclockwise torque $aF\,\mathbf k_0$.

## rb39-graph-accel
View: a plot (not a mechanical view). Horizontal axis R/a from 1 to 8; vertical axis acceleration over g from 0 to 0.8.
Draw: the curve 2/(2 + x^2) for x from 1 to 8 (blue, thick). Mark the point (1, 2/3) with a dot and the label "2g/3"; mark (2, 1/3) and (4, 1/9) with small dots. Dashed grey curve 2/x^2 for x from 2.5 to 8, label "2g a^2/R^2 (large R/a)". Axis labels "R/a" and "xi-ddot_0 / g".
Values to draw: as stated.
Alt: Graph of the initial acceleration of the yo-yo, decreasing from two thirds of g at R equal to a towards zero.
Caption: $\ddot\xi_0/g = 2/(2 + (R/a)^2)$, with $\theta$ fixed at zero: the larger the disks compared with the axle, the slower the fall.

## rb39-graph-omega
View: a plot (not a mechanical view). Horizontal axis R (in units of a reference length R_ref) from 0.2 to 4; vertical axis Omega_0,min over sqrt(g/R_ref) from 0 to 7.
Draw: the curve sqrt(8/x) for x from 0.2 to 4 (blue, thick), label "Omega_0,min ~ sqrt(8g/R), a << R". Mark x = 1 with the value sqrt(8) = 2.83 (dot and label). Axis labels "R" and "Omega_0,min".
Values to draw: as stated.
Alt: Graph of the minimum initial spin, decreasing like one over the square root of R.
Caption: Minimum initial spin to climb from $\xi_0 = 2R$ to the origin, for $a \ll R$: $\Omega_{0,\min} \approx \sqrt{8g/R}$.
