## rb14-rotation
View: plane of the arc, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at C), i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i and j from C. Blue i_B (along the diameter) and j_B (along the axis of symmetry, from the arc towards C) turned counterclockwise by theta. Arcs of theta (counterclockwise, with arrowheads) between i and i_B and between j and j_B, labelled theta. Light translucent triangles: i_B with adjacent leg cos theta along i and dashed opposite leg sin theta; j_B with adjacent leg cos theta along j and dashed opposite leg sin theta towards negative i.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg.
Alt: The body basis of the arc turned counterclockwise by theta from the fixed basis.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k$ (⊙) by $\theta$. A positive angle rolls the arc to the left, as in the figure of the statement.

## rb14-geometry
View: plane of the arc, k out of the paper, fixed origin Q on the plane, i right, j up.
Draw: hatched horizontal plane with Q marked. Rest position dashed grey: semicircle of radius R_0 with centre C_0 at (0, R_0), convex side down, its centre of mass G_0 at (0, R_0 - R_G). Displaced position solid black: semicircle turned counterclockwise by theta, centre C at (-R_0 theta, R_0), contact point P straight below C on the plane (dot, label P). Thick green segment on the plane from Q to P labelled X_C = R_0 theta and the matching rolled arc of the semicircle drawn thick green from P to the body point that touched Q (label "arc R_0 theta"). Dashed radius CP labelled R_0. Point G (dot) on the axis of symmetry at R_G from C. Light translucent orange right triangle C, foot of G on the vertical through C, G: vertical leg labelled R_G cos theta (down), horizontal leg labelled R_G sin theta (to the right), hypotenuse R_G, angle theta at C between the downward vertical and CG. Dimensions from G_0 to G: horizontal x_CM = R_0 theta - R_G sin theta (to the left) and vertical y_CM = R_G(1 - cos theta) (up), dashed.
Values to draw: R_0 = 3, R_G = 1.91, theta = 30 deg.
Alt: The semicircular arc in its rest position and after rolling by theta, with the contact point, the centre of mass and their displacements.
Caption: Main plane. The contact point stays below $C$ and travels $R_0\theta$; $G$ rises by $R_G(1 - \cos\theta)$ and moves by $R_0\theta - R_G\sin\theta$ in the sense of rolling.

## rb14-arc-element
View: plane of the arc in body axes, k out of the paper, origin C, i_B to the right, j_B up.
Draw: the semicircle of radius R_0 below the diameter (from angle 0 to pi measured clockwise from i_B, i.e. points R_0(cos alpha, -sin alpha)). A short thick element ds = R_0 d alpha at angle alpha, labelled dm = (M/pi) d alpha, with the dashed radius from C labelled R_0 and the arc of alpha from i_B. G on the negative j_B axis at R_G = 2R_0/pi (dot, label G), with the dimension R_G.
Values to draw: R_0 = 3, alpha = 55 deg.
Alt: Mass element of the semicircular wire at angle alpha and the centre of mass on its axis of symmetry.
Caption: Arc element $dm = \lambda R_0\,d\alpha$ at the distance $R_0$ from $C$: it gives $I_C = MR_0^2$ and the centre of mass at $R_G = 2R_0/\pi$ below $C$.

## rb14-fbd
View: plane of the arc, same displaced position as rb14-geometry.
Draw: the arc outline (plane faint grey). At G: weight -Mg j (red, down), label Mg. At P: normal reaction N j (green, up), label N; friction F i (green, horizontal), label F. Dashed line from P to G. Curved arrow theta-ddot about C (sense: clockwise, back towards the rest position).
Values to draw: as in rb14-geometry.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the arc: weight at G, normal reaction and friction at the contact point.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The contact force acts at a body point at rest, so it does no work and the mechanical energy is conserved.
