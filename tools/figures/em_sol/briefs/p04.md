# Figure briefs, Problem 04 (Plane at Constant Altitude)

Colour code for the whole problem: fixed axes grey; polar basis blue; position vector black; velocity green; acceleration orange.

## p04-main-plane
kind: plane / meridian section
view: k0 points out of the paper (⊙, small circle with a dot at O); i0 to the right (direction of flight), j0 up.
draw: drawing configuration y0 = 3 units, x = 4 units (so r = 5, theta = 36.87 deg). Ground line: thin grey horizontal line through O. Fixed axes from O in grey: i0 (right), j0 (up), with the symbol ⊙ k0 at O. Dashed horizontal flight line at height y0 across the whole drawing, with a small aircraft symbol (or a filled dot labelled P) at (4, 3). Point H = (4, 0) on the ground. Shaded right triangle O-H-P (light translucent fill), right-angle mark at H. Position vector r (thick black arrow) from O to P along the hypotenuse. Angle arc theta at O from the i0 axis (segment OH) counterclockwise to OP. At P: blue unit vectors e_r (along OP, outward) and e_theta (perpendicular, 90 deg counterclockwise from e_r, i.e. pointing up and to the left). At P also the green velocity arrow v along +i0 (horizontal, to the right) and the orange acceleration arrow a_x along +i0 (drawn slightly below the velocity, shorter). A second faint position of the aircraft at x = 0 (point P0 directly above O, labelled "t = 0") on the flight line.
labels: "O", "H", "P", "P0 (t = 0)", "i0", "j0", "k0" next to ⊙, "r" on the hypotenuse (above it, not on the line), "x = r cos(theta)" under the leg OH (adjacent leg), "y0 = r sin(theta)" to the right of the leg HP (opposite leg), "theta" by the arc, "e_r", "e_theta", "v i0" on the green arrow, "a_x i0" on the orange arrow.
alt: Aircraft P flying at constant altitude y0 above the observer O, with the right triangle O H P that gives its polar coordinates.
caption: Main plane of the motion. The right triangle O H P has hypotenuse r, adjacent leg x = r cos(theta) and opposite leg y0 = r sin(theta); the polar basis e_r, e_theta is drawn at P.

## p04-rotation-view
kind: rotation view
view: along k0, which points out of the paper (⊙ at the common origin); i0 to the right, j0 up.
draw: drawing configuration theta = 37 deg. Common origin O with the symbol ⊙ k0. Fixed axes i0 (right) and j0 (up) in grey, equal length. Rotated axes in blue: e_r at 37 deg counterclockwise from i0, e_theta at 127 deg (37 deg counterclockwise from j0), same length. Angle arc theta from i0 to e_r (counterclockwise, arrowhead at e_r) and a second arc theta from j0 to e_theta (counterclockwise, arrowhead at e_theta). Dashed projections of the tip of e_r on i0 (length cos theta) and on j0 (length sin theta), with the small right triangle shaded. Dashed projections of the tip of e_theta on the negative i0 axis (length sin theta, on the left of O) and on j0 (length cos theta). A curved arrow around ⊙ showing the positive (counterclockwise) sense of rotation.
labels: "i0", "j0", "k0 (⊙)", "e_r", "e_theta", "theta" (twice, by each arc), "cos(theta)" on the projection of e_r on i0, "sin(theta)" on the projection of e_r on j0, "-sin(theta)" on the projection of e_theta on the i0 axis (left side), "cos(theta)" on the projection of e_theta on j0.
alt: View along k0 of the fixed axes i0, j0 and the polar axes e_r, e_theta turned by theta.
caption: Rotation of the polar basis by theta about k0 (out of the paper). The component of e_theta along i0 is negative because e_theta leans toward minus i0.

## p04-acceleration-projection
kind: fold-down triangle
view: k0 out of the paper (⊙); i0 to the right, j0 up.
draw: drawing configuration theta = 37 deg, a_x = 4 units. Point P with the blue polar axes e_r (at 37 deg above the horizontal) and e_theta (at 127 deg) drawn as long thin blue lines through P, and short grey i0, j0 directions. Orange arrow a_x i0 from P, horizontal to the right (the hypotenuse of the shaded right triangle). Its dashed projection on the e_r line: orange component a_r along +e_r (length a_x cos theta = 3.2) and on the e_theta line: orange component a_theta along -e_theta (length a_x sin theta = 2.4, pointing down and to the right). Shaded right triangle with vertices P, the tip of a_r and the tip of a_x i0; right-angle mark at the tip of a_r. Angle arc theta at P between i0 (the hypotenuse) and e_r.
labels: "P", "e_r", "e_theta", "a_x i0" on the hypotenuse, "a_r = a_x x / r = a_x cos(theta)" next to the adjacent leg, "a_theta = -a_x y0 / r = -a_x sin(theta)" next to the opposite leg, "theta".
alt: The horizontal acceleration a_x i0 split into a radial component and a negative azimuthal component.
caption: The acceleration of the aircraft is the hypotenuse; its radial component is the adjacent leg a_x cos(theta) and its azimuthal component is the opposite leg, along minus e_theta.
