## rb19-rot-psi
View: from above, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at G), i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i and j. Blue i_1 and j_1 (= y_2) turned counterclockwise by psi. Arcs of psi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i to i_1 and from j to j_1, labelled psi. Light translucent triangles: for i_1, adjacent leg along i labelled cos psi and dashed opposite leg along j labelled sin psi; for j_1, adjacent leg along j labelled cos psi and dashed opposite leg towards negative i labelled sin psi. Label j_1 also "= y_2 (horizontal)".
Values to draw: psi = 30 deg.
Alt: First Euler rotation, by psi about the vertical, which places the horizontal axis y_2.
Caption: First rotation, by $\psi$ about $\mathbf k$ (⊙). $\psi$ is the angle between $Oy$ and $Gy_2$.

## rb19-rot-theta
View: vertical plane of i_1 and k through G; j_1 = j_2 points into the paper (⊗ at G), i_1 to the right, k up.
Draw: the floor as a horizontal line at distance a below G, contact point P straight below G. Circle of radius a centred at G. Thin grey axes i_1 and k from G. Blue i_2 and k_2 turned clockwise (as seen) by theta: k_2 to the right of k, i_2 below i_1. Point A where k_2 meets the circle (dot, label A). Arcs of theta from k to k_2 and from i_1 to i_2. Light translucent triangles: for k_2 (hypotenuse a, from G to A), vertical leg a cos theta along k and dashed horizontal leg a sin theta along i_1; for i_2, adjacent leg cos theta along i_1 and dashed opposite leg sin theta downwards.
Values to draw: theta = 40 deg, a = 2.
Alt: Second Euler rotation, by theta about the horizontal axis y_2, which tilts GA away from the vertical.
Caption: Second rotation, by $\theta$ about $\mathbf j_1 = \mathbf j_2$ (⊗). $A$ is at the height $a(1 + \cos\theta)$ and at the horizontal distance $a\sin\theta$ from $G$ along $\mathbf i_1$.

## rb19-rot-phi
View: plane normal to k_2, k_2 out of the paper (⊙ at G), i_2 to the right, j_2 up.
Draw: thin grey axes i_2 and j_2. Orange body vectors i_b and j_b turned counterclockwise by phi. Arcs of phi from i_2 to i_b and from j_2 to j_b. Light translucent triangles with legs cos phi and sin phi as in rb19-rot-psi. Point A drawn as a dot at G (it lies on the axis k_2, out of the paper).
Values to draw: phi = 35 deg.
Alt: Third Euler rotation, the spin phi of the sphere about GA.
Caption: Third rotation, by $\varphi$ about $\mathbf k_2$ (⊙): the difference between $Gx_2y_2z_2$ and a body frame. It does not move $A$.

## rb19-spring
View: oblique 3D view as in the statement figure: O at the origin, x towards the viewer (down-left), y to the right, z up.
Draw: grey axes x, y, z from O. C on the z axis at height 2a (dot, label C, dimension 2a). Sphere of radius a with centre G above the point (xi, eta, 0) of the floor; contact point P on the floor below G (dot, label P); dashed lines from P to the x and y axes labelled xi and eta. Point A on the sphere along k_2 from G (dot, label A); blue k_2 from G towards A, blue i_2 and j_2 (j_2 horizontal). The spring as a zig-zag from A to C; an orange arrow at A along AC labelled "F_s = k AC". Dashed vertical drop from A with the height a(1 + cos theta) labelled, and the vertical component of AC, a(1 - cos theta), labelled near C.
Values to draw: a = 1, xi = 1.2, eta = 2.2, psi = 30 deg, theta = 50 deg.
Alt: The sphere on the floor with the spring from A to C and the coordinates xi and eta of G.
Caption: Spring geometry: $\overrightarrow{AC} = -(\xi + a\sin\theta\cos\psi)\,\mathbf i - (\eta + a\sin\theta\sin\psi)\,\mathbf j + a(1 - \cos\theta)\,\mathbf k$. $A$ is never above $C$, so the spring never pushes the sphere down.

## rb19-fbd
View: vertical plane of i_1 and k through G, j_2 into the paper (⊗), same as rb19-rot-theta.
Draw: floor line (faint, hatched), sphere outline, G, P below G, A on the circle at angle theta from the vertical. At G: red weight -Mg k, label Mg. At P: green normal reaction N k, label N; dashed green arrow at P labelled "F_1 i_1 (rough plane only)" and a small text "F_2 along j_1, normal to the paper". At A: orange spring force pointing up and to the left towards the direction of C (drawn generic), label F_s. Dashed arm GA labelled a k_2.
Values to draw: theta = 40 deg, a = 2.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the sphere: weight at G, spring at A, normal reaction and, on the rough plane, friction at P.
Caption: Free-body diagram. On the smooth plane only the spring has a moment about $G$ (arm $a\,\mathbf k_2$); on the rough plane the friction adds the moment of the arm $-a\,\mathbf k$.
