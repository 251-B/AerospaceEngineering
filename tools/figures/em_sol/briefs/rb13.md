## rb13-rotation
View: plane of the sheet, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O), i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i and j from O. Blue i_B (from O towards the centre of the hole) and j_B turned counterclockwise by theta. Arc of theta (counterclockwise, with arrowhead) from i to i_B and from j to j_B, both labelled theta. Light translucent triangles: for i_B, adjacent leg along i labelled cos theta and dashed opposite leg labelled sin theta; for j_B, adjacent leg along j labelled cos theta and dashed opposite leg towards negative i labelled sin theta. Near O a small clockwise curved arrow labelled "initial spin: -omega k (⊗)".
Values to draw: theta = 35 deg.
Alt: The body basis of the sheet, with i_B pointing to the centre of the hole, turned counterclockwise by theta.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k$ (⊙) by $\theta$. The initial spin of the statement is clockwise, so the initial rate of $\theta$ is $-\omega$.

## rb13-geometry
View: plane of the sheet, k out of the paper, fixed origin Q on the line, i right, j up.
Draw: hatched horizontal line (the rough line) with Q marked. Disc of radius R, centre O at height R above the line, shaded grey; white circular hole of radius R/2 whose centre O_h is at distance R/2 from O along i_B (the hole touches the rim from inside). Contact point C straight below O on the line (dot, label C); dashed vertical segment OC labelled R. Centre of mass G (dot, label G) on the line O_h O extended beyond O, at distance R/6 from O. Light translucent orange right triangle with hypotenuse OG (label R/6), horizontal leg labelled (R/6) cos theta pointing left from O and vertical leg labelled (R/6) sin theta pointing down. Blue i_B along O to O_h. Arrow for the velocity of O labelled x-dot along i. Dimension x from Q to the foot of O.
Values to draw: R = 3, theta = 30 deg (hole up and to the right of O), x = 2.
Alt: The sheet with its hole, the contact point below O and the centre of mass G at R/6 from O, opposite to the hole.
Caption: Main plane. $C$ is always below $O$; $G$ lies on the line of $\mathbf i_B$ at $R/6$ from $O$, on the side opposite to the hole, with the legs $\tfrac R6\cos\theta$ and $\tfrac R6\sin\theta$.

## rb13-composite
View: plane of the sheet, k out of the paper, origin O at the centre of the disc, i_B to the right.
Draw: left: full disc of radius R (light grey) labelled "D: mass 4m/3"; minus sign; middle: disc of radius R/2 centred at O_h (R/2 to the right of O) labelled "H: mass m/3"; equals sign; right: the sheet with the hole. On the full disc draw a thin annular element of radius r and width dr (dashed edges), labelled "dm = sigma r dr d alpha". On the sheet mark O, O_h and G (G at R/6 to the left of O) with dimensions R/2 and R/6.
Values to draw: R = 2.
Alt: The sheet as a full disc minus a hole, with the polar mass element and the positions of O, O_h and G.
Caption: Composite body: full disc of mass $\tfrac43 m$ minus the hole of mass $\tfrac13 m$. Both moments of inertia come from the polar element $dm = \sigma\,r\,dr\,d\alpha$; the hole is moved from $O_h$ to $O$ by the parallel axis theorem.

## rb13-fbd
View: plane of the sheet, same position as rb13-geometry.
Draw: the sheet outline (with hole), the line faint grey. At G: weight -mg j (red, downwards), label mg. At C: normal reaction N j (green, upwards), label N; friction F i (green, horizontal), label "F (zero if mu = 0)". Dashed arm from G to C with the horizontal component (R/6) cos theta and the vertical component R - (R/6) sin theta labelled. Curved arrow theta-ddot counterclockwise about G.
Values to draw: as in rb13-geometry.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the sheet: weight at G, normal reaction and friction at the contact point.
Caption: Free-body diagram. About $G$, the normal reaction has the arm $\tfrac R6\cos\theta$ and the friction force the arm $R - \tfrac R6\sin\theta$.
