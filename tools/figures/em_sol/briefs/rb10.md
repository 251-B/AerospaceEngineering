## rb10-parallelogram
View: vertical plane of motion, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn near O_A); i to the right, j up; origin at the left ceiling pin O_A.
Draw: the ceiling as a thin hatched horizontal line with the two pins O_A at (0, 0) and O_B at (b, 0), labelled. The two links as thick grey bars from O_A to A and from O_B to B, both at angle theta from the downward vertical (lower ends to the right). The top edge AB of the door as a thick black segment, horizontal, labelled A and B; the lower part of the door outline in thin black (rectangle of height h, slightly wider than AB). The closed loop O_A -> A -> B -> O_B -> O_A drawn as four blue arrows along the four sides, labelled L e_L (O_A to A), b i_B (A to B), -L e_L (B to O_B) and -b i (O_B to O_A). Small parallel tick marks on the two links (equal length L) and on O_AO_B and AB (equal length b). Dashed vertical through O_A and the arc theta from the downward vertical to the left link, counterclockwise sense (towards +i), labelled theta.
Values to draw: b = 2.2 units, L = 1.6 units, h = 1.8 units, door width 2.6 units centred on AB, theta = 30 deg; 1 unit = 90 px.
Alt: Parallelogram formed by the ceiling, the two equal links and the top edge of the door.
Caption: The ceiling pins, the two links of length $L$ and the top edge $AB$ form a parallelogram, so $AB$ stays parallel to the ceiling: the door translates and $\boldsymbol\omega_{B0} = \mathbf 0$.

## rb10-kinematics
View: vertical plane, k out of the paper (⊙ at O_A), i right, j up; origin O_A.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from O_A. The left link from O_A to A as a thick grey bar; dashed vertical line down from O_A. Shaded right triangle (light blue) with vertices O_A, the foot point (0, -L cos theta) and A: hypotenuse O_A A labelled L, vertical leg (adjacent to theta) labelled L cos theta, horizontal leg (opposite to theta) labelled L sin theta (dashed). Angle theta marked at O_A between the downward vertical and the link (counterclockwise towards +i). At A, the unit vectors e_n (thick orange arrow, along the link pointing back up towards O_A) and e_t (thick orange arrow, perpendicular to the link, pointing up-right, in the sense of increasing theta), labelled. A small copy of the fixed axes i, j at A in grey, with the arc theta from i to e_t and from j to e_n (both counterclockwise). The point G (dot) at A + (b/2) i - (h/2) j, with the dashed segments b/2 (horizontal) and h/2 (vertical) from A to G labelled. The circle of radius L described by G, dashed grey arc, centred at (b/2, -h/2). Velocity of G as a green arrow along e_t labelled v_0^G = L theta-dot e_t.
Values to draw: L = 1.6, b = 2.2, h = 1.8, theta = 30 deg; 1 unit = 95 px.
Alt: Right triangle of the left link with the link basis e_t, e_n and the circular path of the centre of mass.
Caption: Link of length $L$ as the hypotenuse: legs $L\sin\theta$ (horizontal) and $L\cos\theta$ (vertical). $\mathbf e_t$ and $\mathbf e_n$ are $\mathbf i$ and $\mathbf j$ turned by $\theta$ about $\mathbf k$ (⊙); $G$ moves on a circle of radius $L$.

## rb10-plate
View: the door alone, front view, i_B right, j_B up.
Draw: rectangle of width w and height h, thin black outline; provisional axes x' (right) and y' (up) through its centre G (dot, labelled G). The pins A and B on the top edge at x' = -b/2 and x' = +b/2, labelled. Arms r_G^A and r_G^B as blue arrows from G to A and from G to B, labelled. A small mass element dm as a light blue square at (0.5 w/2, 0.4 h/2) with dashed lines to the axes marking x' and y'. Dimension marks: b between A and B (above the top edge), h along the left side, w along the bottom.
Values to draw: w = 2.6, h = 1.8, b = 2.2; 1 unit = 100 px.
Alt: Door plate with its centre of mass and the arms to the two pins.
Caption: Centre of mass of the door at the centre of the rectangle; the arms to the pins are $\mathbf r_G^A = -\tfrac b2\,\mathbf i + \tfrac h2\,\mathbf j$ and $\mathbf r_G^B = \tfrac b2\,\mathbf i + \tfrac h2\,\mathbf j$.

## rb10-fbd
View: vertical plane, i right, j up, k out of the paper.
Draw: the door alone (rectangle of width 2.6, height 1.8) with A and B on the top edge (spacing b = 2.2, symmetric), G at the centre. The links are removed and replaced by forces, drawn as thick red arrows: T_A e_n at A and T_B e_n at B, both pointing up-left along the direction of the links (angle theta = 30 deg from the vertical, towards -i), labelled T_A and T_B; weight mg straight down at G, labelled mg. Thin dashed lines showing the (removed) links' directions continuing beyond A and B. Small orange basis e_t, e_n drawn at a corner for reference. Dashed arms from G to A and from G to B. No inertia forces (inertial frame).
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg; 1 unit = 100 px; arrow lengths: mg 1.2 units, T_A 0.85 units, T_B 0.35 units.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the door with its weight and the two link forces along the links.
Caption: Free-body diagram: weight $-mg\,\mathbf j$ at $G$ and the link forces $T_A\,\mathbf e_n$ at $A$ and $T_B\,\mathbf e_n$ at $B$, along the links (two-force members); for $\theta \gt 0$ the left link pulls harder.
