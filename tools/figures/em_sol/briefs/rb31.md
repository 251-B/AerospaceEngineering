## rb31-meridian
View: meridian plane Ox1z1 of S1, i1 to the right, k1 up, j1 into the paper (draw ⊗ at O with label j1). Origin O on the symmetry axis at the height of the ball centre A.
Draw: the symmetry axis Oz1 as a thin dash-dot grey vertical line through O. The bottom of the frame as a hatched horizontal line at z = -R from x = 1 R to x = 3 R, and the wall as a hatched vertical line at x = 3R from z = -R up to z = 1.6 R (frame d, light grey). The cone c as a light grey filled region on the right of the axis: generatrix from the apex V on the axis (below the bottom) up to the base edge at x = 2R; the base as a horizontal line at the top. The ball b as a circle of radius R centred at A = (2R, 0), lightly filled. Points: O, A, Q = (2R, -R) on the bottom, P = (3R, 0) on the wall, I = (R(2 - cos beta), R sin beta) on the generatrix, G on the axis (label G). Thin grey axis Ox1 through O, A and P. In blue: the unit tangent e_t of the generatrix at I (pointing up along it) and the unit normal n at I (pointing from the cone towards A, i.e. right and down). Shaded right triangle for e_t: hypotenuse "1" along e_t, adjacent leg "cos beta" along k1 (vertical), opposite leg "sin beta" along i1 (horizontal). Shaded right triangle for the arm AI: hypotenuse "R" from A to I, horizontal leg "R cos beta" (dashed), vertical leg "R sin beta" (dashed). Arc of beta at the apex V between the axis and the generatrix, and arc of beta at A between the horizontal through A (towards the axis) and AI. In red: the instantaneous axis of rotation, a dashed line through Q and P extended down-left to meet the axis at the point (0, -3R) (label "IAR, z = -3R"). In orange: the arrow of omega_b0 along minus (i1 + k1) from A (label "omega_b0 = -2 theta-dot (i1 + k1)"), drawn for a positive theta-dot.
Values to draw: R = 2, beta = 30 deg (I at (2.27, 1.0) when R = 2; apex V at (0, -(2 cos beta - 1) R / sin beta) = (0, -2.93)).
Alt: Meridian section with the ball touching the bottom at Q, the wall at P and the cone at I, the normal and tangent of the cone at I and the instantaneous axis PQ.
Caption: Meridian plane ($\mathbf j_1$ into the paper, ⊗): $\mathbf n = \cos\beta\,\mathbf i_1 - \sin\beta\,\mathbf k_1$, $\mathbf e_t = \sin\beta\,\mathbf i_1 + \cos\beta\,\mathbf k_1$, $\overrightarrow{AI} = -R\mathbf n$; with no slip the instantaneous axis is the line $PQ$, which meets the axis at the depth $3R$.

## rb31-rot-theta
View: from above, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O), i0 to the right, j0 up. Origin O on the symmetry axis.
Draw: thin grey axes i0, j0 from O. Circle of radius 2R (dashed grey) as the path of A, circle of radius 3R (solid grey) as the wall of the frame, small filled circle for the top view of the cone base (radius 2R, light grey, under the ball path). Point A on the dashed circle at polar angle theta (dot, label A) and the ball outline of radius R around it. Blue basis at O: i1 along OA, j1 perpendicular (counterclockwise). Arc of theta from i0 to i1 at O, counterclockwise, labelled theta. Shaded right triangle for i1: adjacent leg "cos theta" along i0, dashed opposite leg "sin theta" along j0. Shaded right triangle for j1: adjacent leg "cos theta" along j0, dashed opposite leg "-sin theta" along -i0. Orange arrow at A along j1 labelled "v_A = 2R theta-dot j1". Green curved arrow around O labelled "phi-dot k0 (cone)" in the counterclockwise sense, and a small green tick on the cone base marking the angle phi from i0 (arc labelled phi, larger than theta).
Values to draw: R = 1.5, theta = 35 deg, phi = 70 deg.
Alt: Top view of the bearing with the frame S1 turned by theta about k0, following the centre A of the ball, and the cone turned by phi.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙): $\mathbf i_1 = \cos\theta\,\mathbf i_0 + \sin\theta\,\mathbf j_0$, $\mathbf j_1 = -\sin\theta\,\mathbf i_0 + \cos\theta\,\mathbf j_0$; the cone turns by $\phi$ about the same axis.

## rb31-exploded
View: meridian plane Ox1z1, i1 to the right, k1 up, j1 into the paper (⊗); exploded: the cone pulled to the left by 1.2 R, the frame pulled to the right and down by 0.8 R, the ball in the middle.
Draw: Ball alone (circle radius R, centre A): red weight mg down at A; at Q (bottom point) green normal N_Q up along k1 and green friction F_Qx along i1 (label "F_Qx i1 + F_Qy j1, friction"); at P (right point) green normal N_P pointing left (-i1) and friction F_Pz along k1 (label "F_Py j1 + F_Pz k1"); at I (upper left point) the normal N_I along n (towards A) and friction F_It along e_t (label "F_Ij j1 + F_It e_t"). Out-of-plane components drawn as small ⊗ symbols with their labels. Cone alone: at its point I the opposite force -F_I (orange, same line, opposite sense), red weight 2mg down at G, the joint at G drawn as a short sleeve on the axis with purple X i1 + Y j1 (Y as ⊗) and the moment M_x i1 + M_y j1 as a double-headed arrow normal to the axis (label "no axial force, no axial moment"), and the torque Gamma k0 as a curved arrow about the axis labelled "Gamma k0 (part g)". Frame alone: -F_Q at Q and -F_P at P (orange). Dashed grey lines joining the two copies of I, of Q and of P (action-reaction pairs).
Values to draw: R = 2, beta = 30 deg.
Alt: Exploded view of the cone, the ball and the frame with the contact forces at I, P and Q as action-reaction pairs, the weights and the cylindrical joint of the cone.
Caption: Exploded view: the cone acts on the ball with $\mathbf F_I = N_I\mathbf n + F_{Ij}\mathbf j_1 + F_{It}\mathbf e_t$ and the ball on the cone with $-\mathbf F_I$; the frame acts with $\mathbf F_Q$ and $\mathbf F_P$; the joint holds the cone normal to its axis only.
