## rb42-gravity
View: vertical section through the line of greatest slope; i0 drawn to the right and down (along the plane), k0 normal to the plane (up and slightly to the right), j0 horizontal and into the paper (⊗ drawn at O, label j0). Grey horizontal e_h to the right and vertical e_v up at O.
Draw: the plane as a thick grey line through O descending to the right at gamma below the dashed horizontal; the sphere (circle radius R) touching it at C, centre G at R from C along k0 (dashed segment CG labelled R). Blue i0 and k0 at O. Arc of gamma between e_h and i0 (clockwise, label gamma) and between e_v and k0 (clockwise, label gamma). Red gravity g pointing straight down from G; light translucent right triangle with g as hypotenuse, the leg along -k0 labelled "g cos gamma" (adjacent to gamma) and the dashed leg along +i0 labelled "g sin gamma" (opposite, pointing down the slope); arc of gamma between g and the -k0 leg. Label "xi grows down the slope" with a small arrow along i0 near C.
Values to draw: gamma = 20 deg, R = 1.5.
Alt: Section along the line of greatest slope showing i0 down the slope, k0 normal to the plane, j0 into the paper, and gravity split into g sin gamma along i0 and g cos gamma into the plane.
Caption: Read from the statement figure: $\mathbf i_0$ points down the slope and $\mathbf j_0$ is horizontal, so $\mathbf g = g\sin\gamma\,\mathbf i_0 - g\cos\gamma\,\mathbf k_0$.

## rb42-rot-psi

Draw: thin grey axes i0, j0. Blue i1 and j1 turned counterclockwise by psi. Arc of psi from i0 to i1 and from j0 to j1, both counterclockwise, labelled psi. Light translucent triangle for i1: adjacent leg "cos psi" along i0, dashed opposite leg "sin psi" along j0. Light translucent triangle for j1: adjacent leg "cos psi" along j0, dashed opposite leg "-sin psi" along -i0. Label i1 "line of nodes".
Values to draw: psi = 35 deg.
Alt: Precession: the basis B1 turned by psi about the normal k0 of the plane.
Caption: Precession about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙): $\mathbf i_1 = \cos\psi\,\mathbf i_0 + \sin\psi\,\mathbf j_0$, $\mathbf j_1 = -\sin\psi\,\mathbf i_0 + \cos\psi\,\mathbf j_0$.

Values to draw: beta = 50 deg, R = 3 (disk radius 1.73).

Caption: Turn of the disk about the axle (⊙): $\mathbf i_d = \cos\beta\,\mathbf i_s + \sin\beta\,\mathbf j_s$, $\mathbf j_d = -\sin\beta\,\mathbf i_s + \cos\beta\,\mathbf j_s$; the disk radius $R/\sqrt3$ is the one implied by its tensor.

## rb42-exploded
View: oblique view as in the statement figure (plane drawn as a parallelogram, k0 normal to it). Exploded: the disk lifted out of the sphere along the axle direction by 1.5 R, the sphere in place, the plane pulled down along -k0 by 0.6 R.
Draw: Disk alone (grey ellipse, centre G'): green force R_d at its centre with three components X_d i_s, Y_d j_s, Z_d k_s, green torque M_d as a double arrow normal to the axle (label "M_d = M_1 i_s + M_2 j_s, no component along k_s"), red weight 2m g down. Sphere with its axle AB (circle with the segment AB along k_s): orange -R_d at G and orange torque -M_d (double arrow, opposite sense), red weight m g down at G, and at the contact point C the green force R_C with components F_1 i0, F_2 j0 (friction, in the plane) and N k0 (normal); dashed segment GC labelled "-R k0". Plane alone: orange -R_C at its point C. Dashed grey lines joining the two copies of G and the two copies of C (action-reaction pairs). Small grey basis i0, j0, k0 at O and blue i_s, j_s, k_s at G.
Values to draw: gamma = 20 deg, theta = 30 deg, psi = 35 deg, R = 3.
Alt: Exploded view of the disk, the sphere with its axle and the inclined plane, with the bearing force and torque pair at G, the contact force pair at C and the weights.
Caption: Exploded view: the plane acts on the sphere with $\mathbf R_C = F_1\mathbf i_0 + F_2\mathbf j_0 + N\mathbf k_0$ at $C$; the axle acts on the disk with $\mathbf R_d$ and the torque $\mathbf M_d$ normal to $\mathbf k_s$, and the disk on the axle with $-\mathbf R_d$ and $-\mathbf M_d$.
