## rb11-overview
View: oblique (cabinet) 3D view; x towards the lower left, y to the right, z up, origin O.
Draw: thin grey axes x, y, z. The horizontal circle of radius R in the plane z = R centred at O_1 (0, 0, R) drawn dashed grey, with a counterclockwise arrow (seen from above). The contact circle of radius R on the plane Oxy (dashed light grey). The disc at angle phi: a vertical disc of radius R (light translucent orange fill, orange outline) centred at G = (R cos φ, R sin φ, R), its plane perpendicular to the radial direction i_1. Points O_1, G and C (the contact point, directly below G on the plane) as dots, labelled. The axis of the disc as a solid thin line from O_1 to G, extended a little beyond G. Thick blue unit vectors i_1 (from G, radial, outwards), j_1 (from G, tangent, sense of motion), k_1 (from G, up), labelled. Orange arrow v_0^G along j_1 at G. Dashed green line through C and O_1, extended a little beyond both, labelled "instantaneous axis". Angle arc phi in the plane Oxy from the x axis to the projection of i_1, labelled φ.
Values to draw: phi = 40 deg, R = 1.
Alt: Vertical disc rolling round a circle with its axis through the point O_1 on Oz, the basis i_1, j_1, k_1 at its centre and the instantaneous axis through the contact point and O_1.
Caption: The disc, its centre $G$ on the circle of centre $O_1$, the contact point $C$ and the basis $\{\mathbf i_1, \mathbf j_1, \mathbf k_1\}$ of the key; the instantaneous axis is the line $CO_1$.

## rb11-rotation-phi
View: looking down Oz from above: k points out of the paper (⊙ at O), i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up), labelled. Dashed grey circle of radius R centred at O (projection of the path of G). Point G' (projection of G) on the circle at angle phi, labelled G. Thick blue unit vectors i_1 = cos φ i + sin φ j (from O towards G) and j_1 = −sin φ i + cos φ j (perpendicular, counterclockwise of i_1), labelled; copy of j_1 at G as an orange arrow labelled v_0^G (speed ωR). Counterclockwise arcs with arrowheads from i to i_1 and from j to j_1, labelled φ. Dashed projections of the tip of i_1 with a light translucent orange right triangle (hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg cos φ along i, opposite leg sin φ along j) and leg labels. The disc seen edge-on at G as a thick orange segment of length 2R along j_1 (perpendicular to i_1).
Values to draw: phi = 40 deg, R = 1, unit vectors of length 0.6.
Alt: Top view showing the angle phi of the radial unit vector i_1 and the disc seen edge-on, perpendicular to it.
Caption: View from above ($\mathbf k$ out of the paper). The frame $S_1$ turns counterclockwise by $\varphi$; the disc, seen edge-on, is perpendicular to $\mathbf i_1$ and moves along $\mathbf j_1$.

## rb11-rotation-psi
View: looking at the face of the disc from outside the circle, towards Oz: i_1 points out of the paper (⊙ at G), j_1 to the right, k_1 up. The horizontal line at distance R below G is the plane Oxy.
Draw: the disc as a circle of radius R centred at G (light translucent orange fill). Thin grey axes j_1 (right) and k_1 (up) through G, labelled. Thick dark grey line at the bottom: the plane Oxy. Contact point C at the bottom of the disc, labelled. Thick blue unit vectors j_B = cos ψ j_1 + sin ψ k_1 and k_B = −sin ψ j_1 + cos ψ k_1 drawn from G, labelled, with counterclockwise arcs from j_1 to j_B and from k_1 to k_B labelled ψ (positive sense). A separate clockwise green arrow around G labelled "ψ-dot = −ω (actual sense)". Orange arrow v_0^G = ωR along j_1 at G. Vector GC from G down to C (thin, labelled "GC = −R k_1"). At C, a small orange arrow ωR j_1 and an equal and opposite red arrow labelled "ω × GC" that cancel, with the label "v_0^C(B) = 0".
Values to draw: psi = 30 deg for the drawn basis, R = 1.
Alt: Face view of the disc showing the spin angle psi, the velocity of the centre and the zero velocity of the contact point.
Caption: Disc seen with its axis $\mathbf i_1$ out of the paper. The centre moves to the right at $\omega R$; the no-slip condition makes the spin clockwise (negative), so that the velocity of the body point at $C$ cancels.

## rb11-meridian
View: the vertical meridian plane through Oz and G: i_1 to the right, k_1 (= k) up; j_1 points into the paper (⊗) in this view.
Draw: thin grey axis Oz (vertical, at the left) and the trace of the plane Oxy (horizontal, at the bottom), labelled. Points O (at the corner), O_1 on Oz at height R, G at (R, R), C at (R, 0), labelled. The disc seen edge-on as a thick orange vertical segment through G from height 0 to 2R. Right triangle O_1-G-C with a light translucent blue fill and a right-angle mark at G; legs O_1G labelled "R" (horizontal, along the axis of the disc) and GC labelled "R" (vertical); hypotenuse C-O_1 labelled "R√2". Dashed green line through C and O_1 extended beyond both, labelled "instantaneous axis"; 45-degree angle arc at C between the plane Oxy (towards O) and the axis, labelled 45°. Green arrow omega_B0 = ω(−i_1 + k_1) drawn along the axis from C towards O_1, with its two legs −ω i_1 and ω k_1 dashed. Red arrow a_0^C(B) = ω²R (i_1 + k_1) at C pointing up and to the right (45 deg). Blue arrow a_0^G = −ω²R i_1 at G pointing left towards O_1. Small symbol ⊗ for j_1 near G.
Values to draw: R = 1, omega arrow length 0.6, accelerations length 0.5.
Alt: Meridian plane with the right triangle O_1, G, C, the instantaneous axis along its hypotenuse and the acceleration of the contact point of the disc.
Caption: Meridian plane through $G$ ($\mathbf j_1$ into the paper). The axis of the disc is the leg $O_1G$, the contact radius the leg $GC$; the instantaneous axis is the hypotenuse $CO_1$, at 45 degrees, parallel to $\boldsymbol\omega_{B0}$.
