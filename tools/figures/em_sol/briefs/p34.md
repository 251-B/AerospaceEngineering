## simulator configuration (to connect in milestone H4)

The archetype `rotating-wire-groove` with the `hoop` wire draws a circle in the turning vertical plane (centre at distance e from the axis, theta from the lowest point), here with e = R so that the circle passes through the axis; it turns at a constant rate, while the problem swings the plane (psi = A(1 − cos omega t)), so the swing is only shown through the slider psi. The small oscillation of part (f) is the archetype `oscillator-phase` in forced mode, a second option. Unit values R = 1 m, g = 9.81 m/s², A = 0.2, omega = 1 rad/s. Here q = theta, measured from the lowest point, so phi = theta − 90 deg.
