## rb12-rotation
View: plane of motion, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at the common origin), i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from the origin. Blue unit vectors i_B and j_B rotated counterclockwise by phi: i_B at angle phi above i, j_B at angle phi to the left of j. Arc of phi (counterclockwise, arrowhead at the end) between i and i_B, and a second arc of phi between j and j_B, both labelled phi. Light translucent fill of the right triangle of i_B: hypotenuse i_B (label "1"), adjacent leg along i labelled cos phi, opposite leg (dashed, vertical) labelled sin phi. Same for j_B: hypotenuse j_B, adjacent leg along j labelled cos phi, opposite leg (dashed, horizontal, pointing to negative i) labelled sin phi. Small curved arrow omega = phi-dot about the ⊙ symbol.
Values to draw: phi = 30 deg.
Alt: The body basis i_B, j_B turned counterclockwise by phi from the fixed basis i, j, with k out of the paper.
Caption: Rotation of the box about $\mathbf k$ (⊙) by $\varphi$. The tip of $\mathbf j_B$ moves towards negative $\mathbf i$, which gives the minus sign in $\mathbf j_B = -\sin\varphi\,\mathbf i + \cos\varphi\,\mathbf j$.

## rb12-geometry
View: plane of motion, k out of the paper, origin O at the centre of the semicylinder section, i right, j up.
Draw: grey filled semicircle of radius R centred at O (the cylinder, flat side on the ground line). Top point T of the cylinder (dot, label T). Box (width b, height h, outline only) turned counterclockwise by phi, tangent to the cylinder at C. Contact point C on the circle at angle phi to the left of the vertical through O (dot, label C). Dashed radius OC labelled R; dashed vertical line from O through T. Arc of phi at O between OT and OC (counterclockwise). Light translucent orange right triangle O, foot of C on the vertical through O, C: vertical leg labelled R cos phi, horizontal leg (pointing left) labelled R sin phi, hypotenuse R. Arc T to C on the circle drawn thick green, labelled R phi; the segment from C to M on the bottom face drawn thick green, labelled R phi (equal arcs). Body point M (middle of the bottom face, dot, label M), centre G (dot, label G) at h/2 above M along j_B. Blue body vectors i_B and j_B drawn from G. Dimension h/2 between M and G.
Values to draw: R = 3, b = 4, h = 1.6, phi = 20 deg (R phi ≈ 1.05, well inside b/2).
Alt: The box tilted by phi on the cylinder, with the contact point C, the equal rolled arcs R phi on the cylinder and on the face, and the right triangle of OC.
Caption: Main plane. Tangency puts $C$ on the radius along $\mathbf j_B$; rolling without slipping makes the segment $MC$ on the face equal to the arc $TC$ of the cylinder, both of length $R\varphi$.

## rb12-fbd
View: plane of motion, k out of the paper, same position as rb12-geometry.
Draw: the box outline only (cylinder drawn faint grey). At G: weight arrow -mg j (red, downwards), label mg. At C: normal reaction N j_B (green, along the radius OC, outwards from the cylinder into the box), label N; friction F i_B (green, along the bottom face, pointing to the right along i_B), label F. Dashed arm from G to C with the components -R phi i_B (along the face, label R phi) and -h/2 j_B (label h/2). Blue i_B, j_B at G. Curved arrow phi-ddot counterclockwise around G.
Values to draw: as in rb12-geometry.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the box: weight at G, normal reaction and friction at the contact point C.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The arm from $G$ to $C$ has the components $-R\varphi$ along $\mathbf i_B$ and $-h/2$ along $\mathbf j_B$; friction has the arm $h/2$ and the normal reaction the arm $R\varphi$ about $G$.
