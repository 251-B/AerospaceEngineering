## rb38-triangle
View: the vertical plane OAG, i_1 out of the paper (⊙ at G); j_1 to the right, k_1 = k_0 up.
Draw: ceiling point O at the top (hatched support). G straight below O at distance OG = 2 ell sin(theta/2); A to the left, at the same horizontal distance ell cos(theta/2) from the vertical OG and at mid-height between O and G. Segments OA (string, thin black, labelled ell) and AG (body axis, thick dark grey, labelled ell). The midpoint M of OG (small dot, labelled M) and the altitude AM as a dashed horizontal line labelled ell cos(theta/2). Shade the right triangle AMG (light blue) and AMO (light orange): hypotenuse ell, horizontal leg AM (adjacent to theta/2) labelled ell cos(theta/2), vertical legs MG and OM (opposite) labelled ell sin(theta/2). Angle theta at A between AO and AG (arc labelled theta), split by AM into two arcs theta/2 (labelled). Dashed vertical OG with the dimension 2 ell sin(theta/2). Angle theta/2 between the horizontal through A and AG marked again near A as "axis descends at theta/2". Gravity arrow g at the side.
Values to draw: theta = 110 deg; ell = 1 unit = 150 px.
Alt: Isosceles triangle formed by the string, the body axis and the vertical through O, split into two right triangles.
Caption: The altitude $AM$ splits the isosceles triangle $OAG$ into two right triangles with hypotenuse $\ell$ and angle $\theta/2$ at $A$: $OG = 2\ell\sin\tfrac{\theta}{2}$ and the axis $AG$ descends at $\theta/2$ below the horizontal.

## rb38-psi
View: horizontal plane seen from above, k_0 out of the paper (⊙ at O, labelled k_0 = k_1); i_0 to the right, j_0 up.
Draw: thin grey axes i_0 (right) and j_0 (up), labelled. Rotated axes in blue: i_1 at angle psi from i_0 and j_1 at psi + 90 deg, labelled. Counterclockwise arcs psi from i_0 to i_1 and from j_0 to j_1, labelled psi. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_1: hypotenuse 1, legs cos psi along i_0 and sin psi along j_0 (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for j_1: legs -sin psi along -i_0 (dashed) and cos psi along j_0. The trace of the plane OAG as a thick dashed green line along j_1 through O, labelled "plane OAG"; the trace of the plane Oy_0z_0 as a dashed grey line along j_0.
Values to draw: psi = 35 deg; unit length 150 px.
Alt: Rotation by psi about the vertical, from the plane Oy0z0 to the plane OAG, seen from above.
Caption: $\mathcal B_1$ is $\mathcal B_0$ turned by $\psi$ about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙); $\mathbf j_1$ leans to negative $\mathbf i_0$ (minus sign). The plane $OAG$ contains $\mathbf j_1$ and $\mathbf k_1$.

## rb38-tilt
View: the plane OAG, i_1 out of the paper (⊙ at G, labelled i_1 = i_2); j_1 to the right, k_1 up.
Draw: thin grey axes j_1 (right) and k_1 (up) from G. Rotated axes in blue: j_2 at theta/2 BELOW j_1 (pointing down-right, along AG continued beyond G) and k_2 at theta/2 to the right of k_1 (pointing up-right). Clockwise arcs theta/2 from j_1 to j_2 and from k_1 to k_2, labelled theta/2, with arrowheads showing the clockwise (negative) sense; note "rotation by -theta/2 about i_1". Shaded right triangle (light blue) for j_2: hypotenuse 1, leg cos(theta/2) along j_1, leg sin(theta/2) downwards along -k_1 (dashed), labelled -sin(theta/2). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for k_2: leg cos(theta/2) along k_1, leg sin(theta/2) along +j_1 (dashed). The body axis AG as a thick grey segment ending at G, from the upper left, collinear with j_2.
Values to draw: theta = 110 deg (theta/2 = 55 deg); unit length 150 px.
Alt: Tilt of the axle frame by minus half the angle theta about the normal to the plane OAG.
Caption: $\mathcal B_2$ is $\mathcal B_1$ turned clockwise by $\theta/2$ about $\mathbf i_1$ (⊙), so that $\mathbf j_2$ lies along $AG$: $\mathbf j_2 = \cos\tfrac{\theta}{2}\mathbf j_1 - \sin\tfrac{\theta}{2}\mathbf k_1$.

## rb38-spin
View: plane normal to the axis, j_2 out of the paper (⊙ at G, labelled j_2 = j_b); k_2 to the right, i_2 up (because k_2 x i_2 = j_2).
Draw: the disk outline as a light grey circle. Thin grey axes k_2 (right) and i_2 (up). Body axes in blue: k_b at angle phi from k_2 towards i_2 and i_b at angle phi from i_2 towards -k_2 (counterclockwise), labelled. Arcs phi from k_2 to k_b and from i_2 to i_b. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for k_b: legs cos phi along k_2, sin phi along i_2 (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for i_b: legs cos phi along i_2, -sin phi along -k_2 (dashed).
Values to draw: phi = 30 deg; unit length 150 px.
Alt: Spin rotation by phi about the symmetry axis of the body.
Caption: Spin: $\mathcal B_b$ is $\mathcal B_2$ turned by $\varphi$ about $\mathbf j_2$ (⊙, with $\mathbf k_2$ to the right and $\mathbf i_2$ up); $\mathbf i_b$ leans to negative $\mathbf k_2$.

## rb38-fbd
View: the plane OAG as in rb38-triangle (j_1 right, k_1 up, i_1 out of the paper).
Draw: O on the ceiling; the body (axle AG as a thick bar, disk at its lower end near G as in the statement figure). The string removed and replaced by a thick red arrow T at A pointing from A towards O, labelled T e_AO. Weight mg as a thick red arrow down at G. Guide reaction at G as a horizontal thick red arrow N_2 j_1 (drawn pointing left, towards -j_1, since N_2 = -T cos(theta/2)), labelled N_2, plus a small ⊙/⊗ symbol at G labelled N_1 i_1 (normal to the plane, found zero). The vertical guide line through O and G as a thin dashed grey line. The arm from G to A as a blue arrow labelled r_G^A = -ell j_2, and the perpendicular distance ell sin theta from G to the line AO as a dashed blue segment with a right-angle mark. The moment M_G = -ell T sin theta i_2 as a curved orange arrow at G (clockwise in this view, i.e. about -i_2).
Values to draw: theta = 110 deg; ell = 1 unit = 140 px.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the hanging axisymmetric body with the string tension, its weight and the guide reaction.
Caption: Free-body diagram: weight and the horizontal guide reaction at $G$, tension $T\,\mathbf e_{AO}$ at $A$. About $G$ only the tension has a moment, with arm $\ell\sin\theta$: $\mathbf M_G = -\ell T\sin\theta\,\mathbf i_2$.
