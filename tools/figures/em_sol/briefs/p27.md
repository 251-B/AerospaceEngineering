## p27-potential
View: a plain graph (no frame vectors): horizontal axis "x1 [m]" from 0 to 12, vertical axis "Ṽ = 2 sin x1 − β x1² [m]" from −8 to 3. Ticks and labels at pi, 3pi/2, 2pi, 5pi/2, 3pi on the horizontal axis.
Draw: three curves of Ṽ(x1) = 2 sin x1 − β x1²: β = 0.02 m⁻¹ (solid, label "β = 0.02 (small): oscillation"), β = β_c = 0.0405 m⁻¹ (dashed, label "β = β_c"), β = 0.1 m⁻¹ (dash-dot, label "β = 0.1 (large): escape"). For each curve a thin horizontal dotted line at its starting level Ṽ(pi) = −β pi², starting at x1 = pi and running to the right: for β = 0.02 at −0.197, ending where it meets the curve again near the hump (turning point, mark it with a small open circle labelled "turning point"); for β_c at −0.400, touching the curve at the top of its hump x_c = 7.544 (filled dot labelled "x_c"); for β = 0.1 at −0.987, running to the right edge without meeting the curve again. A filled dot at (pi, Ṽ(pi)) on each curve labelled "release at π" once. A small arrow along the axis from pi to the right labelled "start: Ṽ′(π) < 0".
Values to draw: β = 0.02: Ṽ(pi) = −0.197, Ṽ(3pi/2) = −2.444, Ṽ(5pi/2) = 0.766; β_c = 0.040489: Ṽ(pi) = −0.3996, hump top at x_c = 7.544 with the same value; β = 0.1: Ṽ(pi) = −0.987, Ṽ(3pi/2) = −4.221, hump top at 7.069 with −3.582.
Alt: Scaled potential of the bead without spring for a small, the critical and a large value of beta, with the starting level of each.
