## rb41-rot-psi
View: top view of the ice, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at G, vertical up), i0 to the right, j0 up the page; gravity into the paper (⊗ g, small symbol in a corner).
Draw: thin grey axes i0 and j0 at G (dashed horizontal line through G as in the statement figure). Blue body vectors i_b (i0 turned counterclockwise by psi) and j_b (j0 turned by psi, up and to the left). Arc of psi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i0 to i_b and from j0 to j_b, both labelled psi. Light translucent orange triangle for i_b: adjacent leg along i0 labelled cos psi, dashed opposite leg along j0 labelled sin psi. Light translucent green triangle for j_b: adjacent leg along j0 labelled cos psi, dashed opposite leg along minus i0 labelled sin psi. Arrow U_inf along j0 at the bottom right (wind).
Values to draw: psi = 20 deg, unit vectors 1.5 units long.
Alt: The body basis of the sledge, turned counterclockwise by psi from the fixed basis.
Caption: Rotation about the vertical $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\psi$; the wind blows along $\mathbf j_0$.

## rb41-blades
View: top view in the body basis, k0 out of the paper, i_b to the right, j_b up the page (the sledge drawn with psi = 0).
Draw: G (dot) at the centre. The equilateral triangle A-B-C (thin grey, light translucent fill) with C at (-1, 0), A at (0.5, 0.866), B at (0.5, -0.866) (units of l). The median from C through G to M, the midpoint of AB (dot, label M); dimension marks GC = l, GM = l/2, MA = sqrt(3) l/2, AB = sqrt(3) l. Light translucent orange right triangle G-M-A (right angle at M), legs l/2 and sqrt(3) l/2, hypotenuse GA = l. Blades as short thick black segments: at A and at B parallel to i_b; at C turned counterclockwise by theta from i_b, with the arc theta at C labelled theta. Green unit vectors at C: e_C along the blade and n_C normal to it (k0 x e_C). Dashed normals to the blades: the line through A and B (normal to their blades) extended downwards, and the line through C along n_C extended; their intersection I (dot, label I) below the axis, at (0.5, -1.5 cot theta). Dashed segment GI. Curved arrow about I, clockwise, labelled "turning with u > 0".
Values to draw: theta = 40 deg (I at (0.5, -1.79)), l = 1.
Alt: The triangle of blades, the blade directions, and the instantaneous centre at the meeting point of the normals to the blades.
Caption: The blades are at $\ell$ from $G$; $A$ and $B$ stand $\ell/2$ ahead of $G$. The instantaneous centre $I$ is where the normals to the blades meet: $\overrightarrow{GI} = \tfrac{\ell}{2}\,\mathbf i_b - \tfrac{3\ell}{2}\cot\theta\,\mathbf j_b$.

## rb41-fbd
View: two panels. Left: top view in the body basis (k0 out of the paper, i_b right, j_b up), psi = 0. Right: side view of the plane of i_b and k0 (i_b to the right, k0 up, j_b into the paper ⊗), the ice as a horizontal hatched line.
Draw: Left panel: triangle A, B, C and G as in rb41-blades; side reactions Y j_b at A and at B (green, equal arrows), Y_C n_C at C (green, normal to the blade at C); the horizontal aerodynamic force L + D drawn at G's position (it acts at P above G), red, with label "L + D at P (above G)". Right panel: G on the ice line (dot), P at height l above G (dot, label P, dimension l), C at l behind G and the pair A, B (one dot, label "A, B") at l/2 ahead of G, with the dimensions l and l/2 along the ice; weight mg down at G (red); normal reactions N_C at C and N_A + N_B at A, B, up (green); lift L along i_b at P (red, label L). Curved dashed arrow about the point A,B labelled "tipping about AB".
Values to draw: theta = 40 deg in the left panel, l = 1.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the sledge in top view and side view: weight, normal and side reactions of the blades, and the aerodynamic force at the centre of pressure.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The blades take forces normal to themselves and vertical reactions; the sail force acts at $P$, $\ell$ above $G$. In the side view the lift pitches the sledge about the line $AB$ and unloads the rear blade $C$.

## rb41-wind
View: top view, k0 out of the paper, i0 to the right, j0 up; the sledge moving along i0 (psi = theta = 0).
Draw: velocity triangle at P (seen from above): wind U_inf j0 (black, up), minus the sledge velocity, -v i0 (black, to the left), and their sum, the relative wind u_b^w (blue, up and to the left), with the right angle between U_inf and -v marked; hypotenuse labelled sqrt(U_inf^2 + v^2). Drag D along u_b^w (red, from P), lift L along u_b^w x k0 (red, normal to u_b^w, up and to the right, so that it has a forward component along i0). Dashed projections of L and D on i0: L forward, D backward, labelled "b_L U_inf u" and "-b_D v u" (u = magnitude of the relative wind).
Values to draw: U_inf = 1, v = 1.2, b_L/b_D = 2 (for the arrow lengths: L = 2 D).
Alt: The relative wind of the moving sledge and the lift and drag forces it produces.
Caption: Relative wind $\mathbf u_b^w = U_\infty\,\mathbf j_0 - v\,\mathbf i_0$; the forward force is $\sqrt{U_\infty^2 + v^2}\,(b_L U_\infty - b_D v)$, zero at $v = b_L U_\infty/b_D$.
