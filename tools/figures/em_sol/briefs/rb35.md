## rb35-three-points
View: oblique (cabinet) 3D view of the fixed frame, origin at G; i towards the lower left, j to the right, k up.
Draw: thin grey axes i, j, k. Points G (0,0,0), A (-2,4,0), C (-8,0,2) as dots with labels (scale positions so the figure fits: about 28 px per metre). Dashed black triangle G-A-C. Velocities as orange arrows from each point (scale 0.5 of the position scale): v_0^G = (8,4,0), v_0^A = (4,2,6), v_0^C = (10,-6,8), labelled. Angular velocity (1,1,1) as a green arrow from G, labelled omega.
Alt: The three tracked points of the spaceship with their velocities and the angular velocity.
Caption: The centre of mass $G$, the point $A$ of the solar array and the point $C$ of the rear side, with the measured velocities and the angular velocity that reproduces them.

## rb35-parallel-perpendicular
View: plane that contains the angular velocity and the velocity of G (a plain 2D sketch, no axes of S0).
Draw: from a point G, the unit direction e_omega as a green arrow pointing up-right. The velocity v_0^G as an orange arrow (hypotenuse). Its projection on the direction of omega: blue arrow v_0par along e_omega (adjacent leg), and the perpendicular part v_0perp^G as a blue dashed arrow closing the right triangle (opposite leg), with a right-angle mark and a light translucent fill. Labels: "v_0∥ = 4√3 m/s" on the adjacent leg, "|v_0^G| = √80 m/s" on the hypotenuse, "v_0⊥^G" on the opposite leg. Draw to scale: hypotenuse 8.94, adjacent 6.93, opposite 5.66.
Alt: Right triangle formed by the velocity of G, its part along the angular velocity and its part perpendicular to it.
Caption: The velocity of any body point is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose leg along $\boldsymbol\omega$ is the same for all points; the speed is smallest where the other leg vanishes.
