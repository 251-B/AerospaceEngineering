## rb30-rot-psi
View: along i0, i0 out of the paper (⊙ at G), j0 to the right, k0 up. Origin G at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes j0 (right) and k0 (up), the symbol ⊙ at G labelled i0 = i1. Blue j1 and k1 turned counterclockwise by psi. Arc of psi from j0 to j1 and from k0 to k1, both counterclockwise, labelled psi. Light translucent right triangle for j1: hypotenuse j1 (length 1), adjacent leg "cos psi" along j0, dashed opposite leg "sin psi" parallel to k0. Light translucent right triangle for k1: adjacent leg "cos psi" along k0, dashed opposite leg "-sin psi" along -j0 (to the left).
Values to draw: psi = 35 deg (a drawing value; the initial value is 60 deg).
Alt: First Euler rotation: the basis B1 turned by psi about i0, which points out of the paper.
Caption: First rotation of the 1-2-3 sequence, $\psi$ about $\mathbf i_0$ (⊙): $\mathbf j_1 = \cos\psi\,\mathbf j_0 + \sin\psi\,\mathbf k_0$, $\mathbf k_1 = -\sin\psi\,\mathbf j_0 + \cos\psi\,\mathbf k_0$.

Light translucent right triangle for k2: adjacent leg "cos theta" along k1, dashed opposite leg "sin theta" parallel to i1 (upwards). Light translucent right triangle for i2: adjacent leg "cos theta" along i1, dashed opposite leg "-sin theta" along -k1 (to the left). Small note near the axes: "k1 x i1 = j1 (right-handed view)".
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg (a drawing value; the initial value is 0).
Alt: Second Euler rotation: the basis B2 turned by theta about j1, which points out of the paper.
Caption: Second rotation, $\theta$ about $\mathbf j_1$ (⊙, with $\mathbf k_1$ to the right and $\mathbf i_1$ up): $\mathbf k_2 = \sin\theta\,\mathbf i_1 + \cos\theta\,\mathbf k_1$, $\mathbf i_2 = \cos\theta\,\mathbf i_1 - \sin\theta\,\mathbf k_1$.

## rb30-poinsot
View: side view of Poinsot's construction. The invariant plane is drawn as a horizontal thick grey line (seen edge-on) at the bottom; the unit vector u_H points up, from the plane towards G; G is above the plane at the distance delta. The ellipse is the section of the ellipsoid through G and the contact point P.
Draw: an ellipse centred at G with semi-axes 3.0 (horizontal-ish, tilted) and 1.6 representing the ellipsoid, touching the line of the plane at exactly one point P, below and to the right of G. Point N: foot of the perpendicular from G to the plane; dashed segment GN labelled "delta = 3 sqrt2 l / 2". Segment NP along the plane labelled "rho". Segment GP (solid, red) labelled "r_P = tau l omega" with the red arrow of omega along it from G through P (instantaneous axis, dashed continuation below the plane not drawn). Light translucent right triangle G-N-P with the right angle at N (small square), hypotenuse GP, legs delta (GN) and rho (NP). At P: green arrow of the normal of the ellipsoid (grad F) and blue arrow of H (normal of the plane), both pointing up along u_H from P, drawn side by side as parallel arrows with the label "grad F parallel H". At G: blue arrow H_G0 along u_H labelled "H (fixed in S0)". Small text near P: "v of the body point at P = 0 (rolls without slipping)".
Values to draw: delta = 2.12, rho = 0.71 (initial instant), ellipse semi-axes 3.0 and 1.6 chosen to touch the line at P.
Alt: The ellipsoid fixed to the asteroid touching the invariant plane at P, with the normals of both surfaces along the angular momentum and the right triangle G N P.
Caption: Poinsot's construction: $P$ is on the ellipsoid and on the invariant plane at the distance $\delta$ from $G$; both normals at $P$ are along $\mathbf H_{G0}^b$, so the surfaces are tangent, and $P$, on the instantaneous axis, is at rest.

## rb30-polhode
View: projection on the plane of i_b and j_b, k_b out of the paper (⊙ at G), i_b to the right, j_b up. Axes in units of 1/tau.
Draw: thin grey axes i_b (horizontal, from -3 to 3) and j_b (vertical, from -2.5 to 2.5). Thick orange ellipse 3 omega_x^2 + 4 omega_y^2 = 16: semi-axis 2.309 along i_b and 2 along j_b, centred at G. Red dot at (0, 2) labelled "omega(0): (0, 2, 1)/tau, omega_z = 1 (smallest)". Red dots at (2.309, 0) and (-2.309, 0) labelled "omega_y = 0, omega_z = 1.291 (largest)". An arrowhead on the ellipse just to the left of the point (0, 2), showing the sense of travel counterclockwise (from +j_b towards -i_b). Label inside the ellipse: "polhode around +k_b (major axis of inertia); omega_z never vanishes".
Values to draw: semi-axes 2.309 and 2.
Alt: Polhode of the asteroid projected on the plane normal to k_b: an ellipse around the major axis traversed counterclockwise from the initial angular velocity.
Caption: Projection of the polhode on the plane of $\mathbf i_b$ and $\mathbf j_b$ (units of $1/\tau$): the ellipse $3\omega_{xb}^2 + 4\omega_{yb}^2 = 16/\tau^2$, travelled from the initial point $(0, 2)$ towards minus $\mathbf i_b$, with $\tau\omega_{zb}$ between 1 and 1.291.
