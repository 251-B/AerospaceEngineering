## rb29-bases
View: plane of the wall, k0 out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O), i0 to the right, j0 up. Origin O at the centre of the disk.
Draw: the disk, circle of radius R centred at O, lightly filled grey. Thin grey axes i0 and j0 from O. Point C on the rim at polar angle theta (dot, label C), grey radius OC. Blue disk basis at C: i2 along OC (outward) and j2 perpendicular (counterclockwise direction, tangent to the rim). Arc of theta from i0 to OC at O, counterclockwise, labelled theta. Light translucent triangles at O for i2: adjacent leg cos theta along i0, dashed opposite leg sin theta along j0. At C: a short dashed horizontal line (copy of i0) and the bar basis in orange: i1 at angle phi above the horizontal (up-right), j1 perpendicular to it pointing up-left along the bar. Arc of phi from the horizontal through C to i1, counterclockwise, labelled phi, and arc of phi from the vertical through C to j1, counterclockwise, labelled phi. Light translucent triangle for j1: adjacent leg cos phi along j0 (up) and dashed opposite leg sin phi towards -i0 (left), labelled "-sin phi". Curved arrows labelled "theta-dot k0" about O and "phi-dot k0" about C.
Values to draw: R = 3, theta = 20 deg, phi = 49 deg (consistent with h = 1.15 R: tan phi = 2.819/2.424).
Alt: The disk basis i2, j2 turned by theta and the bar basis i1, j1 turned by phi, both about the axis k0 out of the wall.
Caption: Rotations about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙): $\mathbf i_2$ along $OC$ at the angle $\theta$; $\mathbf i_1 = \cos\varphi\,\mathbf i_0 + \sin\varphi\,\mathbf j_0$ and $\mathbf j_1 = -\sin\varphi\,\mathbf i_0 + \cos\varphi\,\mathbf j_0$ along the bar. The senses of both angles are readings of the statement figure.

## rb29-knob-triangle
View: plane of the wall, k0 out of the paper, i0 right, j0 up. Origin O at the centre of the disk.
Draw: the disk outline (radius R) in light grey, the axis Oy0 as a thin grey vertical line through O, the knob K as a black dot on it at height h (label K, dimension h from O). Point C on the rim at polar angle theta. The bar as a thick grey band from C through K to A (A beyond K, label A, total length L from C), with G at its midpoint (dot, label G). Light translucent right triangle C - corner - K, the corner being the point at the height of C on the axis Oy0: horizontal leg from C to the axis labelled "R cos theta" (opposite to phi), vertical leg from the corner up to K labelled "h - R sin theta" (adjacent to phi), hypotenuse CK labelled s. Mark the angle phi at K between the vertical leg and the hypotenuse (arc), and also at C between the horizontal and the normal i1 (small arc, labelled phi). Dashed grey projections of C on the axes: R cos theta on i0, R sin theta on j0. Orange unit vectors i1 (normal to the bar at K) and j1 (along the bar towards A).
Values to draw: R = 3, h = 3.45, theta = 20 deg, L = 6.5.
Alt: The bar through the knob K with the right triangle of legs R cos theta and h - R sin theta and hypotenuse s that gives the relation between theta and phi.
Caption: $K$ on the line of the bar: $\tan\varphi = R\cos\theta/(h - R\sin\theta)$, with hypotenuse $s = \sqrt{R^2 + h^2 - 2Rh\sin\theta}$ from $C$ to $K$.

## rb29-exploded
View: plane of the wall, k0 out of the paper, i0 right, j0 up; exploded: the disk on the lower right, the bar pulled away up-left along j1 by one disk radius.
Draw: Disk alone: circle of radius R at O with green pin force R_O at O (two components R_Ox along i0, R_Oy along j0), red weight mg down at O, orange force -R_C at C (two components -X along -i0, -Y along -j0). Bar alone (thick band with its groove): orange force R_C at C (components X, Y), red weight 2mg down at G, green knob force N_K along i1 at the bar point K (label "N_K i1, normal to the groove"), purple force F_A at A, horizontal, pointing from A towards the axis Oy0 (A drawn on the left of the axis), label "F_A = -k x_A i0". Dashed grey line joining C on the bar to C on the disk (action-reaction pair). Fixed knob drawn as a black dot on a hatched support at K. Arms along the bar from G: -L/2 to C, s - L/2 to K, L/2 to A (dimension lines). Small orange basis i1, j1 at G and grey i0, j0 at O.
Values to draw: R = 3, h = 3.45, theta = 20 deg, phi = 49 deg, L = 6.5.
Alt: Exploded view of the disk and the bar with the pin pair at C, the pin force at O, the knob force normal to the groove, the pull on A and the weights.
Caption: Exploded view: the pin $C$ acts on the bar with $\mathbf R_C$ and on the disk with $-\mathbf R_C$; the frictionless knob pushes the bar along $\mathbf i_1$; $\mathbf F_A$ pulls $A$ horizontally towards $Oy_0$.
