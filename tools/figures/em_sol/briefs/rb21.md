## rb21-rot-theta
View: from above, k0 out of the paper (⊙ drawn at G), i0 to the right, j0 up. Origin G at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes i0 and j0 from G. Blue i' and j' turned counterclockwise by theta. Arcs of theta from i0 to i' and from j0 to j', labelled theta. Light translucent triangles: for i', adjacent leg cos theta along i0 and dashed opposite leg sin theta along j0; for j', adjacent leg cos theta along j0 and dashed opposite leg sin theta towards -i0. The horizontal projection of the rod drawn as a thick dashed segment along i' from -2.5 to 2.5 (label "projection of the rod"), with small open circles at its ends labelled "G1 (projected)" (on +i') and "G2 (projected)" (on -i').
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg.
Alt: View from above of the azimuth rotation by theta about the vertical k0, with the horizontal projection of the rod along i'.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\theta$: $\mathbf i' = \cos\theta\,\mathbf i_0 + \sin\theta\,\mathbf j_0$ is the horizontal direction under the rod; $\mathbf j' = -\sin\theta\,\mathbf i_0 + \cos\theta\,\mathbf j_0$.

## rb21-rot-phi
View: the vertical plane of the rod; i' to the right, k0 up, j' = j_b into the paper (⊗ drawn at G). Origin G at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes i' (horizontal) and k0 (vertical) from G; a dashed horizontal line along i' labelled "horizontal plane". Blue i_b and k_b turned counterclockwise by phi (the rod raised above the horizontal). Arcs of phi from i' to i_b and from k0 to k_b, labelled phi. Light translucent triangles: for i_b, adjacent leg cos phi along i' and dashed opposite leg sin phi along k0; for k_b, adjacent leg cos phi along k0 and dashed opposite leg sin phi towards -i'. The rod as a thick dark segment along i_b from -L/2 to +L/2 through G; at its ends the wheels seen edge-on as short thick segments of length 2R normal to the rod (along k_b), labelled w1 at +L/2 (G1) and w2 at -L/2 (G2). Small note near G: "rotation about -j_b (out of the paper), counterclockwise as seen".
Values to draw: phi = 45 deg, L = 6, R = 0.9.
Alt: Vertical plane of the rod with the elevation phi from the horizontal projection, the spacecraft basis i_b, k_b and the two wheels at the ends.
Caption: Elevation by $\varphi$ about $-\mathbf j'$ (⊗ for $\mathbf j'$): $\mathbf i_b = \cos\varphi\,\mathbf i' + \sin\varphi\,\mathbf k_0$, $\mathbf k_b = -\sin\varphi\,\mathbf i' + \cos\varphi\,\mathbf k_0$. The sense of $\varphi$ (elevation above the plane $Gxy$) is read from the statement figure.

## rb21-exploded
View: same plane as rb21-rot-phi (i_b up-right at 45 deg, k_b up-left, j_b into the paper ⊗); exploded: the rod in the middle, wheel 1 drawn separated beyond the upper end, wheel 2 separated beyond the lower end.
Draw: the rod alone (thick segment from G2 to G1, centre G marked with the centre-of-mass symbol). At G1 on the rod: orange force -F_1 and orange curved arrow -Q_1; at G2 on the rod: orange force -F_2 and orange curved arrow -Q_2. Wheel 1 alone (edge-on segment of length 2R along k_b, centre G1, shifted 1.2 units further along i_b): orange force F_1 and orange curved arrow Q_1 at G1, with a note "Q_1 . i_b = motor torque". Wheel 2 alone, symmetric, with F_2 and Q_2. Dashed grey lines joining G1 on the rod with G1 on wheel 1 and G2 on the rod with G2 on wheel 2 (action-reaction pairs). Blue curved arrows about i_b on each wheel labelled "rate of phi_1" and "rate of phi_2". Note at the bottom: "no external loads (deep space)".
Values to draw: phi = 45 deg, L = 6, R = 0.9, separation 1.2; force arrows of length 1.2 drawn in an arbitrary direction in the plane (they are unknown internal loads).
Alt: Exploded view of the spacecraft rod and the two reaction wheels with the axle forces and torques as equal and opposite pairs.
Caption: Exploded view: the axle of wheel $i$ acts on the wheel with $\mathbf F_i$, $\mathbf Q_i$ and on the rod with $-\mathbf F_i$, $-\mathbf Q_i$. No external load acts, so the sum of the equations of the three bodies keeps $G$ at rest and $\mathbf H_G$ constant.
