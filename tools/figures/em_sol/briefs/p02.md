caption: The velocity is r theta-dot times (beta e_r + e_theta), so the unit tangent is the hypotenuse direction of a triangle with legs beta and 1. The angle psi between radius and tangent is constant; e_n is e_t turned a right angle counterclockwise.

## p02-spiral-plane
kind: plane / meridian section
view: the plane of the motion, k0 OUT of the paper (⊙ at O); i0 right, j0 up.
draw: alpha = 1, beta = 0.2, curve drawn for theta from -180 deg to 540 deg (r from 0.53 to 6.6; choose a scale where the drawing radius 3.5 fits); point P at theta = 200 deg (r = e^(0.2 * 3.491) = 2.01).
      - Grey axes i0, j0 through O.
      - The spiral r = e^(0.2 theta) as a dark thick curve, with a small arrow in the sense of increasing theta (counterclockwise, growing).
      - Black arrow O->P labelled "r_0^P = r e_r".
      - At P: blue e_r (along OP, outward) and blue e_theta (90 deg counterclockwise from e_r); orange e_t at angle psi = arctan(1/0.2) = 78.7 deg counterclockwise from e_r; green e_n at 78.7 + 90 deg from e_r.
      - Angle arc theta at O from i0 to OP (counterclockwise, label "theta"). Angle arc psi at P from e_r to e_t.
      - Centre of curvature C = O + beta r e_theta: point at distance 0.2 * 2.01 = 0.40 from O along e_theta at P (direction 290 deg in the fixed axes). Draw segment P->C (green dashed, label "rho = r sqrt(1 + beta^2)") and segment O->C (dashed, label "beta r"); shade the right triangle O, P, C with the right-angle mark at O.
      - Faint dashed osculating circle of radius rho = 2.05 about C, through P.
labels: "O", "P", "C", "i0", "j0", "⊙ k0", "theta", "psi", "r", "beta r", "rho", "e_r", "e_theta", "e_t", "e_n", "r = alpha e^(beta theta)".
alt: Logarithmic spiral with the polar and intrinsic bases at P and the centre of curvature C forming a right triangle OPC with the right angle at O.
caption: The tangent makes the constant angle psi with the radius. The centre of curvature lies at beta r along e_theta from the pole, so OPC is a right triangle at O with hypotenuse rho = r sqrt(1 + beta^2).
