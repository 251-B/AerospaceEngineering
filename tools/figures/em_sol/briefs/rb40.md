## rb40-tangent
View: the plane of the cavity, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at O), i0 to the right, j0 up; origin O at the vertex of the parabola.
Draw: the parabola y = x^2/(4R) from x = -2*sqrt(3) R to +2*sqrt(3) R (thick black, hatched below), with short horizontal ground segments at y = 3R beyond each rim; small marks at the rims labelled "y = 3R". Thin grey axes i0 and j0 at O. The contact point P (dot, label P) on the right branch, with a dashed vertical drop to the x axis labelled x_P. At P: the light translucent orange right triangle of the tangent, horizontal leg labelled 2R, vertical leg labelled x_P, hypotenuse along the tangent labelled s = sqrt(4R^2 + x_P^2); arc of beta at the left vertex of the triangle between the horizontal leg and the hypotenuse, labelled beta. Green unit vectors at P: e_t along the tangent (up and to the right) and e_n normal to it (up and to the left, into the cavity). The disk of radius R (thin circle) centred at G on the normal at distance R from P, the segment PG dashed and labelled R e_n, G a dot with label G.
Values to draw: R = 1, x_P = 2 (beta = 45 deg, s = 2*sqrt(2)).
Alt: The parabola with the tangent triangle at the contact point P and the centre G on the normal.
Caption: At $P$ the tangent rises $x_P$ over a horizontal run $2R$: $\cos\beta = 2R/s$, $\sin\beta = x_P/s$. $G$ is at $R\,\mathbf e_n$ from $P$, on the concave side.

## rb40-rot-phi
View: at G, k0 out of the paper (⊙ at G), i0 to the right, j0 up.
Draw: the disk of radius R centred at G. Thin grey axes i0 (horizontal) and j0 (vertical) through G. Blue body vectors i_b (i0 turned counterclockwise by phi, up and to the right) and j_b (j0 turned by phi, up and to the left). Arc of phi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i0 to i_b and from j0 to j_b, both labelled phi. Light translucent orange triangle for i_b: adjacent leg along i0 labelled cos phi, dashed opposite leg along j0 labelled sin phi. Light translucent green triangle for j_b: adjacent leg along j0 labelled cos phi, dashed opposite leg along minus i0 labelled sin phi. Curved arrow above the disk, counterclockwise, labelled Q k0.
Values to draw: phi = 55 deg, R = 1.
Alt: The body basis of the disk turned counterclockwise by phi from the fixed basis.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\phi$, measured from $\mathbf i_0$ as in the statement figure; $\boldsymbol\omega_{b0} = \dot\phi\,\mathbf k_0$.

## rb40-fbd
View: the plane of the cavity, k0 out of the paper, as in rb40-tangent.
Draw: a short arc of the parabola around P (grey, hatched below), the disk of radius R centred at G touching it at P (dot, label P), with the green e_t and e_n drawn at P. At G: weight mg downwards (red), label mg. At P: normal reaction N along e_n (green, pointing from P towards G), label N; friction T_f along e_t (green), label T_f. Curved arrow about G, counterclockwise (red), label Q. Dashed arm from G to P labelled -R e_n. Small dashed triangle at G splitting the weight into -mg sin beta along e_t and -mg cos beta along e_n.
Values to draw: R = 1, x_P = 2 (beta = 45 deg).
Alt: Free-body diagram of the disk: weight, the torque Q and the normal and friction forces at P.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The contact force at $P$ has a normal part $N\,\mathbf e_n$ ($N \ge 0$) and a friction $T_f\,\mathbf e_t$; the torque $Q\,\mathbf k_0$ is a couple.
