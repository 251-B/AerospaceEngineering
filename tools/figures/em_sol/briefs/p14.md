## p14-potential
View: energy diagram, horizontal axis x/a, vertical axis V/(m K a^2).
Draw: grey axes "x/a" (from -4 to 1.6) and "V/(mKa^2)" (from -1.2 to 1.3). Blue curve V/(mKa^2) = (1 - x/a) e^{x/a}: rising from 0 at the left (asymptote, dashed grey line at 0 labelled "V -> 0 as x -> -infinity"), maximum (dot) at (0, 1) labelled "barrier top, unstable", crossing 0 at x/a = 1 (dot, label "launch point, V(a) = 0"), then falling below. Three dashed levels: orange E = 0.72 (the example: m = K = a = 1, v_0 = 1.2) labelled "case 1: v_0 < a sqrt(2K)", meeting the right slope at x*/a = 0.608 (dot, label "x*"); green E = 1 tangent at the top labelled "case 2: v_0 = a sqrt(2K)"; red E = 1.2 above the top labelled "case 3: v_0 > a sqrt(2K)". Arrows: from the launch point towards x*, then a curved reflection arrow back to the right (orange); along the green level towards the top with a dashed arrowhead stopping short of it; along the red level all the way to the left edge (red).
Values to draw: as written; x*/a for E = 0.72 solves (1 - u) e^u = 0.72, u = 0.608.
Alt: The single barrier V = mKa(a - x) e^{x/a} with three energy levels: below, equal to and above the barrier top.
Caption: The launch point $x = a$ lies on the right slope of a barrier of height $mKa^2$; the comparison of $\tfrac12 m v_0^2$ with that height separates reflection, asymptotic approach and crossing.
