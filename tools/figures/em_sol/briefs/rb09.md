## rb09-rotation
View: plane of motion, k_1 out of the paper (⊙ drawn at G); i_1 to the right, j_1 up.
Draw: thin grey axes i_1 (right) and j_1 (up) from G, labelled; ⊙ with label k_1 at G. Body axes in blue from G: i_B at angle theta and j_B at theta + 90 deg, thick arrows labelled i_B and j_B. Counterclockwise arc theta from i_1 to i_B and from j_1 to j_B, both labelled theta. Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_B: hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along i_1 labelled cos theta, opposite leg parallel to j_1 labelled sin theta (dashed). Shaded right triangle (light orange) for j_B: hypotenuse 1, leg along -i_1 labelled -sin theta (dashed), leg parallel to j_1 labelled cos theta.
Values to draw: theta = 60 deg; unit length about 140 px.
Alt: Rotation of the rectangle body axes by the angle theta about the axis normal to the plane.
Caption: Body basis of the rectangle, turned by $\theta$ about $\mathbf k_1$ (⊙). The axis $\mathbf j_B$ leans to negative $\mathbf i_1$, which gives the minus sign of $[{}_0R_B]$.

## rb09-diagonal
View: plane of motion, i_1 right, j_1 up, k_1 out of the paper.
Draw: the rectangle ABCD at theta = 30 deg (so that the diagonal angle beta = 60 deg is clearly visible), thin black outline, vertices labelled (A lower left, B upper left, C upper right, D lower right in body terms: A = G - (b/2) i_B - (a/2) j_B, B = G - (b/2) i_B + (a/2) j_B, C = G + (b/2) i_B + (a/2) j_B, D = G + (b/2) i_B - (a/2) j_B). Body axes i_B, j_B in blue from G. Shaded right triangle (light green) with vertices G, the point G + (b/2) i_B, and C: leg along i_B labelled b/2 = sqrt3 a/2 (adjacent), leg along j_B labelled a/2 (opposite), hypotenuse GC labelled a; angle gamma = pi/6 marked at G between i_B and GC. The diagonal AC as an orange line through G, with GC labelled r_G^C and GA labelled r_G^A = -r_G^C. Horizontal dashed reference line through G and the arc beta = theta + pi/6 from it to GC; the arc theta from the horizontal to i_B, inside it.
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 120 px; b = 1.732; theta = 30 deg.
Alt: Rectangle with the right triangle that shows the half diagonal from G to C of length a at the angle pi/6 to the long side.
Caption: The half diagonal $GC$ has length $a$ and makes the angle $\pi/6$ with $\mathbf i_B$, so its angle from the horizontal is $\beta = \theta + \pi/6$; $A$ is at the opposite end of the same diagonal.

## rb09-fbd
View: plane of motion, i_1 right, j_1 up, k_1 out of the paper.
Draw: the guide as a horizontal line at height 5a/4 (thin, with hatching above it). The rectangle at theta = 60 deg with C on the guide and G directly below C at distance a (as in the statement figure, G drawn at x_1 = 0.9 a for clarity), thin black outline, vertices labelled. Forces as thick red arrows: weight mg down at G; guide reaction N j_1 up at C labelled N; spring force at A pointing from A towards O_1, labelled k|r_0^A|, with the spring drawn as a grey zigzag from O_1 to A. Grey arc showing the positive sense of theta. No inertia forces (inertial frame).
Values to draw: a = 1 unit = 110 px; theta = 60 deg; G at (0.9, 0.25).
Alt: Free-body diagram of the rectangle with its weight, the reaction of the horizontal guide at C and the spring force at A.
Caption: Free-body diagram: weight at $G$, smooth guide reaction $N\,\mathbf j_1$ at $C$ (arm $a\cos\beta$ about $G$) and spring force $-k\,\mathbf r_0^A$ at $A$, pointing to $O_1$.
