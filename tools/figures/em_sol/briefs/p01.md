      - Dashed arc: part of the osculating circle of radius 1.6 about C, passing through P (it is not in this plane; draw it faint and label "osculating circle (seen edge-on, in the plane of e_t and e_n)") - optional, keep faint.

caption: At t = 0 the normal at P points along -i0 and the centre of curvature lies at distance rho from P, beyond the axis, at x = -v_z^2/(a omega^2).
