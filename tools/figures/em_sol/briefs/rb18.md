## rb18-rot-psi
View: from above, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at G), i to the right, j up.
Draw: thin grey axes i and j. Blue i_1 and j_1 turned counterclockwise by psi. Arc of psi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i to i_1 and from j to j_1, both labelled psi. Light translucent triangles: for i_1, adjacent leg along i labelled cos psi and dashed opposite leg along j labelled sin psi; for j_1, adjacent leg along j labelled cos psi and dashed opposite leg towards negative i labelled sin psi.
Values to draw: psi = 30 deg.
Alt: First Euler rotation of the ball, by psi about the vertical axis k.
Caption: First rotation, by $\psi$ about $\mathbf k$ (⊙). $\mathbf j_1$ stays horizontal and is the axis of the second rotation.

## rb18-rot-theta
View: vertical plane of i_1 and k; j_1 points into the paper (⊗ drawn at G), i_1 to the right, k up.
Draw: thin grey axes i_1 (right) and k (up). Blue i_2 and k_2 turned clockwise (as seen) by theta: i_2 below i_1, k_2 to the right of k. Arcs of theta (clockwise arrowheads) from i_1 to i_2 and from k to k_2, labelled theta. Light translucent triangles: for i_2, adjacent leg along i_1 labelled cos theta and dashed opposite leg pointing down (along -k) labelled sin theta; for k_2, adjacent leg along k labelled cos theta and dashed opposite leg along i_1 labelled sin theta. A circle of radius a centred at G and a dot A where k_2 meets the circle (label A, "hole").
Values to draw: theta = 35 deg.
Alt: Second Euler rotation, by theta about the horizontal axis j_1, which carries k_2 towards the hole A.
Caption: Second rotation, by $\theta$ about $\mathbf j_1$ (⊗; positive rotations look clockwise from this side). $\mathbf k_2$ points from $G$ to the hole $A$.

## rb18-slip
View: vertical plane of the lane axis; i to the right, k up, j into the paper (⊗ drawn at G).
Draw: the lane as a horizontal line (hatched below), O at the left with the foul line. Ball: circle of radius a, centre G at height a, contact point P straight below G (dot, label P). Dashed segment GP labelled a (arm -a k). At G: blue velocity xi i (to the right) labelled xi. Clockwise curved arrow about G labelled omega_y (j into the paper, so a positive omega_y looks clockwise). At P: the velocity of the body point split into xi i (blue, right) and -a omega_y i (blue dashed, left); their sum, the slip velocity (xi - a omega_y) i, in orange, labelled v_slip. Text near P: "slip velocity = 0 when rolling without slip".
Values to draw: a = 1.2, xi = 3 units, a omega_y = 0.75 units (the initial state of part (g): a omega_y = xi/4).
Alt: Side view of the ball with the velocity of the centre, the angular velocity and the slip velocity of the contact point.
Caption: Contact kinematics. The body point at $P$ moves with $\mathbf v_0^G$ plus the rotational part $-a\,\omega_y\,\mathbf i$; at the release of part (g) the slip velocity is $\tfrac34\xi_0\,\mathbf i$.

## rb18-fbd
View: same side view as rb18-slip (i right, k up, j into the paper).
Draw: ball outline and the lane line faint grey. At G: red weight -Mg k, label Mg. At P: green normal reaction N k, label N; green friction pointing to the left (against the slip), label "mu N (with slip)". Dashed arm from G to P labelled -a k. Clockwise curved arrow about G labelled "-a F_x j" (the friction to the left has a moment along +j, which looks clockwise from this side and spins the ball forwards). Small inset (top view, x up, y left as in the statement figure) of the lane of width W and length l with the ball on the axis and the pins at x = l.
Values to draw: a = 1.2; inset: W = 1, l = 6.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the bowling ball: weight, normal reaction and friction opposite to the slip.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The friction at $P$ opposes the slip; its moment $a F_y\,\mathbf i - a F_x\,\mathbf j$ about $G$ is the only moment, so $\omega_z$ is constant.

## rb18-velocities
View: graph, time t on the horizontal axis (units xi_0/(mu g)), speed on the vertical axis (units xi_0).
Draw: two straight lines from t = 0: xi(t) = 1 - t (blue, label "xi, speed of G") and a omega_y(t) = 0.25 + 2.5 t (orange, label "a omega_y"). They meet at t* = 3/14 = 0.214 at the value 11/14 = 0.786 (dot, dashed drop lines, labels t* and 11/14). After t* a horizontal line at 0.786 (both, dashed, label "rolling without slip"). Vertical dashed line at t_l = 1 - 2/sqrt(5) = 0.106 labelled "pins (l = xi_0^2/(10 mu g))", with the values 0.894 (xi) and 0.514 (a omega_y) marked on it. Shade the gap between the two lines up to t* lightly (the slip velocity).
Values to draw: horizontal axis 0 to 0.3, vertical axis 0 to 1.
Alt: Speed of the centre and rim speed of the ball against time: they meet at t*, after the ball has already reached the pins.
Caption: During the slip the speed of $G$ falls and $a\,\omega_y$ grows; they would meet at $t^{*} = 3\xi_0/(14\mu g)$, but the ball reaches the pins earlier, at $t_l$, still slipping.
