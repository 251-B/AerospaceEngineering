## rb07-rotation
View: the plane Oxz seen from negative y; i to the right, k up, j into the paper (⊗ drawn at the origin, labelled j = j_B).
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and k (up) from the origin, labelled. Body axes in blue from the same origin: i_B at +45 deg (up and right) and k_B at +135 deg (up and left), thick arrows labelled i_B and k_B. Counterclockwise arc of 45 deg from i to i_B labelled "45°", and the same arc from k to k_B labelled "45°". Shaded right triangle (light blue) for i_B: hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg along i labelled cos 45°, opposite leg parallel to k labelled sin 45° (dashed). Shaded triangle (light orange) for k_B: hypotenuse 1, leg along -i labelled -sin 45° (dashed), leg parallel to k labelled cos 45°. Small note next to ⊗: "rotation +45° about -j (out of the paper)".
Values to draw: angle 45 deg; unit length about 140 px.
Alt: View of the plane Oxz showing the body axes of the ring turned 45 degrees about the axis normal to the paper.
Caption: The body basis of the ring is $\mathcal B_0$ turned by $45^\circ$ about $-\mathbf j$ (out of the paper, $\mathbf j$ ⊗). The axis $\mathbf k_B$ leans to negative $\mathbf i$, which gives the minus sign of $[{}_0R_B]$.

## rb07-contact-section
View: the plane Oxz (vertical section through G), i to the right, k up, j into the paper (⊗). Ground plane z = 0 as a thick horizontal line with hatching below.
Draw: the ring seen edge-on as a thin rectangle (band of half width R/20 along i_B) of length 2R along k_B, centred at G and tilted so that its long side follows k_B = (-i + k)/sqrt2. Point G as a dot. Body axes i_B (45 deg up-right) and k_B (135 deg up-left) in blue from G, short. Point C on the ground: from G go -R/20 along i_B and -19R/20 along k_B (dashed blue segments labelled R/20 and 19R/20, forming a right angle at their junction). Segment GC in orange labelled r_G^C. Shaded right triangle (light green) G - C - foot of the vertical from G on the ground: vertical leg labelled R·sqrt2/2 (height of G), horizontal leg labelled 9·sqrt2 R/20, hypotenuse GC. Mark z_G next to the vertical leg.
Values to draw: R = 1 unit = 150 px; G at height 0.707 above the ground; C at 0.636 to the right of the foot of G.
Alt: Vertical section through the tilted ring showing the centre G, the contact point C on the ground and the height of G.
Caption: Section of the ring by the plane $Oxz$. The coordinates of $C$ in the body frame, turned into $\mathcal B_0$, give the height of $G$, $\sqrt2 R/2$, and the horizontal offset $9\sqrt2 R/20$ of the contact point.

## rb07-mordor
View: oblique (cabinet) view of S_0: i towards the lower left, j to the right, k up.
Draw: thin grey axes from O labelled i, j, k; a small dark disc at O labelled "Mordor (O)". The ring as an ellipse (tilted circle) centred at G = (1.6, 0, 0.707). A generic mass element dm as a small dot P on the ring, with an orange arrow from P towards O labelled dF = -beta r_0^P dm and the position vector r_0^P as a thin grey dashed line from O to P. The vector r_G^P as a thin blue line from G to P. At G, the resultant as a thick red arrow pointing towards O labelled F = -beta M r_0^G, and the text "M_G = 0" next to G.
Values to draw: R = 1 unit = 120 px; G at (1.6, 0, 0.707); P at the angle 60 deg on the ring.
Alt: The ring attracted by Mordor at the origin, with the force on one mass element and the resultant applied at the centre of mass.
Caption: The attraction of Mordor on each element points to $O$ and is proportional to $dm$ and to the distance. Its resultant is $-\beta M\,\mathbf r_0^G$, applied at $G$, with zero moment about $G$.
