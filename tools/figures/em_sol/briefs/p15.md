## p15-fbd
View: same plane as p15-fall-axis: i down the page, j to the right, k out of the paper (⊙).
Draw: the sphere P alone, a grey circle (radius 1 unit) with its centre marked by a dot. Three forces, all drawn from the centre and on the vertical through it, slightly separated horizontally so they do not overlap: red arrow downward, length 3 units (weight), at x = 0; purple arrow upward, length 1.5 units (buoyancy, half of the weight), at x = -0.25 units; orange arrow upward, length 1 unit (drag, drawn for v = 2/3 of the terminal velocity, where the drag is 1.5 x 2/3 = 1 unit), at x = +0.25 units. Labels beside the arrow tips, never on the arrows: "m g i" (red, right of the lower tip), "-rho_0 V g i" (purple, left of its tip), "-6 pi mu r x-dot i" (orange, right of its tip). A thin green arrow outside the sphere, 1 unit to its right, pointing down, length 1.2 units, label "x-dot > 0" under it. Small blue reference axes in the lower left corner: i down, j right, ⊙ k. Note in small text at the bottom: "free sphere: no reaction; inertial frame: no inertia forces".
Values to draw: weight 3 units, buoyancy 1.5 units, drag 1 unit (v = 2 v_t / 3).
Alt: Free-body diagram of the sphere with the weight down and the buoyancy and the drag up.
Caption: Forces on the falling sphere. The buoyancy cancels half of the weight; the drag, opposite to the downward velocity, grows linearly with the speed until it balances the other half at the terminal velocity.
