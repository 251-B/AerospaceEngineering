## p10-potential
View: energy diagram: horizontal axis x, vertical axis V_0 (energy), origin at the bottom centre.
Draw: thin grey axes with labels "x" and "V_0(x)". Blue curve V_0 = c x^4 / 4 for x from -1.25a to 1.25a. Orange horizontal dashed line at height E_0 = c a^4 / 4 across the plot, labelled "E_0 = c a^4 / 4" at its right end. The two intersection points marked with small dots at x = -a and x = +a, with vertical dashed drops to the x axis labelled "-a" (start, "release") and "+a" ("next stop"). The allowed interval [-a, a] shaded lightly (translucent orange band on the x axis). At x = 0 a small double arrow from the curve (V_0 = 0) up to the energy line labelled "T_0 = c a^4 / 4 (largest)". Arrow along the x axis from -a towards +a showing the sense of the first motion.
Values to draw: c = a = 1 (V_0 from 0 to 0.61 at the edges); vertical scale such that E_0 = 0.25 sits at about 40 % of the height.
Alt: The quartic well V_0 = c x^4/4 with the energy level of the release and its two turning points.
Caption: Potential well and energy level of the release. The motion is allowed where $V_0 \le E_0$, between $-a$ and $+a$; the kinetic energy is largest at the origin.
