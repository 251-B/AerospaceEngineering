## rb28-rot-psi
View: the horizontal plane seen from above, k0 out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O), i0 to the right, j0 up; origin O.
Draw: thin grey axes i0 and j0 from O. Contact point C (dot, label C) at distance r from O along the direction psi; segment OC labelled r. Blue unit vectors at C: i1 along OC extended (away from O) and j1 normal to it, turned counterclockwise. Arc of psi (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i0 to OC at O, labelled psi. Light translucent triangles at C for i1: hypotenuse 1, adjacent leg parallel to i0 labelled cos psi, dashed opposite leg parallel to j0 labelled sin psi; for j1: adjacent leg parallel to j0 labelled cos psi, dashed opposite leg towards negative i0 labelled sin psi.
Values to draw: psi = 35 deg, r = 3.
Alt: Polar coordinates r and psi of the contact point and the polar basis i1, j1 at C.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_0$ (⊙) by $\psi$. The frame $S_1$ is attached to the contact point: $\mathbf i_1$ along $OC$ and $\mathbf j_1$ normal to it.

## rb28-rot-beta
View: horizontal plane from above at C, k1 out of the paper (⊙ at C), i1 to the right, j1 up (the picture of rb28-rot-psi turned so that i1 is horizontal).
Draw: thin grey axes i1 and j1 from C. Blue x2 and y2 turned counterclockwise by beta. Arc of beta (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from i1 to x2 and from j1 to y2, both labelled beta. Light translucent triangles: for x2, adjacent leg along i1 labelled cos beta, dashed opposite leg along j1 labelled sin beta; for y2, adjacent leg along j1 labelled cos beta, dashed opposite leg towards negative i1 labelled sin beta. The trace of the coin on the plane as a thick grey segment through C along y2, labelled "tangent of the rim (coin seen from above)".
Values to draw: beta = 25 deg.
Alt: Heading of the coin: the tangent y2 of the rim at C turned by beta from j1.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k_1$ (⊙) by $\beta$. The horizontal tangent of the rim at $C$ is $\mathbf y_2$; $\Lambda$, the rate of $\psi$ plus the rate of $\beta$, is the turning rate of the coin about the vertical.

## rb28-rot-theta
View: vertical section through C normal to the tangent y2: x2 to the right, k1 up, y2 into the paper (⊗ drawn at C), minus y2 out of the paper (note "⊙: minus y2").
Draw: the plane as a hatched horizontal line through C. The coin edge-on: thick grey segment from C upwards and to the left, of length 2R, with G (dot) at the distance R from C; it makes the angle theta with the vertical. Thin grey axes x2 (right) and k1 (up) from C. Blue z3 along CG and x3 normal to the coin (up and to the right). Arc of theta (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from k1 to z3 and from x2 to x3, both labelled theta. Light translucent orange right triangle C, G, foot of G on the plane: hypotenuse CG labelled R, vertical leg labelled R cos theta, horizontal leg towards negative x2 labelled R sin theta. Small triangles for the unit vectors: z3 with adjacent leg along k1 (cos theta) and opposite leg along negative x2 (sin theta); x3 with adjacent leg along x2 (cos theta) and opposite leg along k1 (sin theta).
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, R = 2.
Alt: Edge-on view of the coin tilted by theta, with z3 along CG and the normal x3.
Caption: Rotation by $\theta$ about $-\mathbf y_2$ (⊙). The coin leans towards $-\mathbf x_2$; $G$ is at the height $R\cos\theta$ and at $R\sin\theta$ behind $C$.

## rb28-rot-delta
View: the face of the coin, normal x3 out of the paper (⊙ at G), y2 to the right, z3 up; origin G.
Draw: the coin as a light grey disc of radius R centred at G; C (dot) at the bottom of the rim, at minus R z3. Thin grey axes y2 and z3 from G. Blue body vectors y4 and z4 turned counterclockwise by delta. Arc of delta (counterclockwise, arrowhead) from y2 to y4 and from z3 to z4, both labelled delta. A body-fixed radius (thin dark line, a mark on the coin) along minus z4, ending on the rim. Light translucent triangles: y4 with adjacent leg along y2 (cos delta) and opposite leg along z3 (sin delta); z4 with adjacent leg along z3 (cos delta) and opposite leg towards negative y2 (sin delta).
Values to draw: delta = 30 deg, R = 2.
Alt: The coin seen face-on, spinning by delta about its normal x3.
Caption: Rotation about the normal $\mathbf x_3$ (⊙) by $\delta$. The contact point $C$ is always the lowest point of the rim, at $-R\,\mathbf z_3$, whatever $\delta$.

## rb28-fbd
View: the edge-on section of rb28-rot-theta (x2 right, k1 up, y2 into the paper ⊗).
Draw: the tilted coin edge-on with C and G. At G: weight mg downwards (red), label mg. At C: normal reaction N upwards (green), label N; friction component F_x along x2 (green, to the right), label F_x; the friction component F_y along y2 drawn as the symbol ⊗ in a green circle at C, labelled F_y (into the paper). Dashed arm from G to C labelled R (along minus z3). Small blue basis x3, z3 at G.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, R = 2.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the coin: weight at G and the contact force with its normal and two friction components at C.
Caption: Free-body diagram. The contact force acts at $C$ on the diameter through $G$: its moment about $G$ has no component along $\mathbf z_3$.

## rb28-steady
View: oblique 3D view (axonometric), k0 up, the plane drawn as a light grey ellipse; origin O.
Draw: the vertical axis through O (thin grey, k0). G (dot) on this axis at the height h. The tilted coin (light grey ellipse, edge darker) touching the plane at C, with CG of length R. Dashed circle of radius r on the plane centred at O, traced by C, with an arrow showing the sense of increasing psi (counterclockwise seen from above), labelled "psi-dot". Light translucent orange right triangle O, C, G: horizontal leg OC labelled r, vertical leg OG labelled h, hypotenuse CG labelled R; a short dashed vertical through C and the angle theta marked at C between that vertical and CG (arc with label theta). Thick orange arrow along CG (from C towards G) labelled omega (the angular velocity). At C a green arrow N upwards and at G a red arrow mg downwards.
Values to draw: R = 2, h = 0.9 (theta about 63 deg), r = 1.79.
Alt: Steady motion of Euler's disk: G at rest above O, the contact point circling O.
Caption: Part (f). $G$ stays at the height $h$ above $O$; the contact point runs on the circle of radius $r$, and the angular velocity lies along $CG$. Weight and normal reaction form a couple that turns the angular momentum about the vertical.
