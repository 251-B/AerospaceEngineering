## rb27-string-basis
View: plane Oxy, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at the pulley O), i to the right, j up. Origin O at the top centre.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from O, and a short dashed grey line from O straight down (the downward vertical, label "-j"). Point C at -2L on the x axis (small pulley circle with ⊙, label C) and point B at +2L on the x axis (dot, label B). The string piece O-G as a thick grey line from O down to the right to G (dot, label G), with the dimension "4L - xi" along it. Arc of theta from the dashed downward vertical to OG, counterclockwise as seen (from -j towards i), labelled theta. Light translucent right triangle O - foot - G with hypotenuse OG ("4L - xi"), vertical leg along -j labelled "(4L - xi) cos theta" (adjacent to theta) and dashed horizontal leg along i labelled "(4L - xi) sin theta" (opposite to theta). At G, blue unit vectors i1 (along OG, pointing away from O) and j1 (perpendicular, pointing up-right, i.e. k x i1). A second small arc at O, from i to the direction of i1 measured clockwise, labelled "theta - pi/2" (negative polar angle). The string piece from C straight down to P (dot, label P) with the dimension xi.
Values to draw: theta = 25 deg, L = 1, xi = 2 (OG of length 2 drawn at scale 2 units).
Alt: Plane of the motion with the string piece OG at the angle theta from the downward vertical, its right triangle and the string basis i1, j1.
Caption: $\mathbf i_1 = \sin\theta\,\mathbf i - \cos\theta\,\mathbf j$ along $OG$ and $\mathbf j_1 = \mathbf k\times\mathbf i_1$; the angle from the downward vertical is the polar angle $\theta - \pi/2$. The sense of $\theta$ is a reading of the statement figure.

## rb27-block-basis
View: plane Oxy, k out of the paper (⊙ at G), i right, j up. Origin G at the centre.
Draw: the rectangle (width 2L along i_B, height L along j_B) centred at G, rotated by phi, lightly filled. Thin grey axes i and j from G (dashed horizontal through G, as in the statement figure). Orange unit vectors i_B (from G towards A) and j_B. Arcs of phi from i to i_B and from j to j_B, counterclockwise, labelled phi. Light translucent triangles: for i_B, adjacent leg cos phi along i and dashed opposite leg sin phi along j; for j_B, adjacent leg cos phi along j and dashed opposite leg sin phi towards -i, labelled "-sin phi". Point A at the midpoint of the right short edge (dot, label A) with the dimension L from G along i_B. Curved arrow about G labelled "phi-dot k".
Values to draw: phi = 35 deg, L = 1.5.
Alt: The block with its body basis i_B, j_B turned by phi from i, j and the point A on the axis x_B.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k$ (⊙) by $\varphi$: $\mathbf i_B = \cos\varphi\,\mathbf i + \sin\varphi\,\mathbf j$, $\mathbf j_B = -\sin\varphi\,\mathbf i + \cos\varphi\,\mathbf j$; $A$ is at the distance $L$ from $G$ along $\mathbf i_B$.

## rb27-exploded
View: plane Oxy, k out of the paper, i right, j up; exploded: the weight P on the left, the two pulleys O and C at the top, the block on the right below O, each drawn separately with gaps along the string.
Draw: the block (rotated by phi) with red weight 2mg downward at G, green tension -T i1 at G pointing along the string towards O, purple spring force F_s at A pointing from A towards B (B drawn as a fixed point with a hatched support, and the spring as a zigzag drawn dashed since it is replaced by its force). The weight P (dot) with red weight mg down, green tension T j up (towards C), and grey guide force N_P along i (drawn short, labelled "N_P = 0"), with the guide drawn as a vertical hatched line through C. Each pulley as a small circle with ⊙ and the string tension T on both sides (arrows leaving the pulley along the two string pieces), labelled T; grey axle reaction arrows at the pulley centres labelled "axle". Dashed grey lines joining the string ends at G and at P to the matching pulleys to show the pairs. Small blue basis i1, j1 at G.
Values to draw: theta = 25 deg, phi = 35 deg, L = 1, xi = 2.
Alt: Exploded view of the block, the weight P and the two massless pulleys with the tension T, the spring force, the guide force and the weights.
Caption: Exploded view: the same tension $T$ pulls $G$ towards $O$ and $P$ towards $C$; the spring acts at $A$ towards $B$; the guide force on $P$ is horizontal and turns out to be zero.
