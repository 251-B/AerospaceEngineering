## rb32-rot-theta
View: along the gimbal axis, i_a (equal to i_b) out of the paper (⊙ at G), j_a to the right, k_a up. Origin G at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes j_a (right) and k_a (up), the symbol ⊙ at G labelled i_a = i_b (gimbal axis). Blue j_b and k_b turned counterclockwise by theta. Arc of theta from j_a to j_b and from k_a to k_b, both counterclockwise, labelled theta. Light translucent right triangle for j_b: hypotenuse j_b (length 1), adjacent leg "cos theta" along j_a, dashed opposite leg "sin theta" parallel to k_a (upwards). Light translucent right triangle for k_b: adjacent leg "cos theta" along k_a, dashed opposite leg "-sin theta" along -j_a (to the left). The disk seen edge-on as a thick grey segment through G along k_b (its plane contains i_b and k_b), with the spin axle as a thin dark line along j_b labelled "spin axle j_b = j_d". A small curved arrow about ⊙ labelled "spring -k theta, damper -lambda theta-dot" in the clockwise sense (restoring).
Values to draw: theta = 25 deg.
Alt: Gimbal rotation: the gimbal basis turned by theta about the gimbal axis i_a, which points out of the paper; the spin axle j_b is lifted towards k_a.
Caption: Rotation of the gimbal by $\theta$ about $\mathbf i_a = \mathbf i_b$ (⊙): $\mathbf j_b = \cos\theta\,\mathbf j_a + \sin\theta\,\mathbf k_a$, $\mathbf k_b = -\sin\theta\,\mathbf j_a + \cos\theta\,\mathbf k_a$. Read from the statement figure: the spin axle is the middle bar of the gimbal, along $\mathbf j_b$.

## rb32-rot-phi
View: along the spin axle, j_b (equal to j_d) out of the paper (⊙ at G), k_b to the right, i_b up. Origin G at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes k_b (right) and i_b (up), the symbol ⊙ at G labelled j_b = j_d (spin axle). The disk as a light grey filled circle centred at G (it lies in the plane of the paper). Green k_d and i_d turned counterclockwise by phi. Arc of phi from k_b to k_d and from i_b to i_d, both counterclockwise, labelled phi. Light translucent right triangle for k_d: adjacent leg "cos phi" along k_b, dashed opposite leg "sin phi" parallel to i_b (upwards). Light translucent right triangle for i_d: adjacent leg "cos phi" along i_b, dashed opposite leg "-sin phi" along -k_b (to the left). Small note: "k_b x i_b = j_b (right-handed view)".
Values to draw: phi = 30 deg.
Alt: Spin of the disk: the disk basis turned by phi about the spin axle j_b, which points out of the paper.
Caption: Spin of the disk by $\phi$ about $\mathbf j_b = \mathbf j_d$ (⊙, with $\mathbf k_b$ to the right and $\mathbf i_b$ up): $\mathbf i_d = \cos\phi\,\mathbf i_b - \sin\phi\,\mathbf k_b$, $\mathbf k_d = \sin\phi\,\mathbf i_b + \cos\phi\,\mathbf k_b$.

## rb32-exploded
View: oblique view as in the statement figure (aircraft floor as a grey parallelogram, k_a up, i_a to the right, j_a into the depth). Exploded: the disk pulled out of the gimbal along +j_b by about one disk radius; the gimbal frame (a rectangle containing the gimbal axis i_b and the spin axle j_b) in place; the two aircraft posts with their bearings pulled down along -k_a slightly.
Draw: Disk alone (grey ellipse, centre G'): green force F_bd at its centre, green moment M_bd drawn as a double arrow normal to the axle with label "M_bd = -(k theta + lambda theta-dot) i_b + M_3 k_b, none along j_b", red weight m_d g down at G'. Gimbal frame alone (massless): orange -F_bd and orange double arrow -M_bd at G; at the bearings on the gimbal axis, green F_ab and green double arrow M_ab labelled "M_ab = M_3 k_b, none along i_b"; at the right bearing a curved arrow about i_b labelled "spring -k theta i_b" and another labelled "damper -lambda theta-dot i_b". Aircraft posts alone: orange -F_ab, -M_ab, and the curved arrows +k theta i_b and +lambda theta-dot i_b. Dashed grey lines joining the two copies of G and the two copies of each bearing (action-reaction pairs). Blue basis i_b, j_b, k_b at G; grey i_a, j_a, k_a on the floor; the yaw rate Omega(t) as an upward arrow beside the floor.
Values to draw: theta = 25 deg.
Alt: Exploded view of the disk, the massless gimbal and the aircraft posts, with the spin-axle force and moment pair, the gimbal-bearing force and moment pair, the spring and damper torques about the gimbal axis and the weight of the disk.
Caption: Exploded view: the spin axle carries no moment along $\mathbf j_b$ and the gimbal bearings none along $\mathbf i_b$; about the gimbal axis only the spring $-k\theta\,\mathbf i_b$ and the damper $-\lambda\dot\theta\,\mathbf i_b$ act, and the massless gimbal passes them to the disk.
