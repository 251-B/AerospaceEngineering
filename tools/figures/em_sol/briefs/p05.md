# Figure briefs, Problem 05 (Plane and Glider)

Colour code: fixed axes grey; cable basis blue; position vectors black; velocities green; acceleration orange.

## p05-cable-plane
kind: plane / meridian section
view: k0 points out of the paper (⊙ at O); i0 to the right (direction of flight), j0 up.
draw: drawing configuration theta = 30 deg, r = 5 units, airplane A at (7, 0) (it has flown v_A t = 7 from O). Fixed origin O at (0, 0) with grey axes i0, j0 and ⊙ k0. Dashed horizontal flight line of A through O. Airplane A (small aircraft symbol pointing right, or a filled dot) at (7, 0). Glider B (small glider symbol or filled dot) at (7 - 5 cos 30, 5 sin 30) = (2.67, 2.5). Cable: solid line from A to B. Point H = (2.67, 0) on the flight line below B. Shaded right triangle A-H-B with right-angle mark at H. Black arrows: r0^A from O to A along the flight line (label v_A t), r0^B from O to B. Angle arc theta at A between the segment A-H (pointing back, toward -i0) and the cable A-B, opening upward. Blue unit vectors at A: i1 along the cable toward B, j1 perpendicular, 90 deg counterclockwise from i1 (it points down and back, into the lower left quadrant). Green arrow v_A i0 at A, horizontal to the right.
labels: "O", "A", "B", "H", "i0", "j0", "k0" by ⊙, "v_A t" under O-A, "r" along the cable (above the line), "r cos(theta)" under the leg A-H (adjacent leg, horizontal, pointing back), "r sin(theta)" beside the leg H-B (opposite leg, vertical), "theta" by the arc, "i1", "j1", "v_A i0", "r0^B" on the arrow O-B.
alt: Airplane A towing glider B on a cable of length r inclined at theta above the horizontal, behind A.
caption: Main plane. The position of the glider is the position of the airplane plus the cable vector r i1; the right triangle A H B has hypotenuse r, horizontal leg r cos(theta) toward minus i0 and vertical leg r sin(theta).

## p05-rotation-view
kind: rotation view
view: along k0, out of the paper (⊙ at the common origin); i0 right, j0 up.
draw: drawing configuration theta = 30 deg, so the polar angle of i1 is phi = 150 deg. Common origin with ⊙ k0. Grey fixed axes i0 (right), j0 (up), and a dashed grey half-line along -i0 (left). Blue i1 at 150 deg (up and to the left), blue j1 at 240 deg (down and to the left), same length as the grey axes. Arcs: (1) theta, from the dashed -i0 half-line clockwise up to i1 (small radius, arrowhead at i1); (2) phi = pi - theta, from i0 counterclockwise to i1 (larger radius, arrowhead at i1); (3) phi, from j0 counterclockwise to j1 (arrowhead at j1). Dashed projections of the tip of i1 on the i0 axis (length cos theta, on the negative side) and on j0 (length sin theta), with the small right triangle shaded. A curved arrow around the origin indicating the clockwise turn of the cable as theta grows, labelled "omega = -theta rate k0".
labels: "i0", "j0", "-i0", "k0 (⊙)", "i1", "j1", "theta", "phi = pi - theta" (two arcs), "-cos(theta)" on the horizontal projection of i1, "sin(theta)" on its vertical projection, "-sin(theta), -cos(theta)" near the tip of j1 as its components.
alt: View along k0 of the fixed axes and the cable axes i1, j1, with the angle theta measured from minus i0 and the polar angle pi minus theta.
caption: The cable basis is the fixed basis turned by the polar angle pi minus theta about k0 (out of the paper); a growing theta turns it clockwise. Both components of j1 are negative because j1 is i1 turned a right angle counterclockwise.

## p05-velocity-acceleration
kind: other
view: k0 out of the paper; i0 right, j0 up.
draw: same configuration as p05-cable-plane (theta = 30 deg), zoomed on B. At B: the cable direction drawn as a thin blue line back to A. Green arrows from B: v_A i0 (horizontal, to the right, length 4), -r thetadot j1 (perpendicular to the cable, pointing up and forward, i.e. at 60 deg above +i0, length 3), and their sum v0^B (thicker green) closing the vector parallelogram (dashed sides). Orange arrow a0^B for the constant rate: along the cable from B toward A, i.e. along -i1 (pointing down and to the right at 30 deg below +i0), length 2.5. A dashed light orange arrow along -j1 for the tangential term (drawn short and dashed, labelled as zero at constant rate).
labels: "B", "A", "v_A i0", "-r thetadot j1", "v0^B", "a0^B = -r thetadot^2 i1", "-r thetaddot j1 (zero at constant rate)".
alt: Velocity of the glider as the sum of the airplane velocity and the velocity perpendicular to the cable, and the centripetal acceleration along the cable toward the airplane.
caption: The velocity of B adds the flight velocity of A and a velocity of magnitude r times the angular rate, perpendicular to the cable. At constant rate the acceleration points along the cable toward A.
