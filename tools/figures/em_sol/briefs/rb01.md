## rb01-three-points
View: oblique (cabinet) 3D view of Oxyz; i towards the lower left, j to the right, k up.
Draw: thin grey axes i, j, k from O. Points A (a,0,0), B (0,a,0), C (0,0,a) as dots, labelled, with the dimension a marked on each axis. Light translucent blue triangle A-B-C with dashed edges. Velocities as orange arrows (length v0 = 0.55 a): at A along j; at B along j + k; at C along i + j + k; labelled v_0^A, v_0^B, v_0^C. Angular velocity as a green arrow along j drawn from A, labelled omega; dashed green line through A parallel to j labelled "axis".
Alt: Points A, B and C on the three coordinate axes with their velocities, and the instantaneous axis through A parallel to j.
Caption: The three body points, their velocities and the instantaneous axis of rotation and slip, which passes through $A$ and is parallel to $\mathbf j$.

## rb01-view-along-omega
View: looking along the angular velocity from its positive end: j points out of the paper (⊙). With j out of the paper, k points to the right and i points up (so that k x i = j out of the paper).
Draw: grey axes k (right) and i (up) from O, the ⊙ symbol for j at O. Point A at (i = a, k = 0) with the ⊙ symbol "axis" there. Points B at O' = projection (i = 0, k = 0) and C at (i = 0, k = a). Dashed lines from A to B (length a) and from A to C (length a√2), labelled. Perpendicular velocity parts as orange arrows: at B, v0 along +k (to the right); at C, v0 along +i and +k (up-right, length v0√2), each perpendicular to its dashed radius from A (right-angle marks). Green counterclockwise arc around A labelled omega = v0/a.
Alt: View along the angular velocity showing the perpendicular velocity parts of B and C turning counterclockwise about the axis through A.
Caption: View with $\mathbf j$ out of the paper. The perpendicular parts of the velocities of $B$ and $C$ are normal to their radii from the axis and proportional to them: $v_0$ at distance $a$ and $v_0\sqrt2$ at distance $a\sqrt2$.
