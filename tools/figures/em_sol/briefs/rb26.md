## rb26-polar-basis
View: plane Oxy of the cavity, k out of the paper (⊙ drawn at O), i to the right, j up. Origin O at the centre.
Draw: thin grey axes i (right) and j (up) from O. Grey circle of radius 5d/4 (dashed, "path of the bearing centres"). Point A on it at polar angle theta (dot, label A) with the grey segment OA. Blue unit vectors e_r (along OA, drawn from A outward) and e_theta (at A, perpendicular, pointing counterclockwise). Arc of theta from i to OA, counterclockwise, labelled theta. A second copy of the basis drawn at O: blue e_r and e_theta from O, with arc theta from i to e_r and arc theta from j to e_theta (both counterclockwise). Light translucent triangles: for e_r, adjacent leg cos(theta) along i and dashed opposite leg sin(theta) along j; for e_theta, adjacent leg cos(theta) along j and dashed opposite leg sin(theta) towards -i (to the left), labelled "-sin theta". Also mark points B and C (dots, labels) on the dashed circle at polar angles theta + 135 deg and theta + 225 deg, with dashed grey segments OB and OC and the dashed prolongation of AO beyond O; mark the two 45 deg arcs between the prolongation and OB, and between the prolongation and OC.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, d = 4 (circle of radius 5).
Alt: Plane of the cavity with the polar basis e_r, e_theta of bearing A turned by theta about k, and the positions of the three bearing centres.
Caption: Rotation about $\mathbf k$ (⊙) by $\theta$: $\mathbf e_r = \cos\theta\,\mathbf i + \sin\theta\,\mathbf j$, $\mathbf e_\theta = -\sin\theta\,\mathbf i + \cos\theta\,\mathbf j$. The centres of $B$ and $C$ are $45^\circ$ on either side of the prolongation of $AO$ (reading of the statement figure).

## rb26-rolling
View: close-up of bearing A, plane Oxy, k out of the paper (⊙), drawn rotated so that e_r points to the right and e_theta up (state this in a small basis key in a corner: e_r right, e_theta up, k ⊙).
Draw: on the left an arc of the disk rim (radius d, centre O off the drawing to the left; grey arc, label "disk D"), on the right an arc of the cavity wall (radius 3d/2, hatched on its outer side, label "cavity, fixed"). Between them the bearing: circle of radius d/4 centred at A, touching the disk rim at P_A (left, label P_A) and the cavity at Q_A (right, label Q_A). Velocity arrows (purple), all along e_theta: at A the arrow v_A labelled "(5d/4) theta-dot"; at Q_A zero velocity (small open circle labelled "v = 0"); at P_A an arrow twice as long as v_A labelled "(5d/2) theta-dot"; the straight dashed line joining the tips of the three arrows (velocity distribution across the diameter, through zero at Q_A). A curved arrow around A, clockwise, labelled "omega_A = -5 theta-dot". On the disk rim at P_A the same long arrow, labelled "d omega_D". Dimensions: d/4 from A to Q_A and from A to P_A.
Values to draw: d = 4 (bearing radius 1); rate of theta = 1 (arrow at A 5 units long scaled by 0.4).
Alt: Bearing A between the disk and the fixed cavity with the linear velocity distribution across its diameter, zero at the cavity contact.
Caption: Rolling of bearing $A$: zero velocity at the cavity contact $Q_A$ gives $\omega_A = -5\dot\theta$; the equal velocities at $P_A$ give $\omega_D = \tfrac52\dot\theta$.

## rb26-exploded
View: plane Oxy, k out of the paper (⊙), i right, j up; exploded: the disk drawn at the centre, the three bearings pulled radially outward from their places by one bearing diameter, and short arcs of the cavity wall drawn outside each bearing, also pulled outward.
Draw: disk circle of radius d at O with red weight 2mg downward at O. Bearing A at polar angle theta, B at theta + 135 deg, C at theta + 225 deg; each bearing with red weight mg downward at its centre. For each bearing i, at the cavity contact Q_i (outer side): green normal N_i^c pointing towards O (along -e_r) and green friction F_i^c along e_theta (counterclockwise direction); at the disk contact P_i (inner side): orange normal N_i^d pointing away from O (along e_r) and orange friction F_i^d along e_theta. On the disk, at the matching rim point P_i: orange -N_i^d (towards O) and orange -F_i^d (clockwise direction). Dashed grey lines joining each P_i on a bearing with P_i on the disk to show the action-reaction pairs. Label the forces only for bearing A in full (N_A^c, F_A^c, N_A^d, F_A^d, -N_A^d, -F_A^d); for B and C draw the same arrows with the short labels "c" and "d". Small blue basis e_r, e_theta at bearing A.
Values to draw: theta = 30 deg, d = 4, separation 2 units.
Alt: Exploded view of the disk, the three bearings and the cavity with the normal and friction forces at every contact and the weights.
Caption: Exploded view: the cavity acts on bearing $i$ with $-N_i^c\,\mathbf e_r + F_i^c\,\mathbf e_\theta$, the disk with $N_i^d\,\mathbf e_r + F_i^d\,\mathbf e_\theta$, and the bearing on the disk with the opposite pair.
