## p46-taxiway-plane
View: the vertical plane of the runway; k points out of the paper (⊙ at O); i to the right (sense of taxiing), j up. O on the mean level of the profile, at the left.
Draw: grey axes i, j from O. The profile y = h sin(kappa x) as a dark grey curve for x from 0 to 4 pi (amplitude 1, drawn with h = 1 unit). The wheel B as a small hollow circle on the profile at x_B = 2.4 (y_B = sin 2.4 = 0.675). The gear as a vertical zig-zag spring from B up to P, with a damper (piston symbol) drawn beside it, parallel, both between B and P; label "k" beside the spring and "lambda (part c)" beside the damper, on the outer sides. The airplane P as a grey box (label "m") centred at height y = 3.2 above B's abscissa. Black position vectors from O: r_0^P (to P, label beside its middle above the line) and r_0^B (to B, label below its middle). Dashed projections: from P down to the i axis with the label "c t" under the axis at that abscissa; a dimension line at the right of the gear from B to P labelled "l_s = y - y_b". Dashed horizontal line through B to the j axis labelled "y_b = h sin(kappa c t)". Green velocity arrow at P: horizontal component "c i" to the right, 1.2 units. A small dimension in the first bump: peak height "h" and wavelength "2 pi / kappa".
Values to draw: h = 1; kappa = 1; c t = 2.4; y = 3.2; L (unloaded length) not drawn.
Alt: The airplane P on its landing gear, whose lower end B follows the sinusoidal runway as the airplane taxies at speed c.
Caption: Main plane. The wheel $B$ follows the profile, so its height is $y_b = h\sin(\kappa c\,t)$; the length of the gear is the difference of the heights of $P$ and $B$.
