## p07-fbd

Draw: O (small cross, label "O"); the vehicle A at (a, 0) (filled square, label "A (at rest in S1)"); the dashed circle of radius a about A (relative path of B). The skater B (filled dot, label "B") at psi = -50 degrees, i.e. at (a + a cos 50°, -a sin 50°). The bar AB as a thick dark grey segment. A thin dashed segment from O to B labelled "r_1^B". At B: orange u_AB (along the bar, away from A) and orange u_perp (normal to the bar, in the sense of growing psi: pointing to the upper right of the bar, i.e. at -50 + 90 = 40 degrees from i1), 0.5 units each. Forces at B, each a separate arrow with its label placed off the lines: pink "-T u_AB" along the bar towards A, length 0.9; pink dashed "F_C = m omega^2 r_1^B (inertia)" along OB, away from O, length 1.0; pink dashed "F_COR = 2 m a omega psi-dot u_AB (inertia)" along the bar towards A (psi-dot < 0), length 0.7, drawn parallel to the tension arrow and offset sideways by 0.12 units so the two do not overlap. The relative velocity "v_1^B = a psi-dot u_perp" as a green arrow along minus u_perp (psi-dot < 0), length 0.6. Next to B the symbols "⊙ N k1" and "⊗ m g" with the note "weight and plane reaction normal to the paper, N = m g". Text box at the bottom: "F_A = 0 (O fixed), F_E = 0 (omega constant)".
Values to draw: a = 1.6 units; psi = -50 degrees; psi-dot negative.
Alt: Free-body diagram of the skater in the rotating frame with the bar force towards the vehicle, the centrifugal force away from O, the Coriolis force along the bar and the vertical weight and reaction.
Caption: Forces on $B$ in $S_1$: the bar force $-T\,\mathbf u_{AB}$, the centrifugal force $m\omega^2\mathbf r_1^B$ away from $O$ and the Coriolis force $2ma\omega\dot\psi\,\mathbf u_{AB}$, which points towards $A$ because $\dot\psi \lt 0$; weight and reaction are normal to the plane of the drawing.
