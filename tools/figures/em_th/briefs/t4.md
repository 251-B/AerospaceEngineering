# Figure briefs: Topic 4, Section 4.8 and Chapter 8 of Notes.pdf and Lecture 4

Colour code for the whole topic: fixed axes and fixed basis $\mathcal B_0$ grey; moving or local basis (polar basis
$\mathbf e_r$, $\mathbf e_\theta$, the unit vector $\mathbf u_1$) blue; auxiliary vectors (position and relative position
vectors, velocity, angular momentum, arms) orange; angles and their arcs green; forces (attraction, weight, gravity,
action-reaction pair) pink. Trajectories and orbits dark red; planes light grey, translucent, with a thin dark grey
outline; right triangles and swept areas with a light translucent fill. Thin grey axes, thick vectors with clear
arrowheads, dashed projections. No label may sit on a line or an arrow. ⊙ is drawn as a small circle with a dot, ⊗ as
a small circle with a cross. Named points of the note and where they appear: $O$ (every figure with $S_0$), $A$
(t4-angular-momentum, t4-torque-pair, t4-attraction-example, t4-attraction-circle), $P$ (all but
t4-conic-classes, t4-ellipse-area and t4-effective-potential), $Q$ (t4-torque-pair), $C$ (t4-two-body,
t4-areal-velocity, t4-conic-orbit, t4-conic-classes), $Z$ (t4-two-body).

## t4-torque-pair

Alt: Two particles P and Q pulling on each other with equal and opposite forces along the line PQ, and their arms from a point A, which share the same perpendicular distance to that line.
Caption: An action-reaction pair along the line $PQ$ has the same arm about any point $A$, so its torques cancel ([Lecture 4, Slide 3]; Notes.pdf, Eq. 12.2).

## t4-effective-potential
View: a graph, horizontal axis $r$ (from 0 to 4), vertical axis "$v_{\mathrm{eff}}$" (from $-0.6$ to $1$). Extension: drawn for this note (no figure in the sources).
Draw: the curve $v_{\mathrm{eff}} = -\mu/r + h^2/(2r^2)$ (thick dark red), and dashed grey the two terms $-\mu/r$ and $h^2/(2r^2)$ separately, labelled. Four horizontal energy levels (thin lines, each labelled): $\xi = -0.5$ (only the point $r = p$), $\xi = -0.3$ (ellipse; mark the turning points $r_p$ and $r_a$ with dots and shade the allowed band between them light green on the axis, labelled "radial band"), $\xi = 0$ (parabola; turning point $r_p = 0.5$, allowed to the right), $\xi = 0.3$ (hyperbola; turning point $r_p = 0.442$, allowed to the right). Label the forbidden region near $r = 0$ "forbidden: $v_{\mathrm{eff}} > \xi$".
Values to draw: $\mu = 1$, $h = 1$ ($p = 1$, minimum $-0.5$); ellipse level $\xi = -0.3$: $e = 0.632$, $r_p = 0.613$, $r_a = 2.721$.
Alt: The effective potential of gravity against the distance, with its minimum at the circular orbit and four energy levels showing the band of an ellipse and the open ranges of a parabola and a hyperbola.
Caption: Extension: the effective potential of gravity; the motion is confined to $v_{\mathrm{eff}} \leq \xi$, a band between $r_p$ and $r_a$ for negative energy (built on Notes.pdf, Sec. 7.7; drawn for this note).
