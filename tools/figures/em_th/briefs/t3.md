# Figure briefs: Topic 3, Chapters 6 and 7 of Notes.pdf and Lecture 3

Colour code for the whole topic: fixed axes and fixed basis $\mathcal B_0$ grey; moving or local basis (polar and
cylindrical bases, the rotated basis of $S_2$) blue; auxiliary vectors (velocity, tangent vectors, normal vectors,
gradients, projections) orange; angles and their arcs green; forces (weight, reactions, friction, tension, lift) pink.
Constraint surfaces light grey with a thin dark grey outline; curves (wires, trajectories) dark red. Thin grey axes,
thick vectors with clear arrowheads, dashed projections, right triangles with a light translucent fill. No label may sit
on a line or an arrow. ⊙ is drawn as a small circle with a dot, ⊗ as a small circle with a cross. Chapters 6 and 7 of
Notes.pdf have no figures: every figure redraws a sketch of Lecture 3 or is drawn for this note, as stated in its View
line.
