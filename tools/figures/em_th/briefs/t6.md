## t6-mass-distribution
View: two panels side by side, axonometric axes $x_0$ (towards the viewer, lower left), $y_0$ (right), $z_0$ (up) from $O$. Redraws Notes.pdf Fig. 10.1 and the figure of [Lecture 8, Slide 8].

Thin grey axes, thick vectors with clear arrowheads.

Alt: A discrete set of point masses and a continuous body, each with the position vector of one mass from the origin O.
Caption: Discrete (left) and continuous (right) mass distributions; the moments of the distribution sum or integrate $\mathbf r_0^P$ weighted by the mass (Notes.pdf Fig. 10.1).

## t6-centre-of-mass

## t6-symmetry-theorems
View: axonometric, plane $Oyz$ drawn as a light translucent vertical rectangle through $O$; axes $x_0$ (crossing the plane), $y_0$, $z_0$. No notes figure (illustrates Notes.pdf Sec. 10.3 and 10.4).
Draw: a body symmetric about $Oyz$ (e.g. a rounded shape split evenly by the plane). Two mirror mass elements $dm$ at $(x, y, z)$ and $(-x, y, z)$, joined by a dashed segment normal to the plane, with the signed distances $x$ and $-x$ labelled on each side. $G$ marked on the plane. A small inset or annotation: "products with $Oyz$ cancel".
Values to draw: none.
Alt: A body symmetric about the plane Oyz with a pair of mirror mass elements at opposite x, and its centre of mass on the plane.
Caption: Mirror elements of a plane of symmetry have opposite signed distances to it: $G$ lies on the plane and every product of inertia that involves the plane vanishes.
