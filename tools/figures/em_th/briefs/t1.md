# Figure briefs: Topic 1, Chapter 1 of Notes.pdf (Sections 1 to 8)

Colour code for the whole topic: fixed axes and fixed basis $\mathcal B_0$ grey; rotated (moving) basis blue; auxiliary
vectors (projections, products, the vector $\mathbf A$) orange; angles and their arcs green; right triangles with a light
translucent fill. Thin grey axes, thick unit vectors with clear arrowheads, dashed projections. No label may sit on a
line or an arrow. ⊙ is drawn as a small circle with a dot, ⊗ as a small circle with a cross.

## t1-infinitesimal-rotation
View: axonometric, $x$ towards the viewer, $y$ to the right, $z$ up. No notes figure (illustrates Notes.pdf Secs. 1.7.2, Eqs. 1.71 to 1.73).
Draw: grey axes with three small green arcs, one around each axis near $O$, labelled $d\theta_x$, $d\theta_y$, $d\theta_z$ (right-hand sense). Alt: Three infinitesimal rotations about the coordinate axes combined into one angular velocity vector, and the small change of a vector rotated about it.
Caption: Infinitesimal rotations about the three axes add up to one angular velocity $\boldsymbol\omega$; $[\Omega]\,\mathbf A$ equals $\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf A$ (Notes.pdf Eqs. 1.72 and 1.73).
