## Decisions

- **Page shell.** The `mechanics` flavor takes the head and tail of a "template" page and inserts its own CSS; a generated page used as template would get that CSS twice. Topics 6 to 8 used the hand-written `topic-5.html`, which is replaced now. The shell is kept, byte for byte, in `tools/templates/em-theory-shell.html` and every EM theory page (1 to 8) points to it; the pages of Topics 6 to 8 are unchanged (checked with `build_topic_pages --check`).
- **Mission tools** (deleted when the mission closes): `tools/briefs/EM_THEORY_BRIEF.md` (brief of the note writers), `tools/briefs/check_theory_note.py` (builds a page with placeholders for figures not drawn yet: gate, boxes, KaTeX), `tools/briefs/em_theory_pages.json` (page entries of Topics 1 to 5, moved into `tools/topic_pages.json` when each note is ready, so `build_topic_pages --check` stays green meanwhile).
- **Chapters** (printed numbers of `Notes.pdf`): Topic 1 Ch. 1-2; Topic 2 Ch. 4 Secs. 4.1-4.7 and Ch. 5; Topic 3 Ch. 6-7; Topic 4 Sec. 4.8 and Ch. 8; Topic 5 Ch. 3 and Sec. 4.9.
- **One agent at a time** (user's request): a writer (Opus) per topic or part of a topic, then a figure agent (Sonnet), then a read-only audit (Opus), each finding verified by the orchestrator before it is fixed.

## Conflicts between the brief and the repository (not resolved silently)

1. **Lecture numbers.** The brief gives Topic 1 "Lectures 1 and 2", Topic 3 "Lecture 4", Topic 4 "Lecture 5 (deck 04)" and Topic 5 the deck 05 only. The title slides print: 1 Fundamentals and particle kinematics (deck 01), 2 Particle dynamics (deck 02), 3 Constraints and reaction forces (deck 03), 4 Angular momentum and torque (deck 04), 5 Relative motion (deck 05), 6 Oscillations; `00.pdf` is the course presentation, unnumbered. The brief itself says to use the printed number, and `tools/source_links.json` maps lectures that way, so the notes cite Topic 1 Lecture 1, Topic 2 Lectures 2 and 6, Topic 3 Lecture 3, Topic 4 Lecture 4, Topic 5 Lecture 5.
2. **`render_check`.** `tools/render_check.py` loads KaTeX from the CDN, which is blocked here; pages are rendered instead on a mirror with the npm copy of KaTeX 0.16.8 (the same mirror `build_pdfs --katex` uses).
