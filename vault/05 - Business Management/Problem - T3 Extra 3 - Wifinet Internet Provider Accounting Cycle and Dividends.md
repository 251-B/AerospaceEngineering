# ✏️ Exam Problem: WIFINET S.L. — Telecommunications Accounting Cycle & Dividend Mechanics

## 📄 Official Problem Statement (Transcripción Íntegra)

The company **Wifinet S.L.** was incorporated on **1 January 2016 (01/01/2016)** with a registered capital of **6.003 m.u.**, having an objective of providing wireless Internet in the Alicante coast.

### Fiscal Year 2016 Information:
* To finance operations, the company had taken a bank loan of **2.000 m.u.** at $5\%$ annual interest (payable at the end of each year), the principal being repayable in 2019.
* In 2016, invested in technical equipment worth **5.371 m.u.** (life: $20 \text{ years}$; at the end of 2016 recorded accumulated depreciation of **26 m.u.**).
* Further investment of **206 m.u.** in technology license fees made by 31 December 2016 (useful life: $5 \text{ years}$).
* Spent **636 m.u.** to purchase financial shares of other companies.
* Most of 2016 was devoted to network assembly; operations only ran in the last two months. At the end of 2016, the balance sheet reflected a **loss of 340 m.u.**, and cash in hand/bank was **1.476 m.u.**

In 2017, results were positive. However, the company decided not to distribute dividends but retain all profits to offset the previous year's loss.

### Fiscal Year 2017 Information:
* **Customers & Sales:** Average customers during 2017 was $5,000$ with an average revenue of $0.032 \text{ m.u./month/client}$ in subscription fees. Additionally billed $46 \text{ m.u.}$ for online sales of other products. At the end of 2017, customer receivables were **164 m.u.**
* **Materials:** Stocks exhausted at end of 2016. Purchased home installation supplies for **680 m.u.**, paying **560 m.u.** immediately in cash and deferring the remainder to 2018. Sales consumed **half of the purchased goods**.
* **Labor & Services:** Staff costs (salaries + related) were **559 m.u.**, paid in full in 2017. Paid an additional **312 m.u.** for supplies and outside services.
* **Administrative Costs:** Administrative expenditures totaled **300 m.u.**; postponed payment of **60%** to the following year.
* **Corporate Tax:** Applicable tax rate of **30%**, payable in the first quarter of 2018.

**Required:**
1. Prepare the Income Statement of Wifinet for the year 2017.
2. Construct the Balance Sheet as of **31 December 2017 (31/12/2017)**.
3. Indicate which balance sheet items would be affected and by how much if the company paid a dividend of **20% of net profit at 24:00 on 31 December 2017**.
4. Indicate which balance sheet items would be affected and by how much if the decision was made before the end of 2017 to distribute a dividend of **20% of net profit payable on 30 June 2018**.

---

## 🎯 Exam Strategy & Key Analytical Insights

> [!TIP] How to Approach this Problem on Exam Day
> 1. **Prior Year Losses:** A net loss from a prior year ($-340 \text{ m.u.}$) is recorded in **Stockholders' Equity** as a negative reserve/prior year losses item, reducing net worth.
> 2. **Financial Shares:** Financial shares of other companies ($636 \text{ m.u.}$) represent a **Long-Term Financial Investment** (Non-Current Asset), not an expense!
> 3. **Tax Payable:** The prompt states taxes are payable in Q1 2018 $\implies$ **Corporate Tax Payable** is a **Current Liability** on 31/12/2017 (not a cash outflow in 2017).
> 4. **Dividends Paid vs Declared:**
>    - If paid in cash on 31/12 $\implies$ Cash drops, Retained Earnings drops.
>    - If approved in 2017 but payable in June 2018 $\implies$ Cash is unchanged! A current debt (**Dividends Payable**) is created while Retained Earnings drops.

## 🔢 Step-by-Step Resolution

### Section 1: Income Statement for the Year 2017

#### 1. Commercial Revenues:
* Wireless Subscriptions:
  $$\text{Subscriptions} = 5,000 \text{ clients} \times 0.032 \text{ mu/client/month} \times 12 \text{ months} = \mathbf{1,920.00 \text{ mu}}$$
* Online sales of other products: $\mathbf{46.00 \text{ mu}}$.
* **Total Sales Revenue:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Total Revenues}} = 1,920.00 + 46.00 = \mathbf{1,966.00 \text{ mu}}$$

#### 2. Cost of Goods Sold (Materials Consumed):
* Purchased $680 \text{ mu}$, of which half was consumed:
  $$\mathbf{\text{COGS}} = \frac{680.00}{2} = \mathbf{340.00 \text{ mu}}$$
  *(The other half, $340.00 \text{ mu}$, constitutes ending inventory on the balance sheet).*

#### 3. Operating Expenses:
* Staff salaries and related costs: $559.00 \text{ mu}$.
* Supplies and outside external services: $312.00 \text{ mu}$.
* Administrative expenditures: $300.00 \text{ mu}$.
* **Depreciation & Amortization:**
  * Technical Equipment ($5,371 \text{ mu}$ over $20 \text{ years}$):
    $$D_{\text{equipment}} = \frac{5,371.00}{20} = 268.55 \text{ mu}$$
  * Technology Licenses ($206 \text{ mu}$ over $5 \text{ years}$):
    $$D_{\text{licenses}} = \frac{206.00}{5} = 41.20 \text{ mu}$$
  * Total Depreciation: $268.55 + 41.20 = \mathbf{309.75 \text{ mu}}$.
* **Total Operating Expenses:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Operating Expenses}} = 340.00 + 559.00 + 312.00 + 300.00 + 309.75 = \mathbf{1,820.75 \text{ mu}}$$

#### 4. Operating Profit (EBIT):
$$\mathbf{\text{EBIT}} = 1,966.00 - 1,820.75 = \mathbf{145.25 \text{ mu}}$$

#### 5. Financial Expenses & EBT:
Annual interest on bank loan ($5\%$ of $2,000 \text{ mu}$):
$$\text{Interest Expense} = 5\% \times 2,000.00 = \mathbf{100.00 \text{ mu}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{EBT}} = 145.25 - 100.00 = \mathbf{45.25 \text{ mu}}$$

#### 6. Corporate Taxes & Net Income:
$$\mathbf{\text{Corporate Tax } (30\%)} = 30\% \times 45.25 = \mathbf{13.575 \text{ mu}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Net Income (Net Profit 2017)}} = 45.25 - 13.575 = \mathbf{31.675 \text{ mu}}$$

### Section 2: Balance Sheet as of 31 December 2017

#### Treasury Cash Flow Tracking in 2017:
* Initial Cash (31/12/2016): $+1,476.00 \text{ mu}$.
* Cash Collections from Customers ($1,966 \text{ billed} - 164 \text{ receivables}$): $+1,802.00 \text{ mu}$.
* Cash Outflows:
  * Materials down payment: $-560.00 \text{ mu}$ (leaving $680 - 560 = 120 \text{ mu}$ as Accounts Payable).
  * Staff costs: $-559.00 \text{ mu}$.
  * Outside services: $-312.00 \text{ mu}$.
  * Admin costs paid ($40\%$ of $300$): $-120.00 \text{ mu}$ (leaving $60\% = 180 \text{ mu}$ as Admin Creditors).
  * Bank loan interest paid on 31/12: $-100.00 \text{ mu}$.
* **Ending Cash on 31/12/2017:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Cash}} = 1,476.00 + 1,802.00 - 560.00 - 559.00 - 312.00 - 120.00 - 100.00 = \mathbf{1,627.00 \text{ mu}}$$

#### Asset Values on 31/12/2017:
* **Technology Licenses:** $206.00 - 41.20 = \mathbf{164.80 \text{ mu}}$.
* **Technical Equipment:** $5,371.00 - (26 \text{ prior} + 268.55 \text{ 2017}) = 5,371.00 - 294.55 = \mathbf{5,076.45 \text{ mu}}$.
* **Financial Shares:** $\mathbf{636.00 \text{ mu}}$.
* **Inventories:** $\mathbf{340.00 \text{ mu}}$.
* **Accounts Receivable:** $\mathbf{164.00 \text{ mu}}$.

### Section 3: Impact of 20% Dividend Paid on 31 December 2017

* **Dividend Calculation:**
  $$\text{Dividend Amount} = 20\% \times 31.675 \text{ mu} = \mathbf{6.335 \text{ mu}}$$
* **Balance Sheet Modifications:**
  1. **Cash:** Decreases by $6.335 \text{ mu}$:
     $$\text{New Cash} = 1,627.000 - 6.335 = \mathbf{1,620.665 \text{ mu}}$$
     $$\text{New Total Assets} = 8,008.250 - 6.335 = \mathbf{8,001.915 \text{ mu}}$$
  2. **Stockholders' Equity (Retained Earnings):** Decreases by $6.335 \text{ mu}$:
     $$\text{New Equity} = 5,694.675 - 6.335 = \mathbf{5,688.340 \text{ mu}}$$
     $$\text{New Total Liabilities \& Equity} = 8,008.250 - 6.335 = \mathbf{8,001.915 \text{ mu}}$$

---

### Section 4: Impact of Dividend Approved in 2017, Payable on 30 June 2018

* **Key Accounting Distinction:** No cash outflow occurs on 31/12/2017 because the disbursement takes place on June 30, 2018.
* **Balance Sheet Modifications:**
  1. **Assets:** **Completely Unchanged.** Cash remains at $1,627.000 \text{ mu}$. $\text{Total Assets} = \mathbf{8,008.250 \text{ mu}}$.
  2. **Stockholders' Equity:** Decreases by $6.335 \text{ mu}$ because the dividend commitment has legally reduced retained earnings:
     $$\text{New Equity} = 5,694.675 - 6.335 = \mathbf{5,688.340 \text{ mu}}$$
  3. **Current Liabilities:** A new liability account **"Dividends Payable (Dividendos a pagar)"** appears for $\mathbf{+6.335 \text{ mu}}$:
     $$\text{New Current Liabilities} = 313.575 + 6.335 = \mathbf{319.910 \text{ mu}}$$
  4. **Balance Equilibrium:**
     $$\text{Total Liabilities \& Equity} = 5,688.340 + 2,000.000 + 319.910 = \mathbf{8,008.250 \text{ mu}} \quad \text{(Balanced!)}$$

> Entries were balanced in Python (debits = credits) starting from the 31/12/2016 position implied by the statement. No official numerical key is available in `sources/` (only the statement PDF).

### Phase 1: Hypotheses
* Opening position (31/12/2016) is reconstructed from the data: capital 6,003; loan 2,000; equipment 5,371 less 26 accumulated depreciation; licenses 206; financial shares 636; cash 1,476; loss -340. Check: assets 1,476 + 5,345 + 206 + 636 = 7,663 = loan 2,000 + equity 5,663.
* Licenses are assumed not amortized in 2016 (the statement gives no 2016 amortization; the balance above only closes with the full 206) and are amortized over 5 years in 2017; the equipment is depreciated over 20 years in 2017.
* Stocks were exhausted at end 2016, so half of the 680 purchases is consumed (COGS 340) and 340 remains as inventory. Interest (5% x 2,000) is paid at year end; tax is payable in Q1 2018.

### Phase 2: Definitions and accounting equation
$$A = L + E, \qquad E = \text{Share capital} - \text{Prior-year loss} + \text{Net income}_{2017}$$
* Financial shares are a non-current financial investment, not an expense. A dividend declared reduces equity; it reduces cash only when it is paid, and in between it is a current liability (dividends payable).
