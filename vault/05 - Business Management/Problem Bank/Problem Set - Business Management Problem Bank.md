# Problem Set — Business Management Problem Bank

The bank behind the Extra problems page (1st Midterm: Theory and Problems) and the Exam simulator of the portal. Each line below is one note in this folder; `python tools/build_problem_bank.py` turns them into `subjects/business-management/data/problems.json` and the page `subjects/business-management/extra-problems/index.html`. The Midterm 1 structure (3 short theory cases × 2.0 + 1 Extra Practice problem × 4.0 = 10.0 points, 90 minutes) is in `subjects/business-management/data/exam-blueprint.json`.

> [!info] What is official and what is practice
> P11-P16 are exam-style practice exercises in the style of T3_Additional_EXERCISES.pdf (P11 and P12 supplied by the student, P13-P16 written for the bank), and C01-C24 are short theory cases written for the bank; they are not official exercises and have no official key. The official exercises are solved in their own notes and on the Problems page. Anything the course decks do not define (working capital, current ratio, ROA, ROE, margins, the three-section cash-flow statement, the leverage formula) is marked as an extension in each note.
