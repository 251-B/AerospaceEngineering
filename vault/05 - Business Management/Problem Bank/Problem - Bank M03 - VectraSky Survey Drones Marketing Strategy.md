# Bank M03 — VectraSky survey drones: strategic marketing, product, range and distribution

> [!info] Practice item, not an official exercise
> Written for the bank in the style of Exercise 2 of the examples PDF (TechNova Solutions). The company is invented. Concepts: Topic 5, Slides 6-14 (strategic marketing, segmentation, positioning), 19-24 (product attributes and range of products) and 34-37 (distribution); Examples PDF, Exercise 2.

## Statement

VectraSky is a Portuguese company founded in 2016 in Porto that designs and builds small fixed-wing drones for surveying crops and forests. Before launching its first model, the founders studied the drone market and its competitors: the large manufacturers were focused on hobby users and film-makers, while medium-sized farms and forestry cooperatives in southern Europe had no affordable aircraft able to survey a whole estate in one flight. VectraSky decided to serve only these farms and cooperatives, and its slogan, "The whole farm on one charge", presents its drones as the long-endurance option at a moderate price.

The drones carry the VectraSky name and a green swallow as logo. The wings fold so that the aircraft fits in the boot of a car, it is ready to fly in under five minutes, and it is flown from a tablet application with large buttons that can be used with work gloves. Every airframe is sealed against dust and rain and is flown for 20 hours in the factory before delivery, and the drone comes with a three-year warranty. It is delivered in a rugged, reusable transport case whose label gives the operating instructions in four languages. After the sale, VectraSky ships any spare part within 48 hours and trains the customer's pilots on site. To make itself known, the firm runs flight demonstrations at agricultural fairs and places advertisements in farming magazines.

The current range has two product lines: survey drones (Field 60, Field 90 and Forest 120) and sensor payloads (a multispectral camera and a thermal camera); every product is used for the aerial survey of crops and forests. VectraSky does not sell to its customers directly: its drones are sold by agricultural-machinery dealers, who resell them to the farms and cooperatives. The firm chooses at most two dealers in each region, and only dealers that have a workshop able to repair the drones.

a) Which elements of the strategic marketing approach appear in the text? Identify at least three and justify each with a fact of the text. (0.75 points)

b) Which product attribute strategy does VectraSky follow? Identify at least four attributes, and say which facts of the text are not product attributes. (0.75 points)

c) Describe the range of products of VectraSky (extension or width, depth, length and consistency), and say which characteristics change if the firm adds a Field 75 model to its drone line. (0.5 points)

d) Which distribution strategy does VectraSky follow? Describe the type and length of the channel, whether it is B2B or B2C, and the distribution intensity. (0.5 points)

## (a) Elements of the strategic marketing approach in the text [0.75 pts]

### Hint

Strategic marketing is the analysis system: analysis of the situation, diagnosis, objective, and the strategies of segmentation and positioning. Look in the first paragraph for each of them.

### Checkpoint

- Analysis of the situation: market and competitors' analysis (the founders "studied the drone market and its competitors").
- Market segmentation and targeting: medium-sized farms and forestry cooperatives in southern Europe, a single segment (differentiated concentrated, niche strategy), identified as an unmet need.
- Positioning: "The whole farm on one charge", long endurance at a moderate price (benefits of the product, price-quality relationship).
- Customer orientation (market orientation): the product is designed for the needs of the chosen customers.

### Solution

#### Analysis of the situation

"The founders studied the drone market and its competitors": this is the first stage of strategic marketing, the analysis of the situation (market analysis and competitors' analysis) [Topic 5, Slide 7]. The finding that the large manufacturers left farms and cooperatives without an affordable long-endurance aircraft is an opportunity in the sense of the slide: segmentation identifies the market needs that are not being satisfied [Topic 5, Slide 11].

#### Market segmentation and targeting

"Medium-sized farms and forestry cooperatives in southern Europe" defines a segment with a geographical criterion (southern Europe) and a criterion of type of use of the product (surveying crops and forests) [Topic 5, Slide 10]. "VectraSky decided to serve only these farms and cooperatives": the firm aims its product and its message at a single, well-defined segment, the differentiated concentrated (niche) strategy [Topic 5, Slide 12].

#### Positioning

"The whole farm on one charge ... the long-endurance option at a moderate price" is the image the firm wants to create in the mind of the target customer: positioning [Topic 5, Slide 13]. It is based on a benefit of the product (surveying a whole estate in one flight) and on the price-quality relationship [Topic 5, Slide 14]. Positioning is a strategic decision, not an operative one [Examples PDF, Exercise 4, statement 1].

#### Customer orientation

The whole offer starts from what the chosen customers need, which is the market (customer) orientation of the active marketing stage: producing what potential customers want [Topic 5, Slide 4]. Three of these four elements, each with its fact of the text, earn the full marks.

## (b) Product attribute strategy [0.75 pts]

### Hint

The psychosocial attributes of the product are brand, design, packaging and quality; warranty and post-sale service are further attributes. Promotion is a different element of the marketing mix.

### Checkpoint

- Brand: name VectraSky and a green swallow as logo.
- Design: folding wings, fits in a car boot, ready in under five minutes, tablet application usable with gloves (aesthetic and functional).
- Quality: sealed against dust and rain, 20 hours of factory flight before delivery.
- Packaging: rugged reusable transport case with the instructions in four languages on its label.
- Warranty and post-sale service: three-year warranty, spare parts in 48 hours, on-site pilot training.
- Not product attributes: flight demonstrations at fairs and advertisements in farming magazines belong to the promotion (communication) policy.

### Solution

#### The attributes found in the text

The product is a bundle of tangible and intangible attributes; besides the basic product the customer buys quality, design, packaging, post-sale services and image, and the psychosocial attributes are brand, design, packaging and quality [Topic 5, Slide 19].

| Fact of the text | Attribute of the course |
| :--- | :--- |
| "The VectraSky name and a green swallow as logo" | Brand: a name (the part that can be said) and a logo (design, symbol, colours) [Topic 5, Slide 20] |
| "The wings fold ... fits in the boot of a car ... ready to fly in under five minutes ... large buttons that can be used with work gloves" | Design, from the functional perspective of ease of use [Topic 5, Slide 22] |
| "Sealed against dust and rain ... flown for 20 hours in the factory before delivery" | Quality: the product satisfies the needs of customers who fly in the field [Topic 5, Slide 23] |
| "Rugged, reusable transport case whose label gives the operating instructions in four languages" | Packaging: it protects the content, eases transport and carries the label with information [Topic 5, Slide 22] |
| "Three-year warranty ... spare part within 48 hours ... trains the customer's pilots on site" | Warranty and post-sale services [Topic 5, Slide 23] |

Four of these attributes, each with its fact, earn the full marks.

#### What is not a product attribute

"Flight demonstrations at agricultural fairs" and "advertisements in farming magazines" are not attributes of the product: they belong to the promotion (communication) policy of the marketing mix, the first as an activity close to personal selling and public relations at the fairs, the second as advertising, a paid, non-personal communication through the mass media [Topic 5, Slides 29-30]. The examples PDF draws the same line: communication is not a product attribute [Examples PDF, Exercise 2 (b)].

## (c) The range of products and the effect of a new model [0.5 pts]

### Hint

Count the lines, the products in each line and the products in total. Then ask which of the counts a new model inside an existing line changes.

### Checkpoint

- Extension (width): 2 lines.
- Depth: 3 drones and 2 payloads.
- Length: 5 products.
- Consistency: high, every product serves the aerial survey of crops and forests.
- Field 75: depth of the drone line 3 to 4, length 5 to 6; extension (width) and consistency unchanged.

### Solution

#### Characteristics of the current range

A range of products is an offer of product lines and individual products, described by its extension, depth, length and consistency [Topic 5, Slide 24]. The examples PDF calls the extension the width (breadth) of the range [Examples PDF, Exercise 4, statement 3].

| Characteristic | Definition | VectraSky |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Extension (width) | number of product lines | 2 (survey drones, sensor payloads) |
| Depth | number of products per line | 3 drones; 2 payloads |
| Length | total number of individual products | $3 + 2 = 5$ |
| Consistency | relation of the products with their final use | high: every product is used "for the aerial survey of crops and forests" |

#### A Field 75 model

The Field 75 enters the existing drone line, which is the strategy "extend the product lines" [Topic 5, Slide 24]: the depth of the drone line rises from 3 to 4 and the length of the range from 5 to 6. The extension (width) stays at 2, because no new line is created, and the consistency stays high, because the new drone has the same final use.

## (d) Distribution strategy [0.5 pts]

### Hint

Ask who sells to the final buyer, how many intermediaries stand between the producer and that buyer, whether the buyer is a firm or a consumer, and how many outlets there are in each region.

### Checkpoint

- Indirect channel: independent agricultural-machinery dealers.
- Short channel with one level of intermediaries: producer, dealer, farm.
- B2B: the final buyers are farms and cooperatives, which are firms.
- Selective intensity: at most two chosen dealers per region, with a repair workshop.

### Solution

#### Type and length of the channel

"VectraSky does not sell to its customers directly: its drones are sold by agricultural-machinery dealers": the producer entrusts the sale to independent intermediaries, so the channel is indirect [Topic 5, Slide 35]. One intermediary stands between the producer and the final buyer (producer, dealer, farm), a short channel of one level. Extension: the level of a channel is the number of intermediaries between the producer and the final buyer; the examples PDF calls a channel with none a zero-level channel [Examples PDF, Exercise 2 (c)].

#### B2B and intensity

The final buyers, farms and forestry cooperatives, are firms, so the market is business-to-business (B2B), as in the TechNova case [Examples PDF, Exercise 2 (c)]. "At most two dealers in each region, and only dealers that have a workshop able to repair the drones" is selective distribution, one of the three strategies of Slide 37 [Topic 5, Slide 37]. Extension (definitions not given on the slide): intensive distribution uses as many outlets as possible, selective distribution a limited number of chosen outlets per area, and exclusive distribution a single outlet per area. The workshop requirement shows the criteria of the choice: the characteristics of the product (it needs repairs) and of each intermediary [Topic 5, Slide 37].

## Interpretation

Every decision of VectraSky follows from its strategic choice. A single segment (farms and cooperatives that survey large areas) explains the long-endurance design, the rugged case and the field service; a short, selective channel of dealers with workshops keeps that service close to the customer without the cost of an own sales network.
