# 🏢 Bank C02 — Orbital Fasteners: sorting a day of activity into the functional areas

> [!info] Practice item
> Written for the problem bank. It is not an official exercise and has no official key.

## Statement

Orbital Fasteners S.A. makes titanium bolts certified for satellites. On a single Monday: (1) the purchasing team ordered 2 tonnes of titanium bar; (2) the CNC lines produced 40,000 bolts; (3) the sales engineer presented the company at a space-industry fair; (4) the finance department negotiated a bank loan to buy a new thread-rolling machine; (5) it collected an invoice from a satellite integrator and paid the titanium supplier; (6) HR interviewed two quality inspectors; (7) the plant manager set the production targets for the next quarter and checked last month's scrap rate against the plan; (8) the IT team upgraded the traceability database.

## (a) Classify the eight activities into real, financial and management functions [1.25 pts]

### Hint

Real functions follow the product (supply, production, marketing, support); financial functions follow the money (financing, investing, paying and collecting); management functions are planning, organization and control [Slide 13].

### Checkpoint

- Real: 1 (supply), 2 (production), 3 (marketing), 6 and 8 (support: HR, information systems).
- Financial: 4 (financing and investing), 5 (collecting revenues and paying for inputs).
- Management: 7 (planning and control).

### Solution

| Activity | Function | Group [Slide 13] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. Ordering titanium | Supply (acquisition of inputs) | Real |
| 2. Producing bolts | Production | Real |
| 3. Trade fair | Marketing | Real |
| 4. Bank loan for a machine | Financing and investing | Financial |
| 5. Collecting and paying | Receiving revenues and paying for inputs | Financial |
| 6. Hiring inspectors | Support (Human Resources) | Real |
| 7. Targets and scrap check | Planning and control | Management |
| 8. Traceability database | Support (Information Systems) | Real |

## (b) Explain how the real and the financial cycles relate, and what engineers contribute to the management of a firm like this one [0.75 pts]

### Hint

The financial functions run alongside the real cycle [Slide 13]; recall the role of engineers in business management [Slide 15].

### Checkpoint

- The financial cycle mirrors the real one: funds raised → inputs paid → revenues collected → capital and interest returned.
- Engineers: project planning, time management, problem solving, attention to detail, data processing, technical knowledge.

### Solution

The **financial cycle runs alongside the real cycle** [Slide 13]: the company raises funds (the loan of activity 4), uses them to pay for inputs (the titanium of activity 1, paid in activity 5), turns the inputs into bolts and sells them, collects the revenue (activity 5) and finally returns capital and interest to those who lent the money. Without the financial functions the real cycle stops; without the real cycle there is no revenue to repay the funds.

Engineers bring **project planning, time management and problem solving, attention to detail, experience in data processing, computer skills and technical knowledge** [Slide 15]: exactly what activity 7 (targets and scrap control) and activity 8 (traceability data) require.
