## F12.1 Setting next year's delivery target

- id: F12-S1
- concept_id: u6-management-functions

When the board of a helicopter maker sets the number of aircraft to deliver next year and decides the strategy to reach it, it is performing the organizing function.

### Answer

F

### Reasoning

Setting goals and deciding the strategy to achieve them is planning. Organizing comes afterwards: it arranges the human resources so that their activities contribute to those goals.

## F12.2 A partly known problem

- id: F12-S2
- concept_id: u6-decision-types

A decision on a problem that is neither fully known nor completely new, with alternatives that are only partly planned, is an adaptable decision.

### Answer

T

### Reasoning

The adaptable decision lies between the two extremes of the course: the routine decision (known problem, known alternatives) and the innovative decision (unusual problem, ambiguous and unplanned alternatives).

Topic 6, Slide 5.

## F12.3 The last step of the decision process

- id: F12-S3
- concept_id: u6-decision-process-order

Evaluating the results is the final step of the decision-making process of the course.

### Answer

F

### Reasoning

Evaluating the results is step 7 of eight. The process ends with step 8, implementing changes, which acts on what the evaluation has revealed.

## F12.4 Identifying the critical factors

- id: F12-S4

Identifying the critical factors of a decision is part of gathering information, the second step, which comes before any alternative course of action is considered.

### Answer

### Reasoning

Step 2 of the process is gathering information, described as identifying the critical factors; the alternative courses of action are only considered in step 3.

## F12.5 Entering the launcher market

- id: F12-S5
- concept_id: u6-management-levels

The decision of a satellite manufacturer to enter the market for small launch vehicles is a strategic decision of top management, because it affects the firm as a whole.

### Answer

T

### Reasoning

Top management takes the strategic and general decisions of the management pyramid, those that concern the whole firm and its markets; middle and low management take tactical and operational ones.

## F12.6 Who needs interpersonal skills

- id: F12-S6
- concept_id: u6-managerial-skills

Interpersonal skills are needed only by middle managers, since they are the ones who coordinate the teams of the firm.

### Answer

F

### Reasoning

The interpersonal skill, the ability to interact with people at work successfully, is needed at every level of the hierarchy, from first-line supervisors to top management.

## F12.7 Bargaining over an engine contract

- id: F12-S7
- concept_id: u6-mintzberg-roles

When the CEO of an airframer bargains with an engine supplier over the price of a ten-year contract, the CEO plays an interpersonal role, because the task means dealing with people.

### Answer

F

### Reasoning

Bargaining with another party is the negotiator role, which Mintzberg places among the decisional roles (entrepreneur, disturbance handler, resource allocator, negotiator). The interpersonal roles are figurehead, leader and liaison.

### Source

Topic 6, Slide 9.

## F12.8 Opening a new hangar

- id: F12-S8
- concept_id: u6-mintzberg-roles

When the managing director of a maintenance firm opens a new hangar in a public ceremony, the director plays the figurehead role, one of the interpersonal roles.

### Answer

T

### Reasoning

Acting as the symbolic head of the firm in a ceremony is the figurehead role, listed with leader and liaison among the interpersonal roles. The slide names the roles only; the meaning used is the usual one of Mintzberg's classification.

### Source

Topic 6, Slide 9.

## F12.9 Objectives and alternatives in planning

- id: F12-S9
- concept_id: u6-planning-steps

In planning, the alternative courses of action are developed first, and the strategic objectives are defined afterwards from the best of them.

### Answer

F

### Reasoning

The order of planning is: developing forecasts, defining strategic objectives, developing alternative courses of action and deciding on a course of action. The objectives (step 2) come before the alternatives (step 3).

## F12.10 What a goal is

- id: F12-S10
- concept_id: u6-planning-goal

For the course, a goal is a specific achievement to be attained at some future date, such as certifying a new trainer aircraft by the end of next year.

### Answer

T

### Reasoning

This is the definition of goal on the planning slide; the certification of a given aircraft by a given date is a specific achievement with a future date.
