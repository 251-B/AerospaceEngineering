# ✏️ Problem: Problem 2 — Alfa, S.A.: new debt with a constant financial structure

## 📄 Statement

In year 0 the company Alfa S.A. has the following financial structure: capital 6,000,000; reserves 4,800,000; debt 7,200,000; total 18,000,000. If this company can retain profits of 1,200,000 euros at the end of year 1, what is its ability to obtain new debt in year 1 (how much more can it borrow in year 1 compared to year 0) without changing its financial structure?

## Data and model

| Item | Year 0 (€) |
| :--- | ---: |
| Capital | 6,000,000 |
| Reserves | 4,800,000 |
| Debt | 7,200,000 |
| Total | 18,000,000 |
| Retained profit of year 1 | 1,200,000 |

Equity is capital plus reserves, and the financial structure is the share of equity and of debt in the total [Slides 33-35 of Topic 3]. Retained profit increases the reserves, so equity grows; keeping the structure means keeping those shares.

## Solution

### Step 1. Structure in year 0

$$E_0 = 6{,}000{,}000 + 4{,}800{,}000 = 10{,}800{,}000 \qquad \frac{E_0}{18{,}000{,}000} = 0.60 \qquad \frac{D_0}{18{,}000{,}000} = \frac{7{,}200{,}000}{18{,}000{,}000} = 0.40$$

### Step 2. Equity and total in year 1

$$E_1 = 10{,}800{,}000 + 1{,}200{,}000 = 12{,}000{,}000$$

Equity must still be 60% of the total:

$$0.60 = \frac{12{,}000{,}000}{T_1} \quad\Rightarrow\quad T_1 = 20{,}000{,}000$$

### Step 3. Debt in year 1 and new debt

$$D_1 = 0.40 \times 20{,}000{,}000 = 8{,}000{,}000 \qquad \text{New debt} = D_1 - D_0 = 8{,}000{,}000 - 7{,}200{,}000 = 800{,}000 \ \text{€}$$

## Checks and official key

- **Check of the ratio:** debt-to-equity stays $7{,}200{,}000/10{,}800{,}000 = 8{,}000{,}000/12{,}000{,}000 = 0.667$. ✓
- **Check against the official key** (3_II_NPV_NPV IRR_solutions.pdf, pp. 1-2): equity 60%, debt 40%, total 20,000,000, debt 8,000,000 and new debt 800,000 €. Same results.
