# ✏️ Problem: Exercise 3 — Mini laptop: elasticity and revenue

## 📄 Statement

A company selling mini laptops is marketing a particular laptop in a market where demand corresponds to the expression $Q_d = 25000 - 40p$. This company is considering increasing the selling price of the mini laptop and would like to know what impact the increase in price will have on the total income.

a. Calculate the value of this elasticity considering the current price of its mini laptop is 500 m.u.

b. In view of the above result, state with appropriate reasons whether the firm should increase its price and the effect that total income will have due to this price increase.

## Data and model

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| $Q_d(p)$ | demand | $25{,}000 - 40\,p$ |
| $p$ | current price | 500 m.u. |

Point price elasticity $E_d = \dfrac{dq}{dp}\cdot\dfrac{p}{q}$ (topic 5.2.2.pdf, p. 2), and the relation between elasticity and income of p. 3.

## Solution

### Step 1. Part (a): quantity at the current price

$$q = 25{,}000 - 40 \times 500 = 25{,}000 - 20{,}000 = 5{,}000 \ \text{units}$$

### Step 2. Part (a): point elasticity

The derivative of the demand is $dq/dp = -40$, so

$$E_d = \frac{dq}{dp}\cdot\frac{p}{q} = -40 \times \frac{500}{5{,}000} = -4$$

### Step 3. Part (b): should the firm raise the price?

$\lvert E_d \rvert = 4 > 1$: the demand is **elastic**. A price rise of 1% lowers the quantity sold by about 4%, so total income ($p \cdot q$) **falls** (topic 5.2.2.pdf, p. 3). The firm should **not** raise the price; if anything, a price cut would raise income.

## Checks and official key

- **Check with the revenue function:** $I(p) = p\,(25{,}000 - 40\,p)$, so $dI/dp = 25{,}000 - 80\,p$, which at $p = 500$ is $25{,}000 - 40{,}000 = -15{,}000 < 0$: income falls when the price rises. ✓
- **Check with a small change:** at $p = 501$, $q = 4{,}960$ and $I = 2{,}484{,}960$ m.u., below $I(500) = 2{,}500{,}000$ m.u. ✓
- **Check against the official key** (PED Exercises SOL.pdf, p. 3): $q = 5{,}000$ units, $\eta = -40 \times 500/5{,}000 = -4$, demand elastic, and a price rise would lower total revenue. Same results.
