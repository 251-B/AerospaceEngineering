# 🎙️ Practice 1.1: Elevator Pitch and Value Proposition

> **Practical Guide & Assignment Protocol:** Methodological guide for developing a high-impact, 60-second executive elevator pitch and an accompanying 1-page investment memorandum for an innovative commercial product or service.

---

## 🎯 1. Assignment Specifications and Deliverables
* **Team Structure:** Working groups of up to 6 students.
* **Deliverable 1 (Oral Pitch):** Video or in-class presentation with a **strict maximum duration of 60 seconds**.
* **Deliverable 2 (Executive Report):** Written investment summary with a **strict maximum length of 1 page**.
* **Official Benchmark Reference:** *"Rightline Gear Moki Door Step"* (as pitched on Shark Tank).

---

## ⏱️ 2. The 60-Second Elevator Pitch Structure
An elevator pitch must capture attention, articulate the value proposition, and establish financial credibility within one minute:

```
┌──────────────┬────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Time Window  │ Pitch Segment          │ Core Objective & Key Content                           │
├──────────────┼────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 00 – 10 s    │ The Hook & The Problem │ Identify a painful, widespread consumer friction.     │
│ 10 – 25 s    │ The Solution           │ Present your product/service and why it is unique.    │
│ 25 – 40 s    │ Target Market & Model  │ Size of market, customer segment, and revenue streams. │
│ 40 – 50 s    │ Competitive Traction   │ Current substitutes and your distinct advantage.       │
│ 50 – 60 s    │ The "Ask" & Next Steps │ Capital required, proposed equity/debt, and payoff.    │
└──────────────┴────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 📄 3. Structure of the 1-Page Written Report

The written deliverable must address four mandatory analytical sections:

### 1. Product/Service Description & Core Value Proposition
* Precise technical and commercial description of the product or service.
* Specific pain point or unmet market need it addresses.
* Proprietary advantage (patents, exclusive design, cost advantage).

### 2. Analysis of Existing Market Substitutes
* Detailed audit of current products, services, or workarounds consumers currently deploy.
* Direct comparison matrix highlighting your offering's superiority in price, speed, convenience, or performance.

### 3. Funding Strategy & Capital Sourcing
* Proposed capital structure to launch/scale operations:
  * Internal equity (founders' initial capital).
  * External equity (business angels, venture capital seed rounds).
  * Debt options (subsidized public loans, bank credit, crowdfunding).

### 4. Theoretical Projections & Profitability Expectations
* Preliminary financial forecast:
  * Unit cost of production ($C$) vs retail selling price ($P$).
  * Gross unit contribution margin ($P - C$).
  * Estimated break-even volume and projected timeline to net profitability.
