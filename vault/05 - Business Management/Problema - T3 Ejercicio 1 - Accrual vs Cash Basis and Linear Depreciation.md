# ✏️ Problem 1: Accrual vs Cash Basis, Linear Depreciation & Inventory Sensitivity

## 📄 Enunciado Oficial (Official Statement)

A specific company has started activity on the 1st of January of the present year. To this date (31st of March), it has the following goods that were acquired on 31st of December of the previous year:
* **Factory building:** $1,000 \text{ m.u.}$, Duration $25 \text{ years}$.
* **Office building:** $1,500 \text{ m.u.}$, Duration $50 \text{ years}$.
* **Machinery:** $300 \text{ m.u.}$, Duration $10 \text{ years}$.
* **Transport equipment:** $100 \text{ m.u.}$, Duration $5 \text{ years}$.

*(Note: $\text{m.u.} = \text{monetary units}$)*.

Information corresponding to the 1st quarter is as follows:
* Personnel salaries of the quarter, $450 \text{ m.u.}$, of which $75 \text{ m.u.}$ are pending to be paid after 31/3.
* Materials purchases and used in production, $700 \text{ m.u.}$, of which $125 \text{ m.u.}$ are pending to be paid after 31/3.
* Energy consumption is estimated in $230 \text{ m.u.}$, with nothing being paid till date.
* Sales invoiced to customers have amounted to $1,930 \text{ m.u.}$, of which $315 \text{ m.u.}$ had not been collected till 31/3. The remaining were collected in due time. In manufacturing all sold units, the entire purchased materials have been consumed.

*Do not take into consideration possible existence of Corporation taxes.*

**Questions:**
* **a)** The Company General Manager would like to know at 31/3, the income, expenses, collections/cash inflows, payments/cash outflows, investments and depreciation/amortization of the quarter (assume linear depreciation of the goods along their lifetime).
* **b)** To what extent will the previous response change, if only half of the purchased materials had been consumed in the manufacturing of the sold units?

---

## 📊 1. Data Identification & Accounting Principles

### Accounting Period:
* 1st Quarter: January 1st to March 31st ($t = 3 \text{ months} = \frac{1}{4} \text{ year}$).

### Fixed Capital Goods Acquired (December 31st):
$$\begin{array}{|l|r|c|r|}
\hline
\textbf{Fixed Asset} & \textbf{Cost (m.u.)} & \textbf{Useful Life (Years)} & \textbf{Annual Dep. (m.u.)} \\
\hline
\text{Factory building} & 1,000 & 25 & 1,000 / 25 = 40.0 \\
\text{Office building} & 1,500 & 50 & 1,500 / 50 = 30.0 \\
\text{Machinery} & 300 & 10 & 300 / 10 = 30.0 \\
\text{Transport equipment} & 100 & 5 & 100 / 5 = 20.0 \\
\hline
\textbf{Total Fixed Capital} & \mathbf{2,900} & - & \mathbf{120.0} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

### Quarterly Depreciation Formulation:
Linear depreciation allocates the asset cost evenly over time:
$$D_{\text{quarter}} = \frac{\text{Annual Depreciation}}{4} = \frac{\text{Cost}}{4 \times n}$$

---

## 🔢 2. Step-by-Step Resolution: Part (a)

### Step 1: Depreciation / Amortization for the 1st Quarter
$$\begin{aligned}
D_{\text{factory}} &= \frac{40}{4} = 10.0 \text{ m.u.} \\
D_{\text{office}} &= \frac{30}{4} = 7.5 \text{ m.u.} \\
D_{\text{machinery}} &= \frac{30}{4} = 7.5 \text{ m.u.} \\
D_{\text{transport}} &= \frac{20}{4} = 5.0 \text{ m.u.} \\
\mathbf{D_{\text{total}}} &= 10.0 + 7.5 + 7.5 + 5.0 = \mathbf{30.0 \text{ m.u.}}
\end{aligned}$$

### Step 2: Income / Revenues (Ingresos)
Under the **accrual principle**, revenue is recognized when sales transactions are legally completed and invoiced, regardless of cash collection:
$$\mathbf{\text{Income}} = \text{Total Sales Invoiced} = \mathbf{1,930.0 \text{ m.u.}}$$

### Step 3: Expenses (Gastos)
Expenses represent the economic value of goods and services consumed to generate quarterly revenues:
* **Personnel Salaries:** $450.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (the full labor cost incurred in the quarter).
* **Materials Consumed:** $700.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (since 100% of purchased materials were consumed in production).
* **Energy Consumption:** $230.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (consumed utility cost).
* **Depreciation of Fixed Assets:** $30.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (wear and tear of productive equipment).
$$\mathbf{\text{Expenses}} = 450.0 + 700.0 + 230.0 + 30.0 = \mathbf{1,410.0 \text{ m.u.}}$$

*(Quarterly Net Profit $= \text{Income} - \text{Expenses} = 1,930.0 - 1,410.0 = 520.0 \text{ m.u.}$).*

### Step 4: Collections / Cash Inflows (Cobros)
Collections represent physical liquid cash actually received from customers during the quarter:
$$\mathbf{\text{Collections}} = \text{Total Invoiced} - \text{Pending at 31/3} = 1,930.0 - 315.0 = \mathbf{1,615.0 \text{ m.u.}}$$
*(The remaining $315.0 \text{ m.u.}$ is an Account Receivable on the Balance Sheet).*

### Step 5: Payments / Cash Outflows (Pagos)
Payments represent liquid cash disbursed from the corporate bank account or cash till:
* **Salaries Paid:** $450.0 - 75.0 = 375.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (with $75.0 \text{ m.u.}$ as salaries payable).
* **Materials Paid:** $700.0 - 125.0 = 575.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (with $125.0 \text{ m.u.}$ as accounts payable).
* **Energy Paid:** $0.0 \text{ m.u.}$ ($230.0 \text{ m.u.}$ is entirely pending payment).
* **Depreciation Paid:** $0.0 \text{ m.u.}$ (depreciation is a non-cash expense).
$$\mathbf{\text{Payments}} = 375.0 + 575.0 + 0.0 = \mathbf{950.0 \text{ m.u.}}$$

*(Net Cash Generated $= \text{Collections} - \text{Payments} = 1,615.0 - 950.0 = 665.0 \text{ m.u.}$).*

### Step 6: Investments (Inversiones)
* The fixed assets were acquired on December 31st of the previous year for an initial capital outlay of:
  $$\text{Initial Fixed Capital Investment} = 1,000 + 1,500 + 300 + 100 = 2,900.0 \text{ m.u.}$$
* During the 1st quarter itself, **no new fixed asset investments** were carried out ($\text{Q1 New Fixed Capex} = 0 \text{ m.u.}$).

---

## 🔢 3. Step-by-Step Resolution: Part (b) — Inventory Sensitivity

**Condition:** Only half of purchased materials are consumed ($50\%$), while the remaining half is stored in warehouse inventory.

### Analytical Adjustments:
1. **Materials Consumed (Expense):**
   $$\text{Materials Expense} = \frac{700.0}{2} = 350.0 \text{ m.u.}$$
2. **Ending Inventory (Balance Sheet Asset):**
   $$\text{Raw Materials Inventory} = 700.0 - 350.0 = 350.0 \text{ m.u.}$$
   This $350.0 \text{ m.u.}$ is capital stored as a Current Asset, not an expense of the period.
3. **Impact on Income Statement Expenses:**
   $$\mathbf{\text{New Expenses}} = 450.0 + \mathbf{350.0} + 230.0 + 30.0 = \mathbf{1,060.0 \text{ m.u.}}$$
   $$\Delta \text{Expenses} = -350.0 \text{ m.u.}$$
   $$\mathbf{\text{New Net Income}} = 1,930.0 - 1,060.0 = \mathbf{870.0 \text{ m.u.}} \quad (\text{increased by } 350.0 \text{ m.u.})$$
4. **Impact on Cash Flows (Collections and Payments):**
   * **Incomes:** Unchanged at $1,930.0 \text{ m.u.}$
   * **Collections:** Unchanged at $1,615.0 \text{ m.u.}$
   * **Payments:** **Completely unchanged at $950.0 \text{ m.u.}$!**  
     *Why?* Because cash payments to suppliers depend on **purchases agreed and invoiced** ($700 - 125 = 575 \text{ m.u.}$), regardless of whether the material was consumed in machines or stacked in a warehouse.
5. **Depreciation:** Unchanged at $30.0 \text{ m.u.}$

---

## 🎯 4. Summary Comparison Table

$$\begin{array}{|l|r|r|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Financial Magnitude} & \textbf{Part (a): 100\% Consumed} & \textbf{Part (b): 50\% Consumed} & \textbf{Variation } \Delta \\
\hline
\text{Income (Revenues)} & 1,930.0 \text{ m.u.} & 1,930.0 \text{ m.u.} & 0 \\
\text{Operating Expenses} & 1,410.0 \text{ m.u.} & 1,060.0 \text{ m.u.} & -350.0 \text{ m.u.} \\
\text{Net Income (Profit)} & \mathbf{520.0 \text{ m.u.}} & \mathbf{870.0 \text{ m.u.}} & \mathbf{+350.0 \text{ m.u.}} \\
\hline
\text{Collections (Cash In)} & 1,615.0 \text{ m.u.} & 1,615.0 \text{ m.u.} & 0 \\
\text{Payments (Cash Out)} & 950.0 \text{ m.u.} & 950.0 \text{ m.u.} & 0 \\
\text{Net Cash Flow} & \mathbf{665.0 \text{ m.u.}} & \mathbf{665.0 \text{ m.u.}} & \mathbf{0} \\
\hline
\text{Ending Inventory Asset} & 0.0 \text{ m.u.} & 350.0 \text{ m.u.} & +350.0 \text{ m.u.} \\
\text{Depreciation} & 30.0 \text{ m.u.} & 30.0 \text{ m.u.} & 0 \\
\hline
\end{array}$$
