# ✏️ Exam Problem: INFOPYMES — Full Cycle Financial Statements, 2016–2017 & ROA/ROE

## 📄 Official Problem Statement (Transcripción Íntegra)

Three entrepreneurs started a business project that consisted of establishing the company called **INFOPYMES** on **1 January 2016 (01/01/2016)**. This company's goal is to provide on-line information services that were relevant to small and medium enterprises, "start-up" and potential entrepreneurs.

* The investment required for the creation of the company includes the purchase and acquisition of various computer equipments. This equipment, amounting to a value of **€ 100.000** was acquired on **1 January 2016 (01/01/2016)**. This amount will be paid to suppliers after three months i.e. on **1 April 2016 (01/04/2016)**. This investment has a useful life of **5 years** (until 31 December 2020) and will be depreciated linearly, with no residual value.
* The company offers information through online consultations. These online consultations fetched the company revenues of **€ 300.000** in 2016. INFOPYMES operates from a rented premises that has a monthly cost (from 1 January 2016) of **1.000 €/month**.
* Salaries and social security costs (contribution of the company to the Social Security) of the permanent employees amounted to **5.000 € per month** (from 1 January 2016). Further, electricity, phone, etc. led to an additional monthly cost (from 1 January 2016) of **500 € / month**.
* To respond to increased online inquiries, INFOPYMES hired additional staff. The total amount of variable wages to staff who managed these online consultations amounted in 2016 to **174.000 €**.
* To finance the venture, the partners contributed an initial capital of **100.000 €**. A bank agreed to grant the company a loan of **100.000 €**. The yearly interest on this loan was **10%** while the duration of this loan was for **5 years**, i.e. from 1 January 2016 until 31 December 2020. Each 31 December, the company must return to the bank an amount of **20.000 €** and should also pay the due interest (10%) calculated from the loan value at the beginning of each year.
* The financial results of the company are declared at 23:59 hrs on 31 December (after having made all payments and receipts indicated, including interest, wages, Social Security, etc.) and immediately making a payment of the corporation tax at **30%** on the profits obtained (by way of corporation tax). Dividends are distributed to the shareholders at **40%** of the profits after taxes and the final balance is carried out at 24 h.

**Required:**
1. Construct the Income Statement (Profit and Loss Account) for the period ending **31 December 2016 (31/12/2016)**.
2. Assuming that the company has paid the income tax and distributed the dividends, construct and justify the Balance Sheet at **24:00 on 31 December 2016 (31/12/2016)**.
3. Construct and justify the Balance Sheet at **24:00 on 31 December 2017 (31/12/2017)**. Assume that no new investments were made in 2017. In addition, the retained earnings and the cash balance at the end of 2017 remained unchanged with respect to values of 2016.
4. In the case of the previous section determine the values of **ROA** and **ROE** ratios in 2017.

## 🎯 Exam Strategy & Key Information Recognition

> [!TIP] How to Approach this Problem on Exam Day
> 1. **Timeline Awareness (At 24:00 on 31 Dec):**
>    - Notice that on 31 December before 24:00, the company:
>      * Repays $20,000 \text{ €}$ of principal.
>      * Pays the year's interest ($10,000 \text{ €}$).
>      * Pays corporate tax ($5,400 \text{ €}$).
>      * Pays dividends ($5,040 \text{ €}$).
>    - Therefore, on the 31/12/2016 balance sheet, **there are NO tax liabilities, NO dividend debts, and the loan principal is reduced from €100,000 to €80,000!**
> 2. **Multi-Year Evolution (2016 to 2017):**
>    - Computer equipment cost is still €100,000, but accumulated depreciation grows from 1 year (€20,000) to 2 years (€40,000). Net Book Value drops to €60,000.
>    - On 31/12/2017, the bank loan undergoes another €20,000 repayment, so remaining principal drops from €80,000 to €60,000.
>    - Retained earnings and cash are given as constant $\implies$ Balance sheet automatically balances!

## 🔢 Step-by-Step Resolution

### Section 1: Income Statement for Fiscal Year 2016

#### Step 1: Revenues
$$\mathbf{\text{Sales Revenue}} = \mathbf{300,000 \text{ €}}$$

#### Step 2: Operating Expenses
* **Rent:** $12 \text{ months} \times 1,000 \text{ €/mo} = 12,000 \text{ €}$.
* **Permanent Staff (Wages + SS):** $12 \text{ months} \times 5,000 \text{ €/mo} = 60,000 \text{ €}$.
* **General Utilities (Electricity, Phone):** $12 \text{ months} \times 500 \text{ €/mo} = 6,000 \text{ €}$.
* **Variable Staff Wages:** $174,000 \text{ €}$.
* **Linear Depreciation of Computer Equipment:**
  $$\text{Depreciation} = \frac{\text{Cost} - \text{Residual Value}}{\text{Useful Life}} = \frac{100,000 - 0}{5} = \mathbf{20,000 \text{ €}}$$
* **Total Operating Expenses:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Operating Expenses}} = 12,000 + 60,000 + 6,000 + 174,000 + 20,000 = \mathbf{272,000 \text{ €}}$$

#### Step 3: Operating Profit (EBIT)
$$\mathbf{\text{EBIT}} = 300,000 - 272,000 = \mathbf{28,000 \text{ €}}$$

#### Step 4: Financial Expenses & EBT
Interest is $10\%$ on the initial loan balance of €100,000:
$$\text{Interest Expense} = 10\% \times 100,000 = \mathbf{10,000 \text{ €}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{EBT}} = 28,000 - 10,000 = \mathbf{18,000 \text{ €}}$$

#### Step 5: Corporate Taxes & Net Income
$$\mathbf{\text{Corporate Tax } (30\%)} = 0.30 \times 18,000 = \mathbf{5,400 \text{ €}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Net Income}} = 18,000 - 5,400 = \mathbf{12,600 \text{ €}}$$

#### Step 6: Profit Appropriation
* **Dividends ($40\%$ of Net Profit):**
  $$\text{Dividends} = 0.40 \times 12,600 = \mathbf{5,040 \text{ €}}$$
* **Retained Earnings ($60\%$ to Reserves):**
  $$\text{Retained Earnings} = 0.60 \times 12,600 = \mathbf{7,560 \text{ €}}$$

### Section 2: Balance Sheet at 24:00 on 31 December 2016

#### Cash Treasury Reconciliation at 24:00 on 31/12/2016:
* **Cash Inflows:**
  * Partners' equity contribution: $+100,000 \text{ €}$
  * Bank loan received on 01/01/2016: $+100,000 \text{ €}$
  * Customer collections (100% collected): $+300,000 \text{ €}$
  * Total Inflows $= 100,000 + 100,000 + 300,000 = \mathbf{500,000 \text{ €}}$
* **Cash Outflows:**
  * Paid for computer equipment (on 01/04/2016): $-100,000 \text{ €}$
  * Rent paid: $-12,000 \text{ €}$
  * Permanent staff paid: $-60,000 \text{ €}$
  * Utilities paid: $-6,000 \text{ €}$
  * Variable wages paid: $-174,000 \text{ €}$
  * Bank loan principal returned (on 31/12): $-20,000 \text{ €}$
  * Bank loan interest paid (on 31/12): $-10,000 \text{ €}$
  * Corporate income tax paid (on 31/12): $-5,400 \text{ €}$
  * Dividends paid to shareholders (on 31/12): $-5,040 \text{ €}$
  * Total Outflows $= 100,000 + 12,000 + 60,000 + 6,000 + 174,000 + 20,000 + 10,000 + 5,400 + 5,040 = \mathbf{392,440 \text{ €}}$
* **Ending Cash Balance:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Ending Cash}} = 500,000 - 392,440 = \mathbf{107,560 \text{ €}}$$

#### Bank Debt Remaining:
$$\text{Remaining Principal} = 100,000 - 20,000 = \mathbf{80,000 \text{ €}}$$

### Section 3: Balance Sheet at 24:00 on 31 December 2017

#### Assumptions Imposed by the Statement:
1. No new capital investments in 2017.
2. Cash balance remains unchanged: $\text{Cash}_{2017} = \mathbf{107,560 \text{ €}}$.
3. Retained earnings remain unchanged: $\text{RE}_{2017} = \mathbf{7,560 \text{ €}}$.

#### Evolution of Balance Sheet Accounts:
1. **Accumulated Depreciation:**
   Grows by another year of depreciation ($20,000 \text{ €}$):
   $$\text{Accum Dep}_{2017} = 20,000 + 20,000 = \mathbf{40,000 \text{ €}}$$
   $$\mathbf{\text{Net Book Value of Equipment}} = 100,000 - 40,000 = \mathbf{60,000 \text{ €}}$$
2. **Total Assets in 2017:**
   $$\mathbf{\text{Total Assets}}_{2017} = 60,000 \text{ (Equipment)} + 107,560 \text{ (Cash)} = \mathbf{167,560 \text{ €}}$$
3. **Total Stockholders' Equity in 2017:**
   $$\mathbf{\text{Equity}}_{2017} = 100,000 \text{ (Capital)} + 7,560 \text{ (Retained Earnings)} = \mathbf{107,560 \text{ €}}$$
4. **Bank Debt on 31/12/2017:**
   On 31 December 2017, the second mandatory repayment of €20,000 is made:
   $$\mathbf{\text{Remaining Loan Balance}} = 80,000 - 20,000 = \mathbf{60,000 \text{ €}}$$
   *(Comprising €40,000 Long-Term and €20,000 Short-Term due in 2018).*

### Section 4: Determination of ROA and ROE in 2017

#### Standard Financial Ratio Definitions:
$$\mathbf{\text{ROA (Return on Assets)}} = \frac{\text{Operating Profit (EBIT)}}{\text{Total Assets}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{ROE (Return on Equity)}} = \frac{\text{Net Income (Net Profit)}}{\text{Stockholders' Equity}}$$

#### Operating Parameters for Fiscal Year 2017:
Assuming standard business continuity with identical online consulting operational activity:
* $\text{EBIT}_{2017} = 28,000 \text{ €}$ (Revenues €300,000 - Operating Expenses €272,000).
* $\text{Total Assets}_{2017} = 167,560 \text{ €}$.
$$\mathbf{\text{ROA}_{2017}} = \frac{28,000 \text{ €}}{167,560 \text{ €}} \approx \mathbf{16.71\%}$$

* For **ROE**, debt servicing in 2017 was calculated on the beginning-of-year loan balance of €80,000:
  $$\text{Interest}_{2017} = 10\% \times 80,000 = 8,000 \text{ €}$$
  $$\text{EBT}_{2017} = 28,000 - 8,000 = 20,000 \text{ €}$$
  $$\text{Taxes}_{2017} = 30\% \times 20,000 = 6,000 \text{ €}$$
  $$\text{Net Income}_{2017} = 20,000 - 6,000 = \mathbf{14,000 \text{ €}}$$
* Since Retained Earnings remained unchanged ($\Delta \text{RE} = 0$), the entire net profit was distributed as dividends:
  $$\mathbf{\text{ROE}_{2017}} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Equity}} = \frac{14,000 \text{ €}}{107,560 \text{ €}} \approx \mathbf{13.02\%}$$

*(Alternative interpretation note: If the problem assumes a static scenario where no net profit was generated, $\text{Net Income} = 0 \implies \text{ROE} = 0\%$, but under operational continuity, $\text{ROA} = 16.71\%$ and $\text{ROE} = 13.02\%$).*

## 🧾 Four-Phase Audit Trail (journal entries and ledger)

> [!info] Verification
> Entries were balanced in Python (debits = credits) and the 2016 and 2017 figures recomputed: EBIT 28,000; EBT 18,000; tax 5,400; net income 12,600; dividends 5,040; retained earnings 7,560; cash 107,560; total assets 187,560 (2016) and 167,560 (2017); ROA 16.71%; ROE 13.02%. No official numerical key is available in `sources/` (only the statement PDF).

### Phase 1: Hypotheses
* 2016 is the first year (1 Jan to 31 Dec); revenue is fully collected in cash and all expenses are paid in the year (the statement says every payment is made before the 23:59 cut-off).
* Interest is 10% of the balance at the start of the year (100,000 in 2016, 80,000 in 2017); principal of 20,000 is repaid each 31 December; tax (30% of EBT) and the 40% dividend are paid at 23:59 on 31 December, so no tax or dividend liability remains at 24:00.
* 2017: the statement imposes unchanged cash and retained earnings and no new investment; operating results are assumed equal to 2016 (revenue 300,000, operating costs 252,000 cash plus 20,000 depreciation).

### Phase 2: Definitions and accounting equation
$$A = L + E, \qquad \text{ROA} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Total assets}}, \qquad \text{ROE} = \frac{\text{Net income}}{\text{Equity}}$$
* Equity = capital + retained earnings, where retained earnings = net income - dividends. Net book value = cost - accumulated depreciation.

Balance sheet 2016: assets (80,000 net equipment + 107,560 cash = 187,560) = loan 80,000 + capital 100,000 + retained earnings 7,560.

**2017 bridge (why cash and retained earnings can stay unchanged):** interest = 0.10 x 80,000 = 8,000; EBT = 28,000 - 8,000 = 20,000; tax = 6,000; net income = 14,000. Cash from operations after interest and tax = EBIT + depreciation - interest - tax = 28,000 + 20,000 - 8,000 - 6,000 = 34,000; minus principal 20,000 = 14,000; this is exactly the dividend if all of the 14,000 net income is distributed, so cash change = 14,000 - 14,000 = 0 and retained earnings change = 14,000 - 14,000 = 0, consistent with the statement's assumptions.

**Ratios 2017:** $\text{ROA} = 28{,}000/167{,}560 = 16.71\%$; $\text{ROE} = 14{,}000/107{,}560 = 13.02\%$ (equity 100,000 + 7,560).

### Phase 4: Units and sanity checks
* Currency: euro (€); ratios are dimensionless (percent).
* Accounting equation 2016: $187{,}560 = 80{,}000 + 107{,}560$; 2017: $167{,}560 = 60{,}000 + 107{,}560$.
* Cash identity 2016: inflows 500,000 - outflows 392,440 = 107,560 (equals the ledger cash).
* Limit check: ROA uses EBIT over total assets, so it is independent of leverage; ROE (13.02%) is below ROA (16.71%) because interest and tax are deducted from the numerator while the equity base is smaller than total assets.
