# 🌐 Practice 2: Sector Environment Analysis and Porter's Five Forces

> **Practical Guide & Assignment Protocol:** Comprehensive methodological framework for conducting a dual macro-environmental (PESTEL) and industry-level (Porter's Five Forces) strategic evaluation to assess sector attractiveness and reach a definitive investment recommendation.

---

## 🎯 1. Assignment Parameters & Deliverables
* **Team Structure:** Teams of 5 to 6 students.
* **Deliverable:** Executive Presentation (**PPTX format**, open slide count).
* **Core Objective:** Evaluate the structural profitability of an assigned industry sector and conclude with an evidence-based capital investment verdict (*"Would you invest in this operation? Why?"*).

---

## 📊 2. The Analytical Protocol

### Phase 1: Macro-Environmental Screening (PESTEL)
Examine external systemic drivers influencing the industry:
1. **Political:** Subsidies, defense spending, geopolitical trade restrictions, government procurement rules.
2. **Economic:** Interest rate environment, fuel cost volatility, consumer purchasing power, exchange rate fluctuations.
3. **Socio-Cultural:** Travel demand preferences, public awareness of carbon footprints, demographic shifts.
4. **Technological:** R&D breakthrough cycles, propulsion electrification, automation, digital avionics.
5. **Environmental:** Strict noise limits, emissions trading schemes, airport sustainability requirements.
6. **Legal:** Certification authorities (EASA, FAA), safety mandates, antitrust regulations.

---

### Phase 2: Mapping Industry Structure & Key Players (Task A)
Construct an exhaustive inventory of the sector ecosystem:
* **Direct Industry Competitors:** Established incumbents competing for the same market share.
* **Clients / Buyers:** Direct customers, intermediate distributors, or leasing institutions.
* **Providers / Suppliers:** Tier-1 and raw material suppliers possessing critical technological components.
* **Substitutes:** Alternative products or services addressing the same core customer need.

---

### Phase 3: Detailed Five Forces Audit (Task B)

Analyze the specific economic drivers governing each of the five competitive forces:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Competitive Force                    │ Evaluated Key Drivers & Indicators                     │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Threat of New Entrants            │ • Scale economies & upfront capital intensity          │
│                                      │ • Proprietary technology & learning curve barriers     │
│                                      │ • Certification and regulatory hurdles                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Bargaining Power of Suppliers     │ • Supplier concentration vs industry fragmentation     │
│                                      │ • Uniqueness of inputs & customer switching costs      │
│                                      │ • Credible threat of forward integration               │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Bargaining Power of Buyers        │ • Buyer purchasing volume & buyer concentration        │
│                                      │ • Degree of product standardization & switching costs │
│                                      │ • Buyer profit margins & price elasticity              │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Threat of Substitutes             │ • Relative price-to-performance ratio of substitutes   │
│                                      │ • User switching costs and propensity to substitute    │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 5. Intensity of Rivalry              │ • Number and balance of competitors                    │
│                                      │ • Rate of industry growth & excess capacity            │
│                                      │ • Height of exit barriers (specialized assets)         │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Phase 4: Synthesis of Rivalry and Structural Profitability (Task C)
* Synthesize the collective pressure exerted by all five forces.
* **High Collective Force Intensity:** Results in a low-profit, intensely contested "commodity" industry where value is captured by buyers or suppliers.
* **Low Collective Force Intensity:** Results in an attractive, highly profitable industry where incumbents enjoy durable economic rents.

---

### Phase 5: The Investment Verdict (Task D)
Answer the ultimate managerial decision question:
> **"Will you invest in this operation? Why or why not?"**

Structure the response around three criteria:
1. **Economic Feasibility:** Does the expected Return on Invested Capital exceed the Weighted Average Cost of Capital ($\text{ROIC} > K_{WACC}$)?
2. **Defensibility:** Can the business sustain a cost leadership or differentiation moat against competitive forces?
3. **Downside Risk & Exit Strategy:** What is the magnitude of fixed asset write-downs in the event of failure?
