# ✏️ Problem 3: Libby's Company Balance Sheet Classification and Balancing

## 📄 Enunciado Oficial (Official Statement)

Prepare the **BALANCE SHEET** of **Libby's Company** using the following figures (in euros):

$$\begin{array}{|lr|lr|}
\hline
\textbf{Account Item} & \textbf{Amount (\euro)} & \textbf{Account Item} & \textbf{Amount (\euro)} \\
\hline
\text{Short-term (current) loan debts} & 15,500 & \text{Accounts payable} & 1,500 \\
\text{Long-term loan debts} & 3,000 & \text{Equipment} & 13,000 \\
\text{Money on its bank account} & 3,000 & \text{Raw materials (not used yet)} & 12,500 \\
\text{Cash} & 500 & \text{Furniture} & 1,500 \\
\text{Paid-in capital} & 12,000 & \text{Retained earnings from the year} & 2,000 \\
\text{Accounts receivable} & 3,500 & & \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

---

## 📊 1. Accounting Account Classification

To construct the official balance sheet, each account must be assigned to its precise standardized accounting category:

1. **Non-Current Assets (Activo No Corriente / Inmovilizado):**
   * *Equipment (Equipos/Maquinaria):* Durable productive capital asset ($> 1 \text{ year}$) $= 13,000 \text{ \euro}$.
   * *Furniture (Mobiliario):* Durable office furniture ($> 1 \text{ year}$) $= 1,500 \text{ \euro}$.
2. **Current Assets (Activo Corriente):**
   * *Raw materials (Existencias / Materias primas):* Goods purchased and held in inventory $= 12,500 \text{ \euro}$.
   * *Accounts receivable (Clientes / Deudores comerciales):* Credit extended to customers $= 3,500 \text{ \euro}$.
   * *Money on bank account (Bancos):* Liquid deposit $= 3,000 \text{ \euro}$.
   * *Cash (Caja):* Immediate liquid currency $= 500 \text{ \euro}$.
3. **Stockholders' Equity (Patrimonio Neto):**
   * *Paid-in capital (Capital desembolsado):* Funds contributed by owners $= 12,000 \text{ \euro}$.
   * *Retained earnings from the year (Reservas / Resultados del ejercicio):* Undistributed profit $= 2,000 \text{ \euro}$.
4. **Long-Term Liabilities (Pasivo No Corriente):**
   * *Long-term loan debts (Deudas con entidades de crédito a l/p):* Bank borrowing with maturity $> 1 \text{ year} = 3,000 \text{ \euro}$.
5. **Current Liabilities (Pasivo Corriente):**
   * *Short-term loan debts (Deudas con entidades de crédito a c/p):* Bank borrowing due within one year $= 15,500 \text{ \euro}$.
   * *Accounts payable (Proveedores):* Commercial debt owed to input suppliers $= 1,500 \text{ \euro}$.

---

## 🔢 2. Balance Sheet Construction

$$\begin{array}{|lr|lr|}
\hline
\textbf{ASSETS (Activo)} & \textbf{Amount (\euro)} & \textbf{LIABILITIES \& EQUITY (Pasivo y PN)} & \textbf{Amount (\euro)} \\
\hline
\textbf{NON-CURRENT ASSETS} & \mathbf{14,500} & \textbf{STOCKHOLDERS' EQUITY} & \mathbf{14,000} \\
\text{• Equipment} & 13,000 & \text{• Paid-in capital} & 12,000 \\
\text{• Furniture} & 1,500 & \text{• Retained earnings from the year} & 2,000 \\
& & & \\
\textbf{CURRENT ASSETS} & \mathbf{19,500} & \textbf{NON-CURRENT LIABILITIES} & \mathbf{3,000} \\
\text{• Inventories (Raw materials)} & 12,500 & \text{• Long-term loan debts} & 3,000 \\
\text{• Accounts receivable} & 3,500 & & \\
\text{• Cash at bank} & 3,000 & \textbf{CURRENT LIABILITIES} & \mathbf{17,000} \\
\text{• Cash on hand} & 500 & \text{• Short-term loan debts} & 15,500 \\
& & \text{• Accounts payable (Suppliers)} & 1,500 \\
\hline
\mathbf{\text{TOTAL ASSETS}} & \mathbf{34,000} & \mathbf{\text{TOTAL LIABILITIES \& EQUITY}} & \mathbf{34,000} \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

---

## 🎯 3. Financial Equilibrium & Leverage Analysis

1. **Balance Check:**
   $$\text{Total Assets } (34,000 \text{ \euro}) \equiv \text{Total Liabilities \& Equity } (34,000 \text{ \euro})$$
2. **Working Capital (Fondo de Maniobra):**
   $$\mathbf{\text{Working Capital}} = \text{Current Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities} = 19,500 - 17,000 = \mathbf{+2,500 \text{ \euro}}$$
   The positive working capital indicates that Libby's Company maintains a short-term solvency buffer, financing a portion of its current assets with permanent capital.
3. **Financial Structure & Leverage:**
   * $\text{Total Equity } (E) = 14,000 \text{ \euro} \implies w_e = \frac{14,000}{34,000} \approx 41.18\%$
   * $\text{Total Debt / Liabilities } (L) = 3,000 + 17,000 = 20,000 \text{ \euro} \implies w_d = \frac{20,000}{34,000} \approx 58.82\%$
   * $\mathbf{\text{Debt-to-Equity Ratio }} (L/E) = \frac{20,000}{14,000} \approx \mathbf{1.43}$
   * Notice that current debt represents $85\%$ of total debt ($15,500 / 20,000$), warning management of imminent refinancing requirements.
