# ✏️ Problem: Exercise 7 — Sumun S.A.: break-even point and leverage

## 📄 Statement

We have the following data corresponding to a certain product manufactured by Sumun S.A.:

- Unit margin: €10
- Annual sales volume: 2,000 units
- Annual fixed costs: €1,000

Calculate: a) the break-even point of this product; b) its degree of operating leverage.

## Data and model

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| $m$ | unit margin | 10 €/unit |
| $Q$ | annual sales | 2,000 units |
| $FC$ | annual fixed costs | 1,000 € |

Break-even point $Q^* = FC/m$ [Slide 37], operating leverage $OL = Q\,m/(Q\,m - FC)$ [Slide 40].

## Solution

### Step 1. Part (a): break-even point

$$Q^* = \frac{FC}{m} = \frac{1{,}000}{10} = 100 \ \text{units}$$

### Step 2. Part (b): operating leverage

$$OL_{2000} = \frac{2{,}000 \times 10}{2{,}000 \times 10 - 1{,}000} = \frac{20{,}000}{19{,}000} = 1.05$$

A 1% change in sales changes the profit by about 1.05%: with sales twenty times the break-even point, the profit moves almost in proportion to the sales.

## Checks and official key

- **Check with the BEP form** [Slide 42]: $2{,}000/(2{,}000 - 100) = 2{,}000/1{,}900 = 1.05$. ✓
- **Check against the official key** (BEP  OL Statements  Solutions.pdf, p. 7): $q^* = 100$ units and $AO = 20{,}000/19{,}000 = 1.05$. Same results.
