# ✏️ Problem 4: Hawkins Partners Income Statement and Inventory Adjustments

## 📄 Enunciado Oficial (Official Statement)

Prepare the **Income Statement** for **Hawkins Partners** using the following figures and knowing that corporate tax applicable rate is $30\%$ (figures in euros):

$$\begin{array}{|lr|lr|}
\hline
\textbf{Accounting Concept} & \textbf{Amount (\euro)} & \textbf{Accounting Concept} & \textbf{Amount (\euro)} \\
\hline
\text{1. Initial inventory} & 600 & \text{6. Interests from long-term debt} & 5,500 \\
\text{2. Purchase of goods (sold 100\%)} & 84,000 & \text{7. Wages and salaries} & 12,700 \\
\text{3. Rental costs} & 1,000 & \text{8. Water, gas and electricity} & 1,200 \\
\text{4. Property sold to another company (income)} & 10,500 & \text{9. Sales} & 130,800 \\
\text{5. Interests received by the company} & 15,200 & \text{10. Inventory at the end of period} & 500 \\
\hline
\end{array}$$

---

## 📊 1. Methodological Formulation & Justification

The Income Statement structures revenues and expenses according to operational vs non-operational activity:

1. **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Identity:**
   Before goods are sold, beginning inventory and purchases represent available stock. The cost of goods actually delivered to customers is:
   $$\mathbf{\text{COGS} = \text{Initial Inventory} + \text{Purchases} - \text{Ending Inventory}}$$
2. **Gross Profit:**
   $$\mathbf{\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Sales} - \text{COGS}}$$
3. **Operating Income (EBIT):**
   Deducts ongoing general operating expenses (wages, rent, utilities) from Gross Profit.
4. **Non-Operating Items:**
   Separates extraordinary events (gain from selling corporate property) and financial flows (interests earned minus interests paid) from core operational activities.
5. **Corporate Taxes:**
   $$\mathbf{\text{Taxes} = t \times \text{EBT} = 0.30 \times \text{EBT}}$$

---

## 🔢 2. Step-by-Step Mathematical Computation

### Step 1: Compute Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
$$\text{COGS} = 600 + 84,000 - 500 = \mathbf{84,100 \text{ \euro}}$$
*(There was a net inventory decrease of $600 - 500 = 100 \text{ \euro}$, which adds to purchases).*

### Step 2: Compute Gross Profit
$$\text{Gross Profit} = 130,800 - 84,100 = \mathbf{46,700 \text{ \euro}}$$

### Step 3: Compute Total Operating Expenses
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Wages and salaries} &= 12,700 \text{ \euro} \\
\text{Rental costs} &= 1,000 \text{ \euro} \\
\text{Water, gas and electricity} &= 1,200 \text{ \euro} \\
\hline
\mathbf{\text{Total Operating Expenses}} &= 12,700 + 1,000 + 1,200 = \mathbf{14,900 \text{ \euro}}
\end{aligned}$$

### Step 4: Compute Operating Income (EBIT)
$$\mathbf{\text{Operating Income (EBIT)}} = 46,700 - 14,900 = \mathbf{31,800 \text{ \euro}}$$

### Step 5: Compute Non-Operating Items
Non-operating items comprise non-core revenues, financial interest earnings, and debt interest costs:
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Property sold (extraordinary income)} &= +10,500 \text{ \euro} \\
\text{Interests received (financial income)} &= +15,200 \text{ \euro} \\
\text{Interests from long-term debt (financial expense)} &= -5,500 \text{ \euro} \\
\hline
\mathbf{\text{Net Non-Operating Items}} &= +10,500 + 15,200 - 5,500 = \mathbf{+20,200 \text{ \euro}}
\end{aligned}$$

### Step 6: Compute Earnings Before Taxes (EBT)
$$\mathbf{\text{EBT}} = \text{Operating Income} + \text{Net Non-Operating Items} = 31,800 + 20,200 = \mathbf{52,000 \text{ \euro}}$$

### Step 7: Compute Corporate Taxes & Net Income
$$\mathbf{\text{Corporate Income Taxes}} = 0.30 \times 52,000 = \mathbf{15,600 \text{ \euro}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Net Income}} = \text{EBT} - \text{Taxes} = 52,000 - 15,600 = \mathbf{36,400 \text{ \euro}}$$

---

---

## 🎯 4. Analytical Observations
* **Operating Margin:** $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Sales}} = \frac{31,800}{130,800} \approx 24.31\%$.
* **Net Profit Margin:** $\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Sales}} = \frac{36,400}{130,800} \approx 27.83\%$.
* Notice that Hawkins Partners benefited significantly from non-operating windfalls (€10,500 property sale and €15,200 financial interest income), which represent $38.85\%$ of pre-tax income (€20,200 / €52,000).
