# Problem Set — Engineering Mechanics Midterm 1 Problem Bank

The bank behind the Extra problems pages (1st Midterm: Concepts and Problems) and the Exam simulator of the portal. Each line is one note in this folder; `python tools/build_mechanics_bank.py` turns them into `subjects/engineering-mechanics/data/problems.json` and the two pages. Every item is checked with SymPy (`python scripts/verify_mechanics_problems.py`) and its sketch is drawn by `tools/figures/em_m1/`.

> [!info] What is official and what is practice
> None of these items is an official exercise. They are written for the portal in the style of the Midterm 1 papers (2011 to 2024) and of the true/false questions of the finals. The structure analysis is in `docs/midterm1_blueprint.md`.
