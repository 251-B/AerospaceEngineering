# Master Map of Content

> *"Connect concepts, master the fundamentals."*

Root node of the 2nd-year Aerospace Engineering knowledge vault (first term). The units listed for each
subject are those of the official course material in `sources/cuatrimestre-1/`; the notes column says how far
the vault currently reaches.

---

## Active subjects (first term)

### 🌊 [[01 - Fluid Mechanics/Fluid Mechanics MOC|Fluid Mechanics]]
* **Units (7):** introductory remarks, flow kinematics, conservation laws, Navier-Stokes equations, hydrostatics, dimensional analysis, viscous flow.
* **Vault notes:** units 1-3, the hydrostatics concept notes (topic 5), and two hydrostatics problems whose provenance is still being checked.
* **Laboratory:** [[01 - Fluid Mechanics/Laboratorio 1 - Jet Impact on Surfaces|Lab 1: Jet Impact on Surfaces (RTT and momentum)]] (private note; labs are not published on the web).
* **NotebookLM notebook:** `3080c1f2-5689-4a39-90c3-091d58f39684`
* **Web pages:** `subjects/fluid-mechanics/`

### 🔬 [[02 - Aerospace Materials I/Aerospace Materials I MOC|Aerospace Materials I]]
* **Units (11):** bonding, crystal structures and defects, diffusion, phase diagrams, mechanical properties, electrical properties, magnetic and thermal properties, ceramics, polymers, composites, adhesives.
* **Vault notes:** units 1-4 (Topic 4 complete: Sessions 7-10, with all 26 problems of the two phase-diagram sheets).
* **NotebookLM notebook:** `9b324478-69e9-482c-813c-5709ec031820`
* **Web pages:** `subjects/aerospace-materials-1/`

### 🏗️ [[03 - Engineering Mechanics/Engineering Mechanics MOC|Mechanics Applied to Aerospace Engineering]]
* **Units on disk (8):** particle kinematics, particle dynamics, constraints and reactions, angular momentum and central forces, relative motion, systems of particles, rigid-body kinematics, rigid-body dynamics.
* **Vault notes:** topics 1-5 with solved problems and formula sheets.
* **NotebookLM notebook:** `473546c3-3716-4426-b0c4-58de530f91c8`
* **Web pages:** `subjects/engineering-mechanics/`

### 📐 [[04 - Advanced Maths/Advanced Maths MOC|Advanced Mathematics]]
* **Units (6):** introduction to ODE modelling, first-order ODEs, second-order linear ODEs, systems of ODEs, Fourier series and PDEs, heat/wave/Laplace equations.
* **Vault notes:** chapters 1-3. The web page for Topic 4 (`subjects/advanced-maths/teoria/topic-4.html`) has no vault note yet.
* **NotebookLM notebook:** `c27033c3-5a64-403f-a517-5847831aabcb`
* **Web pages:** `subjects/advanced-maths/`

### 📊 [[05 - Business Management/Business Management MOC|Business Management]]
* **Units (6):** the firm (types and objectives), value creation and environment, financial statements, financial analysis and ratios, operations and marketing, business plan.
* **Vault notes:** units 1-3 and the Topic 3 exercises.
* **NotebookLM notebook:** `e691ea81-0acf-4032-98d1-b9e5a7b93d70`
* **Web pages:** `subjects/business-management/`
