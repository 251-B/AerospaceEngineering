# Concept: Tensile Strength, Necking and Fracture Stress

> **Key idea:** the tensile strength is the highest nominal stress the specimen carries, and after it the specimen necks and breaks.

## Definition and formulas
- **Tensile strength** (ultimate tensile strength, UTS): the maximum nominal stress a specimen supports under tension [Session 11 Slide 30].
- **Necking** follows the tensile strength [Session 11 Slide 30].
- **Fracture stress:** the stress value at fracture [Session 11 Slide 31].
- The more ductile the material, the greater the necking [Session 11 Slide 31].
- A very low fracture stress indicates defects, such as porosity or inclusions [Session 11 Slide 31].
