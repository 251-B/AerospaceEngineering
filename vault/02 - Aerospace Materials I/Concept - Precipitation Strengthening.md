# Concept: Precipitation Strengthening

> **Key idea:** a secondary phase that precipitates inside the matrix works as an obstacle to dislocations, and finer, denser particles block them more strongly.

## Definition and formulas
- Particles of a secondary phase precipitate in the alloy and act as obstacles to dislocation motion. They also distort the crystal structure of the matrix [Session 12 Slide 12].
- $\downarrow\text{size}, \uparrow\text{dispersion}, \uparrow\text{density} \Rightarrow \uparrow\text{strengthening}$ [Session 12 Slide 12].
- The four stages of a dislocation meeting particles: approach ($\tau b$ per unit length), sub-critical (bowing), critical (force $\tau bL$) and escape (loops left around the particles) [Session 12 Slide 12].
- Precipitation starts when the solubility limit is exceeded [Session 12 Slide 9].
- Application: aluminium alloys for aircraft (series 2xxx and 7xxx). UTS: pure Al 45 MPa, solid solution 110 MPa, cold worked 165 MPa, Al-Cu-Mg precipitation 469 MPa, Al-Cu-Mg-Zn precipitation 572 MPa [Session 12 Slide 13].
