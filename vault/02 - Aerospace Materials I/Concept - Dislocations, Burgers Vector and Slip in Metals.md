# 📖 Concept: Dislocations, Burgers Vector and Slip in Metals

> **Key idea in one sentence:** dislocations are line defects whose movement under small forces produces the plastic deformation of metals.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## 1. Types [Session 4 Slides 26-32]
| | Edge (Taylor) | Screw (Burgers) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Picture | extra half-plane of atoms | atom lines twisted into a spiral ramp |
| b vs dislocation line | perpendicular | parallel |
| b vs direction of movement | parallel | perpendicular |

**Burgers vector b:** the vector that closes a loop of equal atom spacings drawn around the dislocation. Mixed dislocations keep the same b along their length. $E \propto |\vec b|^2$.

## 2. Slip [Session 4 Slides 33-37]
- Slip system = slip plane + slip direction; slip direction = direction of maximum packing.
- FCC: $b = 2R$ along $[110]$ ($E \propto 4R^2$) vs $b = 2\sqrt2\,R$ along $[100]$ ($E \propto 8R^2$).
- BCC $\{110\}\langle\bar111\rangle$: 12 systems; FCC $\{111\}\langle\bar110\rangle$: 12; HCP $(0001)\langle2\bar1\bar10\rangle$: 3. More slip systems → more ductility.
