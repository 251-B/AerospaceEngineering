# 📖 Concept: Miller Notation for Cubic and Hexagonal Directions and Planes

> **Key idea in one sentence:** directions [u v w] are the components of a vector reduced to the smallest integers; planes (h k l) are the reciprocals of the intercepts with the axes.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## 1. Directions [Session 3 Slides 39-42]
- **Draw:** origin (or $O'$ with a 1 where an index is negative), scale so the largest index is 1, arrow to the tip.
- **Read:** head − tail, reduce to the smallest integers, square brackets, bar over negatives.
- **Family** $\langle u\,v\,w\rangle$: $\langle100\rangle$ cube edges, $\langle110\rangle$ face diagonals, $\langle111\rangle$ body diagonals.

## 2. Planes [Session 3 Slides 44-50]
- **Indices:** $h = 1/x$, $k = 1/y$, $l = 1/z$ (intercepts); a plane parallel to an axis has index 0.
- **Draw:** origin ($O'$ with a 1 where an index is negative), intercepts $1/h$, $1/k$, $1/l$, join them.
- **Family** $\{h\,k\,l\}$: equivalent planes, e.g. $\{100\}$ = the six cube faces.
- In cubic cells $[h\,k\,l] \perp (h\,k\,l)$.

## 3. Hexagonal cell [Session 3 Slides 51-52]
- Four axes $a_1, a_2, a_3, c$; planes $(h\,k\,i\,l)$ with $i = -(h + k)$.
- Directions: $h = \tfrac13(2h' - k')$, $k = \tfrac13(2k' - h')$, $i = -\tfrac13(h' + k')$, $l = l'$. Examples: $[100] \equiv [2\bar1\bar10]$, $[110] \equiv [11\bar20]$.
