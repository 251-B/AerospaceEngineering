# 📖 Concept: The Arrhenius Equation and Factors Influencing Diffusivity

> **Key idea in one sentence:** the diffusivity grows exponentially with temperature and depends on the diffusing atom, the host structure, its defects and the concentration.

Full explanation: [[Topic 3 - Diffusion in Solids and Mass Transport]].

## Arrhenius [Session 5 Slides 5, 26]
$$D = D_0\,e^{-E_D/RT}, \qquad \ln D = \ln D_0 - \frac{E_D}{R}\cdot\frac{1}{T}$$
$\ln D$ vs $1/T$ is a straight line of slope $-E_D/R$.

![[attachments/T3/arrhenius.svg]]

## Factors [Session 5 Slides 23-25]
- **Mechanism / atom size:** C in γ-Fe $3\times10^{-11}$ vs Fe in γ-Fe $2\times10^{-16}$ m²/s at 1000 °C.
- **Host structure:** C in γ-Fe (FCC) $5\times10^{-15}$ < C in α-Fe (BCC) $10^{-12}$ m²/s at 500 °C (BCC less packed).
- **Defects:** $D_{surface} > D_{grain\ boundary} > D_{volume}$.
- **Concentration** of the diffusing species changes $D$.
- **Temperature:** $T\uparrow \Rightarrow D\uparrow$.

## Summary [Session 5 Slide 27]
Faster in open, low-density, defect-rich structures, for neutral and small species, in low-$T_m$ materials and at high temperature.
