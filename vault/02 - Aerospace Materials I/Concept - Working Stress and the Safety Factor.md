# Concept: Working Stress and the Safety Factor

> **Key idea:** because mechanical properties vary, a design stress is taken well below the yield strength, divided by a safety factor.

## Definition and formulas
- The mechanical properties vary, so design uses the **working stress** $\sigma_w$ [Session 11 Slide 29].
- $\sigma_w = \dfrac{\sigma_y}{N}$, where $\sigma_y$ is the yield strength [Session 11 Slide 29].
- The safety factor is $N = 1.2$ to $4$, usually $2$ [Session 11 Slide 29].
