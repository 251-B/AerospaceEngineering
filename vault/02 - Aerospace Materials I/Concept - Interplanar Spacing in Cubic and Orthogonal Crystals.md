# 📖 Concept: Interplanar Spacing in Cubic and Orthogonal Crystals

> **Key idea in one sentence:** the distance between neighbouring parallel planes (h k l) depends only on the Miller indices and the lattice parameters.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## Formulas [Session 3 Slide 53]
- Cubic: $$d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2 + k^2 + l^2}}$$
- Orthogonal: $$\frac{1}{d_{hkl}^2} = \frac{h^2}{a^2} + \frac{k^2}{b^2} + \frac{l^2}{c^2}$$
- Example: $d_{110} = a/\sqrt2$.
