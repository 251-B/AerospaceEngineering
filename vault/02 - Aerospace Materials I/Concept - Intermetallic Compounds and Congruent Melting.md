# 📖 Concept: Intermetallic Compounds and Congruent Melting

> **Key idea in one sentence:** an intermetallic compound is a new phase with its own composition, structure and properties; it melts congruently if the liquid keeps its composition and incongruently if it decomposes into a liquid and another solid.

Full explanation: [[Topic 4 - Phase Diagrams and Solidification]].

## Intermetallic compounds [Session 8 Slides 14-15]
- Terminal phases at the ends of the diagram; intermediate phases inside it.
- Ionic/covalent character depends on the electronegativity difference of the elements.
- Stoichiometric (fixed composition, a vertical line: Ti₃B₄) or non-stoichiometric (a composition range: TiB, TiB₂).

## Congruent and incongruent melting [Session 8 Slide 17]
- Congruent: the liquid keeps the composition of the solid; a maximum of the liquidus (MgNi₂, pure metals).
- Incongruent: the solid gives a liquid of another composition plus a different solid; it ends at a peritectic line (Mg₂Ni).
