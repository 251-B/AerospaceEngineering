# 📖 Concept: Binary Eutectic Systems and Microconstituents

> **Key idea in one sentence:** with partial solid solubility, the liquid of eutectic composition turns at one fixed temperature into two solids, α and β, in fine alternating layers; other alloys first form a primary phase and then the eutectic.

Full explanation: [[Topic 4 - Phase Diagrams and Solidification]].

## Eutectic reaction [Session 7 Slides 26-27]
$$L \rightarrow \alpha + \beta \quad \text{at the eutectic temperature and composition}$$
Invariant reaction: fixed temperature and fixed compositions ($F = 2 - 3 + 1 = 0$ at 1 atm); the cooling curve shows a plateau like a pure metal. Lines: liquidus, solidus, solvus (solubility limit in the solid).

## Pb-Sn [Session 7 Slides 28, 32]
Eutectic 61.9 wt% Sn at 183 °C; α 18.3 and β 97.8 wt% Sn at 183 °C (the problem sheet's diagram uses 19.2 and 97.5). The eutectic product is 45.2 % α and 54.8 % β.

## Hypo- and hypereutectic alloys [Session 7 Slides 30-31]
- Hypoeutectic ($C_\alpha < C_0 < C_e$): primary α forms first, then the remaining liquid (eutectic composition) gives the eutectic: primary α + eutectic.
- Hypereutectic ($C_e < C_0 < C_\beta$): primary β + eutectic.
- Fraction of eutectic below $T_e$ = fraction of liquid just above $T_e$.
