# 📖 Concept: Planar Defects, Grain Boundaries, Twins and Stacking Faults

> **Key idea in one sentence:** planar defects separate regions with the same crystal structure but different orientation: grain boundaries, twin boundaries, stacking faults and external surfaces.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## Kinds [Session 4 Slides 39-46]
- **Grain boundaries:** where grains that nucleated and grew at the same time meet; 2-5 atomic distances wide, high energy, they block dislocations: grain size ↓ → strength ↑.
- **Twin boundaries:** mirror-image misorientation; formed by deformation or heat treatment; hinder slip and raise strength (austenitic stainless steel, 70/30 brass).
- **Stacking faults:** one or two layers out of sequence, e.g. ABC**AB**ABC in FCC.
- **External surfaces:** lower coordination → higher energy than the interior.
