# Concept: Hooke's Law, Young's Modulus and Non-Linear Elasticity

> **Key idea:** in the elastic zone the stress is proportional to the strain, and the constant of proportionality, Young's modulus, measures the stiffness of the bonds.

## Definition and formulas
- **Elastic deformation** is reversible: on unloading the specimen recovers its original form [Session 11 Slide 12].
- **Hooke's law:** $\sigma = E\,\varepsilon$ [Session 11 Slide 13].
- $E$ is the **Young's modulus** (elastic modulus), the stiffness or resistance to elastic deformation [Session 11 Slide 12].
- **Mechanism:** bond relaxation. The bonds are stretched and the atoms are displaced but do not occupy new positions [Session 11 Slides 12-13].
- $E \propto \left(\partial F/\partial r\right)_{r_0}$: a stronger bond gives a higher $E$ [Session 11 Slide 13].
- Ranking: $E_{\text{ceramics}} > E_{\text{metals}} > E_{\text{polymers}}$ [Session 11 Slide 13].
- **Non-linear elasticity** (some polymers, concrete, grey cast iron): $\sigma = E(\varepsilon)\,\varepsilon$, with the initial $E$ (below 0.5 % strain), the secant $E$ (at 2 %) and the tangent $E$ at the strain of interest [Session 11 Slide 15].
