# Concept: Compression, Impact, Bending, Shear and Torsion Tests

> **Key idea:** the other mechanical tests load the material in other ways, and each one answers a question the tension test cannot answer on its own.

## Definition and formulas
- The tests are tension, compression, shear, bending, torsion and impact [Session 11 Slide 3].
- **Compression:** for fragile materials such as concrete, and widely applied to ceramics [Session 11 Slide 5].
- **Impact:** determines toughness as the energy absorbed until fracture. Izod and Charpy arrangements with a notched specimen and a pendulum hammer [Session 11 Slide 5].
- **Three-point bend:** modulus in bending, flexural stress and strain; the upper surface is compressed and the lower surface stretched. $\text{MOR} = \dfrac{3FL}{2bh^2}$ [Session 11 Slide 6].
- **Shear:** $\tau = F/A_0$, $\gamma = a/h = \tan\theta$, $\tau = G\gamma$ in the elastic regime [Session 11 Slide 7].
- **Torsion:** a variant of the shear test; $\tau = f(F_{\text{torsion}})$ and $\gamma = f(\phi)$ [Session 11 Slide 7].
