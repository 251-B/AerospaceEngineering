# Concept: Uniaxial Tension Test and Engineering Stress-Strain

> **Key idea:** the tension test turns the load and the elongation of a standard specimen into a stress-strain curve, and every mechanical property of Topic 5 is read from that curve.

## Definition and formulas
- **Specimen:** a uniform cross-section with a calibration (gauge) length, long enough for the stress to be transmitted uniformly. The norm for metals is ASTM E8 [Session 11 Slide 4].
- **Destructive test** for the mechanical properties under tensile load [Session 11 Slide 4].
- **Engineering stress:** $\sigma_n = F/A_0$, in MPa or psi [Session 11 Slide 9].
- **Engineering strain:** $\varepsilon_n = (l - l_0)/l_0$, non-dimensional [Session 11 Slide 9].
- $F$ is the load, $A_0$ the original cross-section, $l$ the length and $l_0$ the initial length.
