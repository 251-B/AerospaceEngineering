# 📖 Concept: Tetrahedral and Octahedral Interstitial Sites

> **Key idea in one sentence:** interstitial sites are the gaps between the regular positions of a lattice that other atoms can occupy: tetrahedral (4 neighbours) or octahedral (6 neighbours).

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## 1. Two kinds of site [Session 3 Slide 30]
- **Tetrahedral:** surrounded by 4 lattice atoms. **Octahedral:** surrounded by 6.
- In compact structures with $n$ atoms per cell: $2n$ tetrahedral and $n$ octahedral sites.

## 2. Number and size of sites [Session 3 Slides 31-35; Session 4 Slide 10]
| Structure | Tetrahedral | Octahedral |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| FCC ($n = 4$) | 8 (inside the cell), radius $0.225\,r$ | 1 centre + 12 edges × ¼ = 4, radius $0.41\,r$ |
| BCC ($n = 2$) | 6 faces × 4 × ½ = 12, radius $0.291\,r$ | 6 face centres × ½ + 12 edges × ¼ = 6, radius $0.15\,r$ |
| HCP ($n = 6$) | 12, radius $0.225\,r$ | 6, radius $0.41\,r$ |
