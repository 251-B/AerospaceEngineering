# 📖 Concept: Polymorphism and Allotropy in Metals and Ceramics

> **Key idea in one sentence:** the same element or compound can have different crystal structures depending on pressure and temperature.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## Definitions and examples [Session 4 Slides 49-50]
- **Polymorphism:** a solid with more than one crystal structure. **Allotropy:** an element with two or more forms in the same physical state.
- **Carbon:** diamond (3-D covalent: hard, transparent, insulator) vs graphite (layers with secondary bonds: soft, opaque, conductor).
- **Zirconia:** monoclinic → tetragonal → cubic; the transformation consumes energy and raises toughness.
- **Iron:** BCC at room temperature, FCC from 910 °C → heat treatment of steels.
