# 📖 Concept: Substitutional and Interstitial Solid Solutions, Hume-Rothery Rules

> **Key idea in one sentence:** a solid solution is a single-phase crystal with two or more elements mixed at the atomic scale, either replacing host atoms or sitting in its interstitial sites.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## 1. Types [Session 4 Slide 21]
- **Substitutional:** solute replaces host atoms. **Interstitial:** solute in interstitial sites (small atoms: H, C, N, B).

## 2. Ionic solids [Session 4 Slide 22]
$3\,\text{Mg}^{2+} \leftrightarrow 2\,\text{Al}^{3+} + 1\ \text{vacancy}$ keeps MgO neutral when Al₂O₃ dissolves in it.

## 3. Hume-Rothery rules [Session 4 Slide 23]
- Substitutional: same crystal structure; similar radii (< 30 % ceramics, < 15 % metals; > 15 % → < 1 % solubility); similar electronegativity; same valence.
- Interstitial: smaller solute; similar electronegativity.
- All met → not necessarily total solubility; some not met → partial solubility.

## 4. Order-disorder [Session 4 Slide 24]
Cu-Au: disordered above 390 °C, ordered below.
