# Concept: Grain Size Strengthening

> **Key idea:** grain boundaries separate grains with different orientations, so each boundary is a barrier to slip, and smaller grains mean more barriers.

## Definition and formulas
- The grain boundaries separate crystals with different orientations. Their width is 2 to 5 interatomic spacings [Session 12 Slide 10].
- The boundary makes the sliding of dislocations difficult, because of the angle between the grains [Session 12 Slide 10].
- $\downarrow d \Rightarrow \uparrow\text{grains} \Rightarrow \uparrow\text{barriers to dislocation motion} \Rightarrow \uparrow\text{strengthening}$ [Session 12 Slide 10].
- The slide plots the yield strength against $d^{-1/2}$ (mm$^{-1/2}$), with the grain size $d$ (mm) on a second axis: a straight line over the range shown [Session 12 Slide 10].
- The micrograph of the slide shows the grain boundaries blocking the dislocations [Session 12 Slide 11].
