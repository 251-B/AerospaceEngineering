# 📖 Concept: Carburizing and Industrial Applications of Diffusion

> **Key idea in one sentence:** diffusion is used to harden surfaces, sinter powders, join parts and, in polymer films, it must be kept low.

Full explanation: [[Topic 3 - Diffusion in Solids and Mass Transport]].

## Applications [Session 5 Slides 29-34]
- **Carburizing (case hardening):** interstitial diffusion of C into the surface of steel; C blocks dislocations → hard surface, compressive residual stresses; gears and shafts (tough core, hard surface); machined soft, carburized afterwards.
- **Nitriding** (Si₃N₄): hard surface whose hardness falls with depth.
- **Sintering:** thermally activated mass transport that bonds powder particles and reduces porosity.
- **Diffusion bonding:** cowlings and fan blades (Rolls-Royce Trent 500 wide-chord fan).
- **Polymer films:** barriers to water vapour (< 10 g/(m²·day) at 23 °C, 90 % RH) and oxygen in food packaging.
- Also: integrated circuits (impurities diffused into Si wafers), diffusion soldering.
