# 📖 Concept: FCC, BCC and HCP Metallic Structures and Packing Factor

> **Key idea in one sentence:** most metals crystallize in BCC, FCC or HCP because their atoms are equal in size and their bond is non-directional, so they pack as densely as possible.

Full explanation: [[Topic 2 - Structure of Materials and Crystalline Defects]].

## 1. Packing of layers [Session 3 Slides 16-22]
- Close-packed planes stack as **ABAB** (HCP) or **ABCABC** (FCC).

## 2. The three structures [Session 3 Slides 24-28]
$$APF = \frac{V_{atoms}}{V_{cell}}$$

| | BCC | FCC | HCP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Examples | Fe, Cr, Mo, V, W, K | Al, Cu, Pb, Ni, Pt, Ag | Ti, Mg, Cd, Co, Zn |
| Atoms per cell | $1 + 8\cdot\tfrac18 = 2$ | $8\cdot\tfrac18 + 6\cdot\tfrac12 = 4$ | $12\cdot\tfrac16 + 2\cdot\tfrac12 + 3 = 6$ |
| Atoms touch along | body diagonal: $4R = \sqrt3\,a$ | face diagonal: $4R = \sqrt2\,a$ | hexagon edge: $2R = a$ |
| Coordination number | 8 | 12 | 12 |
| APF | $\sqrt3\,\pi/8 = 0.68$ | $\pi/(3\sqrt2) = 0.74$ | $\pi/(3\sqrt2) = 0.74$ |

For HCP: base area $B = 3\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a^2$, height $c = \frac{2\sqrt2}{\sqrt3}a$, $V_{cell} = 24\sqrt2\,R^3$.
