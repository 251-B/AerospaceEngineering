# Concept: Poisson's Ratio and the Shear Modulus

> **Key idea:** a longitudinal stretch makes a material thinner sideways, and Poisson's ratio is the constant that relates the two strains.

## Definition and formulas
- **Poisson's ratio:** $\nu = -\varepsilon_{\text{transverse}}/\varepsilon_{\text{longitudinal}}$ [Session 11 Slide 19].
- For uniaxial stress on an isotropic material: $\nu = -\varepsilon_x/\varepsilon_z = -\varepsilon_y/\varepsilon_z$ [Session 11 Slide 19].
- Ideal material: $\nu = 0.5$ (no change in volume). The slide also states $\nu = -1$ for no change in the aspect ratio [Session 11 Slide 19].
- Real materials: $\nu = 0.2$ to $0.4$. Natural rubber 0.499-0.49, polymers 0.3-0.45, metals 0.25-0.4 (as printed) [Session 11 Slide 19].
- **Shear modulus:** in the elastic regime $\tau = G\,\gamma$ [Session 11 Slide 7].
- $E = 2G\,(1+\nu)$ [Session 11 Slide 7].
