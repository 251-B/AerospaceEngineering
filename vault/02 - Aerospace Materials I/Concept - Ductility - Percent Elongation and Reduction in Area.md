# Concept: Ductility - Percent Elongation and Reduction in Area

> **Key idea:** ductility is the plastic deformation a material can take before fracture, measured after fracture as an elongation or as a reduction of area.

## Definition and formulas
- **Ductility:** the measure of the degree of plastic deformation that a material can support before fracture [Session 11 Slide 32].
- **Percent elongation after failure:** $EL\,(\%) = \dfrac{l_f - l_0}{l_0}\times 100$, measured after fracture, when the elastic recovery has taken place [Session 11 Slide 32].
- Before fracture $\varepsilon_t = \varepsilon_e + \varepsilon_p$; after fracture $\varepsilon_t = \varepsilon_p$ [Session 11 Slide 32].
- **Percent reduction in area:** $RA\,(\%) = \dfrac{A_0 - A_f}{A_0}\times 100$, measured after fracture [Session 11 Slide 33].
- Normally $\varepsilon\,(\%) \neq AR\,(\%)$ [Session 11 Slide 33].
- Ductility shows how many forming operations, such as lamination, can be performed in sequence [Session 11 Slide 33].
