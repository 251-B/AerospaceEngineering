# 📖 Concept: Phases, Components and Microconstituents

> **Key idea in one sentence:** components are the chemical substances, phases are the homogeneous regions they form, and microconstituents are the structures you actually see under the microscope, made of one or several phases.

Full explanation: [[Topic 4 - Phase Diagrams and Solidification]].

## Definitions [Session 7 Slide 2]
- **Component:** chemical substance or compound with a fixed composition (Al, Al₂O₃).
- **Phase:** chemically and structurally homogeneous region of the microstructure (ferrite, austenite, CaCO₃).
- **Microconstituent:** each structure visible on a polished surface; one phase (ferrite) or several (pearlite = ferrite + cementite).
