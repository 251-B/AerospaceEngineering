# Concept: Hardness Tests (Mohs, Brinell, Rockwell, Vickers, Knoop)

> **Key idea:** hardness is the resistance to permanent deformation, and each test defines it through its own indenter and load, so the results of different tests cannot be converted directly.

## Definition and formulas
- **Hardness:** the surface resistance to permanent (plastic) deformation. "The way you measure it is the way you define it"; there is no universal conversion [Session 12 Slide 15].
- **Mohs:** abrasion resistance, 10 minerals on the scale, introduced in 1820 [Session 12 Slide 16].
- **Brinell:** $\text{HB} = \dfrac{2P}{\pi D\left(D - \sqrt{D^2 - D_i^2}\right)}$, with $P$ in kg, $D$ the ball diameter and $D_i$ the indentation diameter. Load factor $Q = P/D^2$ [Session 12 Slides 19-20].
- **Rockwell:** $HR = N - h/S$ with the scale constants: $S = 0.002$ and $N = 100$ (A, C, D), $S = 0.002$ and $N = 130$ (B, E, F, G, K), $S = 0.001$ and $N = 100$ (coatings). Preload 10 or 3 kg, load 65 to 150 kg [Session 12 Slides 22-23].
- **Vickers:** $\text{HV} \approx 2F\sin(136^\circ/2)/d^2 = 1.854\,F/d^2$, with $F$ in kgf and $d$ the mean diagonal in mm. Microhardness at a load not above 1 kgf [Session 12 Slides 26-27].
- **Knoop:** $\text{KHN} = 14.2\,P/l^2$ [Session 12 Slide 29].
- Precautions: flat and clean surface; edge distance greater than $4\cdot\varnothing$; thickness greater than $2\cdot\varnothing$ (Brinell, Rockwell) or 1.5 times the diagonal (Vickers) [Session 12 Slide 28].
