# 📖 Concept: Fick's First Law, Steady-State Diffusion

> **Key idea in one sentence:** in steady state the flux does not change with time and is proportional to the concentration gradient.

Full explanation: [[Topic 3 - Diffusion in Solids and Mass Transport]].

## Law [Session 5 Slides 15-17]
$$J = -D\,\frac{\Delta C}{\Delta x}, \qquad \frac{\Delta C}{\Delta x} = \frac{C_{ext} - C_{int}}{x_1 - x_2}, \qquad \frac{\partial J}{\partial t} = 0$$
- $J$: flux (kg/(m²·s) or at/(m²·s)); $D$: diffusivity (m²/s); the minus sign: atoms move down the gradient.
- $J$ is constant only if the compositions on both sides are kept constant.

![[attachments/T3/fick1.svg]]

## Example [Session 5 Slide 35]
Pd plate, 0.2 m², $1.733\times10^{-3}$ kg/h of H₂, $C$ = 1.5 and 0.3 kg/m³, $D = 10^{-8}$ m²/s: $J = 2.4\times10^{-6}$ kg/(m²·s) and $\Delta x = D\,\Delta C/J = 5$ mm.
